# dynamics/coupled/force_torque_models/

## Purpose
The effector families as submodules: gravity, aerodynamics, perturbations, thrusters, guidance-model glue and robot-arm reaction forces.

## Design & Implementation
Six submodules — `GravityEffectors`, `AerodynamicEffectors`, `PerturbationEffectors`, `ThrusterModels`, `GuidanceModels` and `RobotArmReactionEffectors` — each defining its models and their `calcForceTorque` methods. `RobotArmReactionEffector` feeds the arm's base wrench back into the bus.
