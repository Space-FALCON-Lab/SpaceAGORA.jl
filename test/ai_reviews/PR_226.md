## Findings

No actionable source defect was identified in the reviewed scope. Format 1 requires zero-wind declarations and no wind fields. Format 2 requires all three stored ENU wind fields, the stored-component convention and explicit boolean semantics. Existing payload hash, metadata, support and datum validation remains in force; constructed wind-layer availability must match the catalog.

The extension delegates evaluation and clipping to GRAMSuite. The engine still masks returned winds when its environment disables them. Snapshot sampling continues to use current coordinates. Combined-model dispatch remains lower strictly below the handover and upper at or above it, with each component's result or domain error preserved.
