# simulation/engine/rhs_calibration.jl

## Purpose
Auto-calibration of the RHS execution plan: times candidate plans on the real problem before the solve and pins the fastest, remembering results per machine and workload.

## Design & Implementation
`_calibrate_rhs_plan_if_needed!` drives `_run_rhs_sweep!` over `_rhs_plan_candidates` with warmup and timed evaluations; results are stored under a signature from `_rhs_calib_signature` and persisted by `_rhs_calib_save!`/`_rhs_calib_load!`; `_make_calib_*_plan` build candidate plans.
