# gnc/guidance/rpo/

## Purpose
Rendezvous and proximity-operations guidance: plans a safe path from chaser to goal around a station, retimes it, and replans as the vehicle drifts.

## Design & Implementation
`hypr/` is the particle-swarm path planner with RRT warm start and refinement; `hypr_planning/` handles replanning triggers; `comparison_methods/` provides RRT*, CHOMP and STOMP baselines for benchmarking; `rpo_plan_buffer.jl` and `rpo_guidance_hooks.jl` turn a path into the `RPOPlan` the controller tracks.
