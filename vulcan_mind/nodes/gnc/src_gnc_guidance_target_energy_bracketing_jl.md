# gnc/guidance/target_energy_bracketing.jl

## Purpose
The energy-depletion guidance model: at each guidance tick, determines the pass's target energy by bracketing what is reachable and hands the targeting state to control.

## Design & Implementation
`AerobrakingEnergyDepletionConfig`, `AerobrakingEnergyDepletionState` and the guidance model struct; `calcGuidanceEffect!` runs `_edg_run_target_energy_bracketing!`, which predicts the low-drag and max-energy outcomes, interpolates the bracket, validates the configured mode set, and sets or falls back the target.
