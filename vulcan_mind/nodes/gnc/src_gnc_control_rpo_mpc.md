# gnc/control/rpo_mpc/

## Purpose
Rendezvous control: a finite-horizon LQ-MPC on Clohessy-Wiltshire dynamics, a six-thruster allocator, and the effector that ties them into the simulation.

## Design & Implementation
`lqmpc.jl` builds and solves the OSQP quadratic programme; `thruster_allocator.jl` maps a commanded body wrench onto six thruster magnitudes and back; `rpo_control_types.jl` and the `RPOMPCControlModel` effector hold the latched actuation and implement the control hooks, including reaction-wheel torque commands.
