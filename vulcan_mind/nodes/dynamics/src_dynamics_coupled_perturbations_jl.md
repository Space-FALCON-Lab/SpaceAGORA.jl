# dynamics/coupled/perturbations.jl

## Purpose
Perturbing effectors beyond point-mass gravity and drag: spherical-harmonics gravity, third-body gravity, solar radiation pressure with albedo and infrared, magnetic torque rods, eddy-current damping, and an LVLH attitude controller.

## Design & Implementation
Grouped by prefix: `_harmonics_*` for the Pines recurrence and batch pool, `_nbody_*` for third-body positions from cache, memo or SPICE, `_srp_*` for Sun position and the cannonball kernels, `_spice_*` for name and lock handling, the `gravity_backbone_*` hooks for the split solver, and the effector structs and their `calcForceTorque` and `wrench` methods.
