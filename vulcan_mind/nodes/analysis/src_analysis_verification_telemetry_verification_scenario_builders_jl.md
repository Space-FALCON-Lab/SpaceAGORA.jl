# analysis/verification/telemetry_verification/scenario_builders.jl

## Purpose
Builds the runnable `SimulationConfiguration` for a scenario from its parsed manifest entry: planet, initial state, vehicle, force models, atmosphere truth, wind, manoeuvres and study tolerances.

## Design & Implementation
Planet and effector resolvers, the atmosphere-truth model selector with its tabulated and GRAM-fallback builders, initial-condition frame conversion, and the layered `_with_*` rebuilders that add orbit-counted termination, wind, campaign manoeuvres and study integrator settings. `_make_orbit_args` and `_make_time_aligned_args` are the two top-level products.
