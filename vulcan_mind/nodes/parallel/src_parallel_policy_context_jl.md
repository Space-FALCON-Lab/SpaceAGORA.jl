# parallel/policy/context.jl

## Purpose
Policy scopes and the persistent worker pools behind them: a scope isolates telemetry and adaptive state, and its pools keep threads warm between calls.

## Design & Implementation
`_active_policy_context` and the scope-id helpers resolve the live `PolicyContext`; `_create_persistent_foreach_pool` and the worker-loop functions spawn long-lived tasks fed by channels; the spin-barrier pool variant serves latency-critical batches; `_destroy_persistent_foreach_scope!` and the shutdown helpers tear everything down.
