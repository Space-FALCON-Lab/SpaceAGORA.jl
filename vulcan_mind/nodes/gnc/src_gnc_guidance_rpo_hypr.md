# gnc/guidance/rpo/hypr/

## Purpose
The HYPR path planner for RPO: particle-swarm search over Bezier or polyline control points, warm-started by RRT-Connect, with clearance-adaptive sampling, culling, stagnation learning and post-refinement.

## Design & Implementation
`pso_path_planning.jl` is the swarm loop; `pso_parameters.jl` the configuration; `pso_refinement.jl` the shortcut, handle-tightening and degree-lowering passes; `path_sampling.jl`, `path_costs.jl` and `path_retiming.jl` the geometry; `rrt_connect.jl` the warm start; `pso_adaptive_policy.jl` sizes the search from geometry complexity.
