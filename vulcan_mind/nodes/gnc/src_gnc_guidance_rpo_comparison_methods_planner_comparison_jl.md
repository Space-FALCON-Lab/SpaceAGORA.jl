# gnc/guidance/rpo/comparison_methods/planner_comparison.jl

## Purpose
The benchmarking harness: runs each planner on a scenario, collects metrics, and renders comparison plots and tables.

## Design & Implementation
Scenario and settings builders per planner, a progress bar, metric collectors for cost per iteration and final clearance, `rpo_comparison_cost_iteration_plot` and related plotters, and `rpo_comparison_artifact_slug` for output naming. A fixed `rpo_740_mpc_final_pso_config` reproduces a published configuration.
