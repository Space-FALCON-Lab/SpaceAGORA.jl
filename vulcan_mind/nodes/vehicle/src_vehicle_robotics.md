# vehicle/robotics/

## Purpose
Kinematics of the cloth-handling serial arm: model types, forward kinematics, damped least-squares inverse kinematics and surface targeting.

## Design & Implementation
The `Robotics` module with `ClothArmModel` and its link, joint, pose and state types; `cloth_fk` and `cloth_fk_state`; `cloth_ik` with a finite-difference Jacobian; `closest_surface_target` for aiming at a sampled surface; `default_cloth_arm_model` for the three-link reference arm.
