# vehicle/structure/

## Purpose
Mass and geometric properties of the assembled vehicle: mass, centre of mass, inertia tensor, reference areas, length and surface normals.

## Design & Implementation
`get_spacecraft_mass`, `get_COM`, `get_inertia_tensor` with `set_inertia_tensor!` and the `update_inertia_tensor` pair recompute properties over the link tree via `traverse_bodies`; `get_spacecraft_reference_area`, `get_SA_area`, `get_SC_area` and `get_spacecraft_length` give aerodynamic references; `get_normal_vector` and `get_tangent_vector` give inertial surface axes.
