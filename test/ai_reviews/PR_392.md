## Findings

One P1 from the automated review, fixed in 55997fa61; no other P1 or P2
findings. Points a reviewer should check:

- Fixed from review (P1): with the stop enforced only through the rate, an
  accepted step could pass a stop (by about 3e-8 rad in the test run), and
  the gimbal then froze beyond it with `cmg_momentum` on the overshot angle.
  The stop event now sets the angle exactly on the stop at the crossing; a
  condition that starts at zero does not fire, so a gimbal leaves a stop
  under an inward command.
- The split solver modes keep finite stops allowed (decided with the
  coordinator against refusing them as batteries are): in `split_imex` the
  event sees the full state; in `multirate` the gimbal moves only in the
  fast subsolves, which carry the run's callbacks, and each subsolve after
  the stop starts from the clamped state, where the condition is zero and
  does not fire again (a repeated firing would set the same value). A test
  in both modes, with the stop reached between control ticks, ends exactly on
  the stop, never saves an angle past it and conserves the inertial angular
  momentum; without the event both modes overshoot (0.3102 and 0.3100008 rad
  for a 0.31 rad stop).
- Low: the reported level saturates each mounted effector's command at the
  stop, while the right-hand side saturates the sum of the effectors' rates
  for a CMG; with one effector commanding a CMG (the usual case) they agree.
- Low: `cmg_steering` drops a stopped CMG whole; it does not keep its
  inward-moving share when the solution would move it back.

## P1 Assessment

The review's P1 (a gimbal frozen past its stop) is fixed by the stop event
and covered by the stop tests; none is unresolved. Without stops
`cmg_angle_saturate` returns the rate itself, the steering loop exits after
its first solve, no event is added, and the four fingerprints are bitwise
unchanged against the base.
