# gnc/guidance/rpo/ (small files)

## Purpose
The RPO guidance model type and the reference builder that turns a planned path into a trackable, time-stamped reference.

## Design & Implementation
`RPOGuidanceModel` carries the planner configuration, station and chaser geometry, replanning settings and the plan buffer per satellite; `rpo_reference_from_path` samples a planned path at the controller's step, retimes it under curvature limits, and packs it into the `RPOPlan` layout the LQ-MPC previews.
