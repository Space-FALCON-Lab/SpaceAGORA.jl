# gnc/robotics/robot_arm_hypr/swarm_and_retiming.jl

## Purpose
The arm-specific swarm mechanics and the retiming that assigns joint speeds: culling, schedule weights, stagnation, the base-wrench reaction cost, and cloth-state coupling.

## Design & Implementation
Control-point packing, swarm culling and elite protection, iteration weights and material-improvement tests, the base-wrench ratio computations (with a cloth-aware variant) that penalise plans disturbing the bus, link centre-of-mass histories, and retiming under joint rate limits.
