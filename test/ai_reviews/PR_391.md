# Review record: PR 391: mounted wheel and CMG rates in the split solver modes

## Scope

Review of the SpaceAGORA `split-mode-actuator-rates` branch against main
(9ea941a1c). A fix found while assessing CMGs in the split solver modes: in
`split_imex` (inside the atmosphere) and `multirate`, a mounted reaction
wheel's momentum rate was integrated twice. Two commits: the fix with its
tests, then the docs sentence on which runs refuse CMGs.

Authorship disclosure: this record was drafted by the author of the PR. The
plan assigns behaviour PRs of the actuator layer an independent reviewer (the
PI, or the second reviewer when available); this record does not replace that
review.

Independent review: the coordinator reviewed the diff under Giusy's delegation on
2026-10-17. Each `_assign_orientation_rhs!` call was checked against its
right-hand-side component: the implicit and slow components (flat route
`rhs_kind` `:implicit` and `:slow`; `spacecraft_dynamics_implicit_atmosphere!`
and `spacecraft_dynamics_slow!`) pass `actuator_rates=false`, and the explicit,
full and fast-control components keep the default, so each mounted wheel's and
CMG's rate is integrated once.

## Changed Files

- `src/orchestration/simulation/engine/dynamics_rhs.jl`: `_assign_orientation_rhs!` takes `actuator_rates` (default true): it gates the mounted wheels' commanded rates and the CMGs' torque and gimbal rates (the rotors' momentum stays in the gyroscopic term); the implicit and slow components pass false (`spacecraft_dynamics_implicit_atmosphere!`, `spacecraft_dynamics_slow!`, and the `:implicit` and `:slow` kinds of `_assemble_flat_satellite!`).
- `src/orchestration/simulation/engine/execution.jl`: CMGs refused only by `gravity_backbone_split` (no attitude state).
- `src/orchestration/simulation/engine/setup.jl`: CMGs on articulated spacecraft refused, as reaction wheels.

Outside `src/`: a sentence of the extensibility page, tests.

## Findings

No P1 or P2 findings. Points a reviewer should check:

- The bug: the component without control received the wheel assembly with a
  zero `rw_torque_body`, so legacy wheels (`control_wheel_torque`) gained
  nothing there, but the mounted wheels' loop added the shared buffer
  `mounted_rates` written by the control component: twice their rate in the
  sum (`split_imex`) or in the two subsolves (`multirate`). In `split_imex`
  the implicit component returns zero outside the atmosphere, so the bug
  showed only inside it.
- Low: in `multirate`, the CMGs' momentum enters the slow component's
  gyroscopic term and their torque and gimbal rates the fast component; the
  splitting error is the method's own (the new test conserves the inertial
  angular momentum to 1e-6 relative).
- Low, pre-existing: in `multirate`, control ticks and fault switches run
  again in each overlapping subsolve, for every controller.
- Low: the `cmg_angles` state keeps the tolerances it had (the orbit's).

## P1 Assessment

No P1 findings. The default solver modes call the attitude equation with
`actuator_rates=true`, the previous arithmetic; the four fingerprints are
bitwise unchanged against main.
