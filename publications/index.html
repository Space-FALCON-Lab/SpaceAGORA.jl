Publications

Use this page to find papers that are directly connected to SpaceAGORA.jl. The project citation metadata remains in the repository CITATION.cff file; the papers below document SpaceAGORA-based analyses, verification, and guidance studies.

Direct SpaceAGORA papers

  • Evan Yu, Nihal Simha, and Giusy Falcone, "A Comparative Analysis of Aerobraking Dynamics Across Celestial Bodies Using SpaceAGORA," AIAA SCITECH 2026 Forum, Orlando, FL, 2026. DOI: 10.2514/6.2026-1692.

    This paper uses SpaceAGORA.jl for comparative aerobraking perturbation analysis across Mars, Venus, Earth, and Titan, including atmosphere, gravity, third-body, and solar-radiation-pressure effects.

  • Evan Yu, James Cutler, and Giusy Falcone, "Verification of SpaceAGORA.jl 6-DoF Dynamics Using University of Michigan SmallSats Telemetry," 2026 IEEE Aerospace Conference, Big Sky, MT, 2026, pp. 1-14. DOI: 10.1109/AERO66936.2026.11520056.

    This paper validates SpaceAGORA.jl translational and attitude dynamics against University of Michigan SmallSat telemetry and Basilisk comparisons.

Closely connected implementation papers

  • Nihal Simha, Evan Yu, and Giusy Falcone, "Generalized Energy Depletion Guidance for Aerobraking at Mars, Venus, Earth, and Titan," AIAA SCITECH 2026 Forum, Orlando, FL, 2026. DOI: 10.2514/6.2026-1378.

    This paper implements and exercises the generalized Energy Depletion Guidance framework in SpaceAGORA.jl for drag passages at Mars, Venus, Earth, and Titan.
