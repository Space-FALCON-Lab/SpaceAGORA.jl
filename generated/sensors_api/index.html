Sensors API

This page documents the supported public interface available from SpaceAGORA.

Sensors

SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.AbstractTypes.AbstractSensor — Type
AbstractSensor

Stable extension interface for a measuring instrument mounted on a spacecraft link: a rate gyro, a star tracker, a magnetometer, and kinds users add. A kind implements sensor_measure and sensor_dimension; mount it with add_sensor!. Sensors observe the state; they do not act on the dynamics.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.RateGyro — Type
RateGyro(; period_s=0.1, angle_random_walk=0.0, rate_random_walk=0.0, initial_bias=zero, seed=1)

A three-axis rate gyro: it measures the body rate in its link's frame, plus a bias and white noise. angle_random_walk (rad/√s) sets the white noise, angle_random_walk / √period_s per axis and sample; the bias starts at initial_bias (rad/s) and walks with rate_random_walk (rad/s/√s). seed selects its random stream. Zero noise measures the rate exactly.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.StarTracker — Type
StarTracker(; period_s=1.0, cross_boresight_noise_rad=0.0, about_boresight_noise_rad=0.0, seed=1)

A star tracker whose boresight is its link's +z axis: it measures the attitude quaternion (inertial to body, scalar last), perturbed by a small rotation with cross_boresight_noise_rad about the two axes across the boresight and about_boresight_noise_rad about it (one standard deviation, rad). Zero noise measures the attitude exactly.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.Magnetometer — Type
Magnetometer(; period_s=0.1, noise_t=0.0, bias_t=zero, seed=1)

A three-axis magnetometer: it measures the local magnetic field in its link's frame (T), from the run's torque-rod field model (dipole or IGRF, Earth only), plus a constant bias_t and white noise noise_t per axis and sample.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.GNSSReceiver — Type
GNSSReceiver(; period_s=1.0, position_noise_m=0.0, velocity_noise_mps=0.0, frame=:inertial, max_altitude_m=Inf, seed=1)

A GNSS receiver: it measures the position and velocity (m, m/s; six components, position first) in the run's inertial frame, or with frame=:planet_fixed in the planet-fixed frame, with white noise position_noise_m and velocity_noise_mps per axis and sample. Above max_altitude_m over the planet it has no fix (NaN), as a receiver above the navigation constellation's coverage. The antenna is taken at the centre of mass.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.SunSensor — Type
SunSensor(; period_s=0.1, noise_rad=0.0, field_of_view_rad=π/2, min_lit_fraction=0.5, albedo=0.0, seed=1)

A sun sensor whose boresight is its link's +z axis: it measures the direction to the Sun as a unit vector in its link's frame, turned by a small rotation with noise_rad (one standard deviation) about each axis. It reports no direction (NaN) when the Sun is more than field_of_view_rad from the boresight or when less than min_lit_fraction of the Sun's disk is lit (eclipse). The Sun's direction comes from the run's ephemerides, the lit fraction from the eclipse model of solar radiation pressure.

albedo (0 to 1, about 0.3 for the Earth; default 0, none) adds the sunlight the planet reflects, as a coarse sun sensor sees it: while the planet's disk is within the field of view, the reading is the normalized sum of the Sun's direction and the nadir direction weighted by η = albedo (2/3) (R/r)² Φ(α), the irradiance of a Lambertian sphere of radius R at distance r relative to the Sun's, with the Lambert phase law Φ(α) = (sin α + (π - α) cos α)/π and α the angle at the planet between the Sun and the sensor (Φ is 1 over the subsolar point, 0 over the night side). The reflected light is taken from the planet's centre; reflectivity maps and the planet's infrared are not modelled.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.Accelerometer — Type
Accelerometer(; period_s=0.1, noise_mps2=0.0, bias_mps2=zero, position_m=zero, seed=1)

A three-axis accelerometer at position_m in its link's frame: it measures the specific force there, the acceleration less gravity (m/s²), in its link's frame, plus a constant bias_mps2 and white noise noise_mps2 per axis and sample. Away from the centre of mass (the body origin), at r, the rotation adds ω̇ × r + ω × (ω × r) to the centre of mass's specific force. Gravity is the force of the run's effectors for which is_gravitational is true, so in free fall it reads zero and under thrust, drag or radiation pressure it reads their acceleration.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Sensors.SensorTruth — Type
SensorTruth

What a sensor measures from, at a sample: the inertial position and velocity (m, m/s), the attitude quaternion (inertial to body, scalar last), the body rate (rad/s), the mass (kg), the time (s into the run), and, each NaN unless the sensor asks for it, the local magnetic field in the body frame (T), the direction to the Sun in the body frame (a unit vector) with the lit fraction of the Sun's disk (0 in umbra, 1 in full sunlight) and the planet's equatorial radius (m), the specific force (the non-gravitational acceleration, m/s², in the body frame) with the body's angular acceleration (rad/s², body frame), and the position, velocity (planet-fixed frame) and altitude (m) over the planet. Without attitude propagation the attitude is the identity and the rate and angular acceleration zero.

SensorTruth(pos_ii, vel_ii, q_ib, ω_body, mass_kg, t_s, magnetic_field_body) leaves everything after the field NaN; the ten-argument form (through specific_force_body) leaves the rest NaN.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Sensors.sensor_measure — Function
sensor_measure(sensor, truth::SensorTruth, ctx::ActuatorContext, rng) -> measurement

Required: one measurement (an SVector or a number) of truth by sensor, mounted where ctx says (ctx.link_to_body maps the sensor's frame, its link's, to the body frame). Draw any noise from rng, the sensor's own random stream. Called once per sample, so a sensor may advance its own state (a bias random walk) here.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Sensors.sensor_dimension — Function
sensor_dimension(sensor) -> Int

Required: the number of components of the sensor's measurement, which sets its result columns.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Sensors.sensor_requirements — Function
sensor_requirements(sensor) -> ActuatorRequirements

Optional: what the sensor needs sampled. Sensors may ask for the magnetic field (magnetic_field=true), the Sun (solar=true: its direction, the lit fraction and the planet's radius) and the planet frame (planet_frame=true: the planet-fixed position, velocity and altitude); the atmosphere is refused when it is mounted. Default: nothing.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Sensors.sensor_period_s — Function
sensor_period_s(sensor) -> Float64

Optional: the sampling period, s. Default: the sensor's period_s field.

Besides its parameters, a sensor kind holds its run state in four fields the engine fills: seed (an integer the user sets, which selects the random stream), rng (the stream), latest (the latest measurement: a vector of sensor_dimension components, or a number for a one-component sensor; NaN before the first sample) and latest_time_s (its time, s into the run). The period must be finite and positive.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Sensors.initialize_sensor! — Function
initialize_sensor!(sensor, truth::SensorTruth, ctx::ActuatorContext, rng) -> nothing

Optional: reset a sensor's own run state at the start of a run. It is called once per run, after the engine has reset the fields it owns (the random stream, the latest measurement and, for a gyro, the bias) and just before the first measurement. It receives that measurement's truth and ctx and the fresh rng. It is not called again for later checkpoint segments. A sensor that keeps more state than those fields, such as a filter, resets it here. Default: nothing.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Sensors.sensor_needs_specific_force — Function
sensor_needs_specific_force(sensor) -> Bool

Optional: whether the sensor measures the specific force, which the engine then computes at its samples: the vehicle's acceleration less the acceleration of the run's gravitational effectors (see is_gravitational). Default: false.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.SensorMounting.add_sensor! — Function
add_sensor!(model::SpacecraftModel, link::Link, sensor) -> sensor

Mount sensor on one of model's links; the link's frame is the sensor's frame (a sensor on a hinged panel turns with it). Sensors are numbered in mounting order, links in order, for sensor_measurement and the result columns. Returns the sensor.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.SensorMounting.mounted_sensors — Function
mounted_sensors(model) -> Vector{NamedTuple{(:link, :sensor)}}

Every sensor mounted on model, with its link, in mounting order.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.SensorMounting.sensor_measurement — Function
sensor_measurement(sensor) -> measurement
sensor_measurement(model::SpacecraftModel, k::Int) -> measurement

The latest measurement of sensor, or of the k-th sensor mounted on model (NaN before its first sample). A navigation effector reads it in update_navigation!, from the spacecraft of the run (p.args.dynamics_model.spacecraft[i]).
