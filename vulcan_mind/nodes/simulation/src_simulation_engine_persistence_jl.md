# simulation/engine/persistence.jl

## Purpose
Writes the results bundle: the results CSV, collision CSV, series columns from saved segments, and a manifest with SHA-256 digests.

## Design & Implementation
`_write_results_bundle!` assembles the bundle under `_results_bundle_prefix`; `_append_saved_segment!` and `_append_series_columns!` flatten solver output into columns; `_results_csv_path` and `_collision_results_csv_path` name files; `_sha256_hex` digests them.
