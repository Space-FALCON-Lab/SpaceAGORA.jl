# Review record: PR 404: double-gimbal control moment gyros

## Scope

Review of the SpaceAGORA `double-gimbal-cmg` branch against main
(c588eefd0). A follow-up of the actuator layer: a `ControlMomentGyro` can
carry an inner gimbal (`CMGInnerGimbal`), so the rotor's spin axis turns about
two axes; the dynamics, the gimbal stops, the steering law and the result
columns cover the inner gimbals. One commit.

Authorship disclosure: this record was drafted by the author of the PR. The
plan assigns behaviour PRs of the actuator layer an independent reviewer (the
PI, or the second reviewer when available); this record does not replace that
review.

Independent review: the coordinator reviewed the change under Giusy's delegation on
2026-10-18. The momentum and torque follow from nesting the inner gimbal inside the
outer one: with `h = h₀ R(g_o, α) R(g_i, β) s`, `ḣ = α̇ (g_o × h) + β̇ (R(g_o, α) g_i) × h`,
which is what `cmg_torque_axes` returns and the attitude equation uses; a CMG without
an inner gimbal keeps its former branch, and the rates keep the outer gimbals first
when a CMG is mounted after an inner gimbal. One low finding, fixed in 559220c26: the
new `_cmg_gimbal` helper sat between the `get_cmg_callbacks` docstring and its
function, so the docstring documented the helper; the helper now comes first.

## Findings

No P1 or P2 findings.

Points a reviewer should check:

- Low, by design: as for the single gimbal, the model takes the rotor's momentum only; the gimbal frames' inertia and the gimbal motors' reaction torques are not modeled.
- Low, by design: an inner gimbal mounts on the root link, where the CMGs live, and after its CMG; a CMG mounted after an inner gimbal moves that inner gimbal's level one place on (the inner gimbals follow every CMG in the mounting order, as documented).
- Low: where the inner gimbal turns the rotor's momentum onto the outer gimbal axis (a quarter turn when the spin, inner and outer axes are mutually perpendicular) the outer torque axis vanishes (gimbal lock); the steering law's singularity-robust inverse keeps the command bounded there but does not steer away from it.
- Low: the axis checks use a fixed tolerance (perpendicular to the spin axis within 1e-9 of the normalized dot product), so an axis given with rounded components is refused rather than projected.

## P1 Assessment

No P1 findings. A CMG without an inner gimbal goes through the branch it took
before (the same arithmetic, in the same order), and a run without inner
gimbals has the same state layout and columns; the base fingerprints below are
identical.
