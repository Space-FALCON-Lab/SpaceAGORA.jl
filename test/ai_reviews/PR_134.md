## Findings

The original wrapper bypassed the typed aerodynamic path, losing propagated-attitude torque and saving unscaled components. The fix restores the shared behavior and correctly forwards the implicit partition under `split_imex`.

Independent review exposed a stale thermal-angle input after moving the wrapper to typed aerodynamics. The follow-up fixes that pre-existing built-in defect for both bare and scaled models. Corrected heat can change integrated heat histories and adaptive stepping; force-formula equality is not a trajectory-identity guarantee. Propagated-attitude and rotated-link cases require particular attention. Fixed-attitude cases keep the old heat input when it already equals geometric incidence.

The shipped energy-depletion comparison changed maximum-link heat diagnostics while retaining panel commands and trajectory in the executed case. Those diagnostics include the uncontrolled bus; the controller's limits apply to controlled panels. A heat-dependent command change remains possible, not demonstrated by the executed example. Campaigns identified statically were not rerun, and no general numerical bound is asserted.

The 56 existing control-logic assertions operate on synthetic states and do not exercise thermal sampling. Wind-relative flow enters the incidence calculation when attitude is propagated; fixed modes use geometric policies. The documentation now states both limitations and the built-in/custom ownership boundary.
