## Residual Risk

This is configurable Apollo-inspired guidance with bounded direct body torque and approximate RCS propellant accounting. Per-jet levels are diagnostic visualization, not individual jet dynamics. Terrain contact deactivates the craft and holds inertial state; it does not model a body-fixed resting craft, bounce or contact forces. The paired plume is phenomenological and uncalibrated against regolith measurements. Merge the terrain and plume dependencies first, then retarget and verify this branch before merge.
