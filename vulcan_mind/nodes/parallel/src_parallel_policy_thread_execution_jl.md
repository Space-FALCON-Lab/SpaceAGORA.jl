# parallel/policy/thread_execution.jl

## Purpose
The threaded loop primitives every parallel call site uses, each consulting the policy and routing to a transient or persistent worker pool.

## Design & Implementation
`threaded_foreach`, `threaded_collect!` and `threaded_reduce` for simple loops; the `_persistent` variants and `threaded_foreach_worker_persistent` for pooled workers with a worker index; `threaded_foreach_worker_spin` for the spin-barrier pool; `thread_worker_count` and the pool lookup helpers behind them.

Work is partitioned statically per call unless the dynamic scheduler mode is on; a skewed workload leaves workers idle.
