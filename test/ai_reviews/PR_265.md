## Findings

No actionable blocking source defect was identified in the reviewed changes.

The serial environment is applied only when the plan has one consumer and its effective budget is at most one. An explicit budget remains bounded by the inherited ceiling. An undeclared budget still leaves the inner budget undeclared, and a larger effective budget retains adaptive settings. The dynamic environment scopes restore the caller's values after dispatch.

The new cost term defaults to zero, preserving existing constructor call sites. Its constructor rejects negative and nonfinite values. Makespan adjustment is confined to plans with pool workers, at most one pool round, and a fixed cost meeting the stated five-percent threshold. Route feasibility, candidate bounds, margin selection, and the underlying schedule are unchanged.

The transient timing map is protected by a lock and ignores nonfinite or nonpositive observations. With no recorded sample time, the fallback contributes no fixed cost. Otherwise it supplies only the seconds-to-sample conversion; it does not mutate correction terms or the corrections file.

## Residual Risk

The 25 ms fixed cost and five-percent cutoff are heuristics based on the cited TRX50 observations. These tests verify their implemented selection rules, not portable wall-time improvement. The PR description itself reports no reproduced local gain for the short grid workload. Hardware benchmark acceptance remains outstanding.

The sample-time fallback is process-local, persists when corrections are disabled, and is populated by process campaigns including forced process measurements. Consequently, disabling corrections or forcing a measurement does not imply identical later routing in the same process. The existing promise that forced plans skip the corrections fold and save remains intact.

Mixed-dispatch timing uses the guard's observed worker samples, which stop accumulating once the guard decides. This review does not establish that this estimate, or the last unguarded sample mean, generalizes to different loads sharing a routing signature. Global environment scoping retains the existing campaign concurrency assumptions.
