# simulation/engine/setup.jl · _rhs*

## Purpose
The RHS execution planner and its knobs: batch and flat-queue thresholds, packet-scheduler parameters, harmonics batching, cost-model settings and the routing chain itself.

## Design & Implementation
Thirty-odd `_rhs_*` readers feed `_snapshot_rhs_plan_env_config`; `_rhs_execution_plan` and `_rhs_execution_plan_uncached` implement the routing chain with its forced modes and heuristics; `_rhs_flat_supported` and the single-effector flat predicates gate the flat queue; `_rhs_effectors_have_heavy_or_heterogeneous_cost` judges the effector set.
