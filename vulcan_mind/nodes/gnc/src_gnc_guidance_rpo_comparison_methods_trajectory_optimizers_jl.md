# gnc/guidance/rpo/comparison_methods/trajectory_optimizers.jl

## Purpose
Gradient-based and stochastic trajectory optimisers used as comparison baselines: CHOMP-style smoothing with obstacle gradients and STOMP-style sampled updates.

## Design & Implementation
Waypoint packing helpers convert between paths and internal-point vectors; `rpo_chomp_obstacle_potential` and `rpo_chomp_numeric_gradient` define the CHOMP objective; `rpo_stomp_plan_path` and `rpo_stomp_waypoint_state_cost` implement STOMP; `rpo_second_difference_metric` is the smoothness term.
