# simulation/callbacks/density_callbacks/runtime.jl

## Purpose
The density callback body: per-satellite or batched atmosphere evaluation through the vacuum cache, the track cache or the model, written into shared buffers.

## Design & Implementation
`_density_state_from_kinematics!` is the three-tier decision; `update_density_sat!` is the per-satellite unit; `affect!` chooses batch, threaded or serial and reports timing to the policy; small extractors and buffer writers surround them.
