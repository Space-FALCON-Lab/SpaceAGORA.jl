## Residual Risk

The tangential-start limitation above remains. Enabling EDG's existing crossing
pipeline also enables staged density where required, exit-time thruster scheduling
that may call guidance, and solver-phase reapplication. Additional root stops can
change integration steps and trajectories. Startup phase classification affects
non-EDG configurations using atmosphere-dependent settings too. Between-pass
commands can change forces when density, solar pressure or attitude coupling is active.
No bitwise mission-equivalence or performance claim is made. Numerical guidance/control
formulas, scientific limits and recomputation policy are unchanged. Full missions,
scientific variants, prediction/actuation separation and independent EDG packaging
remain separate work.
