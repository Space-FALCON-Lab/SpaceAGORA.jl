# analysis/verification/telemetry_verification/manifest_parsing.jl/ (small files)

## Purpose
The small standalone helpers of manifest parsing: boolean-string interpretation, solver mode and iteration-cap resolution, and the request-to-study conversions.

## Design & Implementation
`_safe_parse_bool` interprets on/off spellings, `_is_maxiters_error` recognises a solver failure by message, `_study_config` and `_request_from_study_config` convert between the programmatic `VerificationRequest` and the runner's `StudyConfig`, and `parse_cli` reads the command line.
