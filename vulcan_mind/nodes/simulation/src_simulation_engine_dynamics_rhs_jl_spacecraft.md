# simulation/engine/dynamics_rhs.jl · spacecraft*

## Purpose
The six right-hand-side entry points the solvers call: monolithic, slow, fast-control, gravity backbone, implicit atmosphere and explicit remainder.

## Design & Implementation
`spacecraft_dynamics!` is the monolithic RHS; `spacecraft_dynamics_slow!` and `spacecraft_dynamics_fast_control!` split control from dynamics at two rates; `spacecraft_dynamics_gravity_backbone!`, `spacecraft_dynamics_implicit_atmosphere!` and `spacecraft_dynamics_explicit_remainder!` serve the partitioned solvers. Each obtains an execution plan and routes to the flat queue when chosen.
