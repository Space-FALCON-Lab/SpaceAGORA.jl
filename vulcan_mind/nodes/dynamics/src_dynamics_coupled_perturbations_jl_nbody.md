# dynamics/coupled/perturbations.jl · _nbody*

## Purpose
Third-body gravity evaluation: per-body acceleration kernels and the position sources they draw on — ephemeris cache, single-epoch memo, or live SPICE.

## Design & Implementation
`_nbody_acceleration_ii` and `_nbody_body_force_ii` implement the direct-minus-indirect term; `_nbody_body_position_from_cache_j2000_m` interpolates the table; the `_from_spice_*` trio routes between memoised and direct queries with atomic call counting; `_nbody_workspace_for_sat!` supplies scratch.
