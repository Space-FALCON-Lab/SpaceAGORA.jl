# parallel/routing/outer_route_state.jl

## Purpose
The learned outer-routing statistics and their persistence: features, per-route stats, tuning constants and the state container.

## Design & Implementation
`OuterRouteFeatures` describes a campaign, `OuterRouteStats` accumulates samples and timings per route, `OuterRouteTuning` holds exploration constants, and `OuterRouteState` maps signatures to stats; `load_outer_route_state!`, `save_outer_route_state` and `reset_outer_route_state!` manage the on-disk copy.
