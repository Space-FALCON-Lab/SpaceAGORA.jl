# Independent Review of PR #593

## Scope

Reviewed the complete change from base `3ee4b5eac76eea1a6d65a6e3bf18d1ea4ac9ebfe` to head `bcea6261b586acb2d89e15c9a852324387fc1415`. The change adds an experimental lifecycle extension (version 0.1.0) to the external propagation contract 2.0.0. The extension is versioned on its own and is opt-in. An external owner that opts in gets three things:

- a run-local force service that returns the total configured acceleration, including the held control command;
- synchronization before each periodic control tick, and `external_control_changed!` after it;
- checkpoints of its own state, with identity checks, an endpoint handoff and an exact restore check.

Every saved output time of a run with a configured-force owner is a solver stop.

The review had two passes:

- The first pass covered head `311f85abd4804e7fb51b4987d95788ec6e4be025`.
- The recheck covered the two disposition commits `14c3447f3` (refusals, runtime check, tests) and `bcea6261b` (documentation).

The first two commits of the branch were ported from an earlier experimental branch (`7f4fcebe5`, `a7f602812`). The port was compared with `git range-diff`. It brings the original changes onto current main, which adds effector scopes to the force service and GNC scope positions to the control tick. The output-stop commit reads the live inner saver of the run-clock saving callback, which the original did not have.

Areas examined:

- Whether the force service matches what the run's right-hand side applies to an ordinary spacecraft, including effector scopes, GNC scopes (`_control_members`) and environment sampling.
- The order of owner synchronization, control update and `external_control_changed!` within a tick, including several clocks ticking at once and ticks pending at a resume.
- The checkpoint endpoint handoff, the checkpoint save and restore, the identity and force-configuration checks, the exact restore check, and the output-stop callback.
- Interactions with features on main: GNC and effector scopes, saved results rows kept in checkpoints, the run-clock saver, control forecasts, link attitudes, maneuver buffers, orbit and entry counters, `_solve_run_interval!` and cached integrators, and operational mission segments.
- Owners that do not opt in, including SpaceAGORAMuJoCo at the pinned gitlink `a73635fecb975894f27a24e65fd38146eba3f154` and the companion starter under `templates/starters/companion/`.
- The contributor note, the reference page, the adapter standard and the architecture page.

## Findings

- **P2, fixed by refusal: decimal cadences crashed the solve through the output stops.**
  - Cause: checkpoint segment ends are built by adding the interval, while the output grid is built by index. With a decimal interval, a save time landed one ulp after a segment start and became a solver stop the step size could not reach.
  - Reproduced on the first head:
    - owner step 0.1 s, checkpoint interval 0.1 s, output interval 0.5 s;
    - control period 0.3 s, output interval 0.1 s, checkpoint interval 0.3 s.
  - Both aborted with retcode `Unstable`, after the integrator reported that dt was forced below floating-point epsilon. The same configurations without an owner completed.
  - Disposition: `_check_external_lifecycle_cadences!` in `src/orchestration/simulation/engine/external_propagation.jl` now requires binary-exact times for a configured-force owner. The owner step, control periods, output interval, mission end and checkpoint interval must be whole multiples of 2^-20 s. The control periods, the checkpoint interval and the checkpointed mission end must also be exact multiples of the owner step.
  - Rechecked: sums and whole multiples of such times are exact below 2^33 s, so segment ends, the output grid, control ticks and the owner grid coincide bit for bit. `floor(t / step)` is exact at every such time, because a non-integer quotient of two such times lies farther from an integer than the division's rounding error. Both reproductions are now refused at setup with the new message.

- **P2, fixed by refusal: the 16-eps clock checks were stricter than the sync contract and the run clock.**
  - Where: the checks before ticks, at the handoff, at the checkpoint and at restore.
  - Reproduced on the first head: an owner that syncs to `floor(t / step)`, as the 2.0.0 docstring of `external_sync!` reads, failed at the first control tick with period 0.3 s and step 0.1 s.
  - Computed: with a 0.1 s checkpoint interval, accumulated segment ends drift past 16 eps from the owner grid after 126 segments.
  - Disposition: the binary-exact rule above makes every lifecycle time exact. The tolerance is therefore no longer exercised by rounding, and the literal floor is exact. Rechecked: the reproduction is refused at setup.

- **P2, fixed by refusal: checkpoint-only owners were admitted but documented as not covered.**
  - Reproduced on the first head: an owner with `checkpoint=true` and `configured_forces=false` passed setup and resumed. Its saved rows differed from the uninterrupted run, because rows between owner steps are taken from the shadow integration.
  - Disposition: setup now refuses `checkpoint` without `configured_forces`. Rechecked: refused with the new message.

- **P2, fixed in documentation: the adapter standard required a new contract version for any added name.**
  - The PR adds hooks and a named list to `ExternalPropagationServices` while keeping contract 2.0.0. Keeping 2.0.0 is what lets existing companions keep loading.
  - Disposition: `docs/src/reference/adapter_contract.md` now defines when an opt-in extension may keep the contract version: its own version and exact version check, its own named list, and unchanged base inventories and behavior for owners that do not opt in. The contract history lists lifecycle extension 0.1.0.

- **P3, fixed: the restart test emulated a stop by running a shorter mission and resuming with a longer one.**
  - That is bit-faithful only when nothing depends on the mission end. The planet-frame table covers the run's own span, so with J2 such an emulation differed in the last bit. Ordinary spacecraft do the same on main.
  - The independent review stopped a same-mission run after its checkpoint, with a throwing callback, and resumed it with the same configuration. Rows and owner state were bit-identical without gravity, with central gravity and with J2.
  - Disposition: the test now uses this stop. It covers no gravity and J2, at a control tick and between two ticks. The docs state the same-mission-end condition.

- **P3, fixed in part: refusal coverage.**
  - A table now covers each setup refusal with its message: decimal step, output interval, mission end and checkpoint interval; off-grid checkpoint and period; checkpoint-only owner; malformed capabilities; two spacecraft; attitude simulation; gravity-gradient torque; undeclared dynamic effector; event-driven control.
  - The runtime refusals are also tested with their messages: torque, propellant flow, a write to the owned state, and nonfinite service input.
  - Still not exercised: guidance and navigation effectors, interlinks, mounted or non-ideal control, and a nonfinite service output. See Residual Risk.

- **P3, fixed: documentation accuracy.**
  - The architecture page now lists the owner state among the run state a checkpoint holds, and qualifies the sentence that external owners refuse checkpoints.
  - Constant mass and the absence of torque are described as runtime checks.
  - The handoff diagnostic is described as before and after values.
  - The `external_capabilities` docstring gives the exact named-tuple shape and key order.
  - Keys and snapshots must survive `Serialization` and compare with `isequal`.
  - The force service is single-threaded.
  - The companion boundary page mentions `LIFECYCLE_FUNCTIONS`.

- **P3, fixed: redundant endpoint-row rebuild.** With output stops, the saver's row at a segment end is already the synchronized owner state. The rebuild also called save-field getters with a reduced integrator view. It is removed, and `_append_checkpoint_saved_segment!` matches main. Rechecked: the faithful stop and resume with central gravity is still bit-identical after the removal.

- **P3, fixed: a control update that wrote the owned state was silently overwritten at the next sync.** `_external_after_control_tick!` now stops the run if a control update changed the owned position or velocity. This is tested.

- **P3, documented: the stage key may miss environment or spacecraft parameters a custom model reads.** The `external_stage_key` docstring and the contributor note now require the key to cover any environment or spacecraft parameter the model reads that the engine's force configuration does not record.

- **P3, accepted as residual: per-step cost.** A configured-force owner is installed into the shadow state, with a derivative discontinuity, at every accepted step. This costs extra right-hand-side evaluations. Correctness is unaffected.

- **P3, accepted (recheck observation): the test's stop helper treats any `CompositeException` as the intended stop.** The assertion that follows, that the checkpoint exists at the stop time, guards against an unrelated failure being counted as the stop.

**Opted-in scope.**
- The force service follows the right-hand side's serial effector sum and control path, including effector and GNC scopes.
- Its environment sampling writes no shared buffers.

- The configurations that crashed on the first head are now refused at setup.

Control forecasts and operational mission segments refuse external propagators. Interlinks, guidance and continuous events are refused for configured-force owners.

## Residual Risk

- **The extension is experimental and narrow.** It admits one constant-mass spacecraft in translation only, the simple ephemerides frame backend, central or J2 gravity, declared stage-safe models, ideal periodic control and binary-exact cadences. Decimal cadences and checkpoint-only owners are refused until they are supported.
- **Stage safety is a declaration.** At run time the engine checks propellant rates, mass rate, torque, finiteness and writes to the owned state. A model that keeps hidden history or otherwise breaks the purity rule is caught only in part.
- **Some refusals are untested.** Guidance and navigation effectors, interlinks, mounted or non-ideal control, and a nonfinite service output have refusal code but no test.
- **Restart equality is bit-for-bit only for a resume with the same configuration, mission end included.** A resume with a later mission end can differ in the last bit with J2, as an ordinary spacecraft does on main.
- **Output stops cover the engine's result table only.** The returned solution's per-step states, `TrajectoryRecorder` and user saving callbacks still hold shadow states between synchronizations. This is documented.
- **The force service reuses one trial buffer.** Adapters must call it from one thread at a time. This is documented.
- **Per-step cost.** A configured-force owner is installed at every accepted step with a derivative discontinuity, which adds right-hand-side evaluations.
- **The test owner is a fourth-order Runge-Kutta integrator, not a production owner.** The extension says nothing about an adapter's numerical accuracy, and no production owner exists in this repository.

Recommendation: no unresolved P1 or P2 findings. The change is merge-ready for its stated experimental scope, subject to the repository's CI and the author's pending runs.
