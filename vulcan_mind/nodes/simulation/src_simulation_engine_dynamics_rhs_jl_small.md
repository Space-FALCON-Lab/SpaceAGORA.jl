# simulation/engine/dynamics_rhs.jl/ (small files)

## Purpose
The scheduling and derivative-assignment helpers of the RHS file: work-item and packet preparation, scratch sizing, partition predicates, effector evaluation, orientation and heat-rate derivatives, and atmosphere short-circuit tests.

## Design & Implementation
`_evaluate_dynamic_effector` routes an effector to wrench or legacy evaluation; `_prepare_rhs_flat_work_items!`, `_prepare_rhs_flat_work_packets!`, `_build_constellation_execution_plan!` and `_ensure_rhs_flat_effector_scratch!` set up the flat queue; `_assign_orientation_rhs!` and `_assign_heat_rate_derivative!` write derivatives; the `_has_any_*`, `_count_*`, `_partition_*` and `_flat_*` predicates classify effectors.
