## Findings

- **P3, resolved — Qualify the full-rank requirement for push-only reachability.** `docs/src/user/edge_scheduling.md:273-275` says that a full-rank Gramian is necessary for a target to have a nonnegative solution of `R u = y`. This is false for an individual target: `R = [1; 0]`, `y = [1; 0]`, and `u = [1]` give a reachable target with a rank-one, two-dimensional Gramian. Full row rank is necessary to reach every output target with signed inputs; even then it does not guarantee push-only controllability. Rephrase the sentence around reaching every output direction, while retaining the nonnegative feasibility condition for an individual target. The coordinating review corrected the isolated merge checkout to say that a rank-deficient Gramian can allow particular targets, while full rank is necessary to reach every output target and does not alone guarantee push-only reachability. That exact diff was inspected and accepted. The implementation was already correct: `minimum_input_energy` checks the actual target residual rather than requiring full rank. No unresolved findings remain.

## Residual Risk

The implementation is a planning model evaluated at discrete sample times, with user-supplied linear dynamics. It does not establish feasibility between samples or validate the resulting inputs against nonlinear propagation, finite power limits, or positive nominal-force bounds. The documented signed minimum-energy solution is not a nonnegative constrained optimizer; a negative component does not prove that no different nonnegative solution exists. These limitations are explicit and acceptable for this API.

Output scaling, dependent output rows, near-singular Gramians, and the choice of log-determinant regularizer remain modeling responsibilities. Range outputs remove rigid-motion sensitivity but do not automatically guarantee independent rows for every formation and pair set. Greedy selection has no claimed optimality bound under the combined terminal and duty-cycle constraints. The guide appropriately limits its intended scale to small formations and short horizons.

Recommendation: implementation is merge-ready subject to the repository's CI/review-artifact requirements, including the inspected P3 documentation correction described above.
