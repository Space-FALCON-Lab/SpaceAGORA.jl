# vehicle/thermal/

## Purpose
Aerodynamic heating models for aerobraking: the free-molecular Maxwellian heat rate on a flat plate, with optional convective and radiative formulations for the transitional regime.

## Design & Implementation
`MaxwellianHeat` carries the thermal accommodation factor and the planet whose gas constant and specific-heat ratio it needs; `getHeatRate` dispatches on the model to `heatrate_convective`, `heatrate_radiative` or `heatrate_convective_radiative`, each returning watts per square centimetre from density, temperature, speed ratio and incidence; `VehicleThermalModels` is the module wrapper.

## Limitations
Wall temperature is not integrated as a state — heating is reported as an instantaneous rate and an integrated load only — so re-radiation from a hot panel is not modelled.
