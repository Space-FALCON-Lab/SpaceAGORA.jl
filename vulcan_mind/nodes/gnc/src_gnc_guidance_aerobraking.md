# gnc/guidance/aerobraking/

## Purpose
Per-pass aerobraking guidance: given the state at atmospheric entry, compute the angle-of-attack switching profile that hits the pass target under an energy-based or time-based strategy.

## Design & Implementation
`interfaces.jl` defines the input/output records and the strategy singletons; `dispatcher.jl` maps a kind to a strategy; `e_edg/` and `t_edg/` implement the two strategies; `common/` holds the closed-form trajectory and switching-function solvers both share.
