## Findings

Author's notes for the reviewer (no P1 or P2 known to the author):

- **Low, strict mode by design.** The warnings use `_preflight_warning`, the shared
  pre-run warning for configuration checks (log group `:preflight`). They do not
  use the deprecation helper, since a period mismatch is not a deprecation. Under
  `--depwarn=error` it throws, so a mismatched configuration fails strict runs.
  None of the repository's configurations has one (see the evidence).
- **Low, semantics.** Equality is exact (G-5 = a). Each warning fires once per
  model type and per kind of mismatch (`maxlog=1`, keyed on `(T, :gnc_period_s)` or
  `(T, :discretization_step)`).
- **Low, scope.** Only periodic control models are checked. Event-driven ones have
  no periodic callback. The planner's agreement with its controller stays in its
  own preflight until PR 7.
- **Note, struct change.** The two controller structs gain a last field. Their only
  constructor calls are inside the package.
- **Note, registry.** `gnc_period_s` needs a row in `docs/extension_hooks.jl` once
  #361 is on main. The row is prepared and will be its own commit.

Reviewer to verify:
