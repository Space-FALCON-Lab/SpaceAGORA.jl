# vehicle/actuators/

## Purpose
Thruster models and their firing logic: a base thruster with a Schmitt-trigger ramp, the six-axis RPO thruster set, and impulse integration.

## Design & Implementation
`BaseThrusterModel` and `SixAxisThrusterModel`; `schmitt_trigger` and `thrust_calculation_schmitt_trigger!` shape thrust rise and fall; `update_thrusters!` advances per-thruster state; `integrate_impulse!` accumulates delivered impulse; `ThrusterHooks` and `ThrusterModels` are the interface and module.
