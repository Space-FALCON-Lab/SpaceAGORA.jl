# gnc/robotics/robot_arm_hypr/clearance.jl

## Purpose
Clearance statistics for a sampled robot-arm trajectory against the configured sphere obstacles, the feasibility term of the arm planner's cost.

## Design & Implementation
`robot_arm_clearance_stats_from_samples` runs forward kinematics at every sample, treats each link as a capsule of its radius between joint origins, measures the distance to every `RobotArmSphereObstacle`, and returns the minimum clearance and the fraction of samples in violation, in the same shape the RPO planner's clearance statistics use.
