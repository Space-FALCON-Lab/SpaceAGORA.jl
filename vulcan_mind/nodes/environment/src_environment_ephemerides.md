# environment/ephemerides/

## Purpose
Planets and their orientation over time: planet constants and kernels, analytic and SPICE ephemerides, and topography.

## Design & Implementation
`planets.jl` defines `Earth`, `Mars`, `Venus`, `Moon` and their constants and furnishes SPICE kernels; `ephemerides_models.jl` declares the ephemerides abstraction; `simple_ephemerides.jl` is the kernel-free analytic model; `planet_shapes.jl` evaluates spherical-harmonic topography.
