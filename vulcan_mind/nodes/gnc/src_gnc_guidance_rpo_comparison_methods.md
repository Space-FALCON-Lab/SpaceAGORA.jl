# gnc/guidance/rpo/comparison_methods/

## Purpose
Baseline planners and the benchmarking harness used to compare HYPR against RRT*, CHOMP and STOMP on the same scenarios.

## Design & Implementation
`planner_comparison.jl` runs each planner over a scenario set, records cost, runtime and clearance, and produces plots and artefact slugs; `trajectory_optimizers.jl` implements the CHOMP and STOMP optimisers over internal waypoints with obstacle potentials.
