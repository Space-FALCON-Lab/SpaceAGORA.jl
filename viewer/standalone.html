SpaceAGORA viewer

Open a trajectory straight from a data file. No simulation, no Julia: the page builds the scene here in the browser.

Trajectory

CSV with a time column (seconds) and either SpaceAGORA's sc1_pos_1..3 / sc1_vel_1..3 / sc1_q_1..4 columns (its own results CSV opens as is, several spacecraft included) or plain x,y,z[,vx,vy,vz][,qx,qy,qz,qw]; positions inertial (J2000) about the body's center. Several files open as an ensemble with a 3σ tube around the first (the nominal).

Body and epoch

The epoch sets the body's rotation (IAU 2009 pole and prime meridian), so the ground track lands where it should.

Spacecraft
Reference (optional)

A second table on the same epoch, drawn as a translucent copy of the spacecraft with its separation reported.

Page
Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, Space to pause, F to follow.
