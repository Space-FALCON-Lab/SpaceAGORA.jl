# dynamics/multibody_cloth/cloth_robot_arm_dynamics.jl

## Purpose
Adapts the compliant model to the serial robot arm: builds the arm's bodies and joints from the kinematic model, tracks a planned joint trajectory, and writes the coupled derivatives into the spacecraft RHS.

## Design & Implementation
Constructs a `CompliantMultibodyModel` from `ClothArmModel` links, derives per-joint rest quaternions from the plan's reference angles at time `t`, applies actuator torques, and `assign_coupled_cloth_robot_arm_rhs!` folds the arm's base reaction into the bus force and torque while integrating the arm state.
