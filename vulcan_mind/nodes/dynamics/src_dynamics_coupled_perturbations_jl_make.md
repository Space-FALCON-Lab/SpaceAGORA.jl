# dynamics/coupled/perturbations.jl · _make*

## Purpose
Allocators for the harmonics and N-body scratch workspaces, single-satellite and batched.

## Design & Implementation
`_make_harmonics_scratch_workspace` and `_make_harmonics_batch_workspace` allocate the Helmholtz arrays with the position-independent diagonal preinitialised; `_make_nbody_scratch_workspace` allocates the per-body vectors.
