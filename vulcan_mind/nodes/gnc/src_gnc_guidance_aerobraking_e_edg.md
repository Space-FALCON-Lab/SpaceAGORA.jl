# gnc/guidance/aerobraking/e_edg/

## Purpose
Energy-based entry-descent guidance: chooses the switch time so the pass depletes a target amount of orbital energy.

## Design & Implementation
`compute_e_edg_guidance_window!` is the entry; `switch_calculation` and `switch_calculation_with_integration` solve the switch closed-form or by integration; `second_time_switch_recalc` variants refine after the first switch; `security_mode` falls back to a safe low-drag profile when the solve fails.
