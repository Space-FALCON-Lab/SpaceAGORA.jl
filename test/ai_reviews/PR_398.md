# Review record: PR 398: gimballed thruster directions for the viewer

## Scope

Review of the SpaceAGORA `thruster-direction-frames` branch against main
(e5cc7d1ed). The core half of showing a gimballed thruster's turned cone and
plume in the viewer: a save field and a viewer-frames block; the viewer's
half is a change of the companion SpaceAGORAVisualizer.jl. One commit.

Authorship disclosure: this record was drafted by the author of the PR. The
plan assigns behaviour PRs of the actuator layer an independent reviewer (the
PI, or the second reviewer when available); this record does not replace that
review.

Independent review: the coordinator reviewed the change under Giusy's delegation on
2026-10-18: the `thruster_direction` field is saved only when the scene is saved and a
spacecraft carries a thruster gimbal; it stores each thruster's normalized exhaust
direction in its link's frame; the viewer payload adds the `thruster_direction` frame
block only when those columns exist, so runs without gimbals are unchanged.

## Update after review (c48305045)

- The automated review's P1 (comment 4305363023): a sample inside a step is
  saved after the callbacks at the step's end, and the saving callback comes
  after the control callbacks, so when a mounted controller turned a gimbal
  at the step's end, the step's earlier samples took the new direction, and
  the viewer turned the cone and plume before the tick. Fixed:
  `ThrusterGimbal` keeps the angle before its latest tick, and
  `_save_thruster_directions` uses it for a sample before the end of a step
  at which the gimbal ticked (control ticks are solver stops, so a step never
  contains one; the check uses the step's end, not the gimbal's last tick
  time alone, so a spacecraft reused from an earlier run is not misread).
- New test in `test/unit/vehicle/thruster_gimbal_tests.jl`: ticks every 5 s,
  a sample every 0.5 s, the gimbal turned at the 10 s tick; the samples
  before 10 s keep the nominal exhaust direction and those from 10 s on the
  turned one. It fails on 938103094 (the samples of the step ending at 10 s
  are turned) and passes on c48305045.
- On c48305045: `thruster_gimbal_tests` 31 of 31, `scene_visualization_tests`
  212 of 212, `viewer_bundle_tests`, `apollo_descent_tests` 181 of 181 and
  `actuator_fault_tests` 38 of 38. The payload format is unchanged, so the
  companion change is unaffected.
