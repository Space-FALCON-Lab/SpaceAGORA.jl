# gnc/control/heat_load_control.jl

## Purpose
The heat-load sub-mode of energy-depletion guidance: solves for the low-drag window within a pass that keeps integrated heat load under its limit while depleting as much energy as possible.

## Design & Implementation
Closed-form and integrated trajectory predictors (`_edg_closed_form_heat_load_trajectory`, `_edg_heat_load_profile_for_k`), the two-point boundary solve for the switch pair (`_edg_balanced_tpbvp_heat_load_window`, `_edg_solve_heat_load_switches`), the alpha-profile builders under the constraints, the Kepler helpers for passage duration, and `_edg_heat_load_low_drag_active` which the controller polls.
