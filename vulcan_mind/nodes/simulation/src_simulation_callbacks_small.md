# simulation/callbacks/ (small files)

## Purpose
The `SimulationCallbacks` module and the small builders for guidance and navigation callbacks, plus the two submodule declarations.

## Design & Implementation
`SimulationCallbacks` includes every callback file; `get_guidance_callbacks` and `get_navigation_callbacks` build one periodic callback per effector at its rate; `density_callbacks` and `gram_track_cache` are the submodule wrappers.
