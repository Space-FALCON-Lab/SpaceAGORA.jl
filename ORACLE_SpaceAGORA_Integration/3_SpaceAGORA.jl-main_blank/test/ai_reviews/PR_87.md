# AI Review — PR #87: GMST-aligned Earth rotation for the kernel-free ephemerides backend

## Scope

A geographic-frame registration fix on the kernel-free ephemerides backend. `SimpleEphemeridesModel`'s default Earth frame was a spin about +z with a zero prime meridian at the J2000 epoch — Greenwich planted on the vernal equinox instead of its GMST-aligned position (280.46 deg at J2000), with a further slow drift because the stored spin rate sat below the mean sidereal rate. Every geographic (lat/lon-keyed) model evaluated under the default simple backend — `:igrf` and `:dipole` magnetic fields, tesseral gravity harmonics, lat/lon-dependent atmospheres, geodetic outputs — sampled a longitude shifted by that angle. Pure z-rotation error: magnitudes and latitude structure were unaffected. The SPICE backend (ITRF93 chain) was independently verified healthy and is untouched. A follow-up commit corrects the stored Earth spin rate so the frame propagation and the `planet.ω` transport/co-rotation consumers advance together.

## Changed Files

- `src/environment/ephemerides/simple_ephemerides.jl` — `prime_meridian_at_reference_rad` defaults to `NaN`, a sentinel for the planet-true convention: Earth's rotation angle is IAU-82 GMST (`_earth_gmst_iau82_rad`) evaluated on the model's leap-second-free UTC timeline (UT1 ≈ UTC, under 4e-3 deg of rotation); an explicit finite prime meridian keeps the legacy linear formula exactly; non-Earth planets keep zero-at-reference. `ephemerides_cache_key` maps the sentinel to `typemin(Int64)` (`round` would throw on NaN).
- `src/SpaceAGORA.jl` — `SimpleEphemeridesModel` docstring states the convention and the override semantics.
- `src/environment/ephemerides/planets.jl` — Earth `ω[3]` set to the IAU-82 GMST mean rate so the frame's angular rate and the transport term in the velocity transforms (`r_intor_p!`, `_planet_relative_state`) and the aero co-rotation wind agree.
- `src/environment/ephemerides/planet_data.jl` — same constant in the legacy compatibility table.

## Findings

- The defect was measured against two independent references before any change: SatelliteToolboxTransformations' `r_eci_to_ecef(J2000(), PEF(), jd)` and a hand-implemented IAU-82 GMST. Offset 79.54 deg at J2000, shrinking ~0.155 deg/yr — a pure z-rotation (trace-angle equals z-angle to 1e-4 deg). It decomposes exactly into the missing GMST epoch value plus the spin-rate deficit.
- `SpiceEphemeridesModel` agrees with the independent IERS-consistent implementation to ≤0.0022 deg across 2000–2025 (UT1−UTC plus PEF-vs-ITRF93 polar-motion floor); no change needed on that path.
- Blast-radius audit: all shipped examples pair the simple backend with `NoAtmosphereModel`; telemetry verification uses the SPICE backend; the magnetic-field tests assert bands/signs/trends; the reference-system dispatch probe asserts only self-consistency. One test embedded the old convention (below).
- **P2 (convention-dependent test)**: the harmonics normalization parity test in suite 04 fed a body-fixed position in as the inertial state and compared the inertial output force to SatelliteToolboxGravityModels' body-fixed acceleration — an identity that held only while the frame was exactly `I` at `et = 0`. Only its rotation-variant tesseral comparison failed (~1e-4 relative); zonal terms are rotation-invariant. Repaired to transform through the effector's own planet frame, making it convention-agnostic.
- **Codex (post-open)**: with the frame propagating at the GMST rate, the transport term in the frame velocity transforms and the aero co-rotation wind still used the previous slightly-low `planet.ω[3]`, leaving an Earth-fixed point with ~0.6 mm/s of spurious planet-fixed velocity under the default simple backend (the SPICE-backed frame carried a same-order mismatch all along, since the stored constant was below the true rotation rate). Fixed by setting the stored rate to the IAU-82 GMST mean rate in both definition sites; the finite-differenced frame rate now matches `planet.ω[3]` to ~1e-15 rad/s (the GMST quadratic term), a residual of ~1e-8 m/s at the equator. The golden Earth regression tolerances (10 m position / 0.01 m/s velocity) dwarf the ~0.6 mm/s co-rotation shift, and no test pins the old constant literally.

## P1 Assessment

The misregistered default frame was itself P1-class for any geographic-field study under the kernel-free backend; this PR removes it. The `NaN` default changes results for existing default-constructed `SimpleEphemeridesModel` runs by design (that is the fix); the explicit-override escape hatch preserves the legacy convention bit-exactly for anyone who needs it.

Unresolved P1: No

## Tests Added/Updated

- `test/probes/reference_system_probes.jl` — new "SimpleEphemeridesModel Earth rotation pins" testset: pins the frame against an independent degree-form IAU-82 GMST formulation (not a transcription of the source's seconds-form series; 1e-5 deg), cross-checks the SPICE ITRF93 chain at four epochs spanning the vendored kernels (0.5 deg), and guards the explicit-override formula, the non-Earth default, the exact z-invariant block, and the sentinel cache key (no throw, distinct from an explicit zero).
- `test/suites/04_solver_env_and_regression_tests.jl` — the harmonics parity comparison transforms through the effector's planet frame instead of assuming identity at the epoch.
- Full `test/runtests.jl` green locally after each commit, including the golden Earth regression, probe drivers, and the magnetic/aero probe suites.

## Residual Risk

The simple backend remains a mean-sidereal model: no equation of equinoxes, precession-nutation, or polar motion, and UT1 is read as UTC — residual vs the full IERS chain is sub-half-degree over the vendored kernels' span and is pinned at that tolerance by the probe. Non-Earth planets keep the zero-at-reference convention (their IAU prime-meridian epoch values are not modeled); geographic registration for Mars or Venus under the simple backend is a known follow-on if a lat/lon-keyed model is ever paired with one. The legacy lowercase-named planet table in `src/environment/ephemerides/planet_data.jl` would not match the `"Earth"` name check and would keep the linear convention — it has no callers outside its own file and is retained only for the old positional constructor.
