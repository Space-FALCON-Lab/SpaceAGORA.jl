## Findings

Author's notes for the reviewer (no blocking finding known to the author):

- **Low, compatibility by design:** until step 5 moves the HYPR pin, every job that
  installs HYPR from the pinned source fails, including the unit shards that load it
  through `test/helpers/load_hypr.jl`, because the pinned HYPR 0.1.1 asks for
  contract 1.0.0. That is the lockstep protocol (hook design 4.8).
- **Note, #350:** #350 added the per-facade table and the "Contract history" table;
  this branch was rebased onto it and changes only the HYPRServices entry and row.
- **Note, enforcement of the rule:** the facade cannot see keyword defaults in the
  provider's methods. The rule is enforced by version: the facade accepts only HYPR
  0.2.0, and HYPR 0.2.0's integration test checks that each of the five planners
  throws `UndefKeywordError` without `rng`.
- **Note, shim version:** the shim stays at 0.2.0 with a narrower `HYPR` compat; a
  version bump would also need HYPR's weak-dependency compat to change.

Verified before acceptance:

- **Only versions change in `src/`:** `check_provider` and `activate!` are unchanged;
  the constants are the whole change.
- **No core caller omits `rng`:** the random-stream gate (#343) scans `src/` and
  `packages/SpaceAGORAHYPR/src`, and the HYPR-integrated tests below call through
  the facade.
- **The docs read correctly**, in particular that the inventory is unchanged.
- **The HYPR PR's hosted CI result:** all ten checks of Space-FALCON-Lab/HYPR.jl#5 passed at `3990f31`, with its integration CI pinned to this branch's contract commit `aa2aa3efe`: the core job at one and four threads, the documentation job, and the integration job in both load orders, each in the push and the pull-request run.
