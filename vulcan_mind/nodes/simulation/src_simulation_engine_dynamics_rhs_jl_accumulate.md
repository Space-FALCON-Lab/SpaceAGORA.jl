# simulation/engine/dynamics_rhs.jl · _accumulate*

## Purpose
Effector summation: per-satellite accumulation of dynamic and control effectors, partition-aware variants for the split solver, and the vectorised all-satellite kernels of the flat path.

## Design & Implementation
`_accumulate_dynamic_effectors!` and `_accumulate_control_effectors!` serve the per-satellite RHS; `_accumulate_dynamic_effectors_partitioned!` filters by solver partition; `_accumulate_dynamic_effectors_flat_batch!` drives the flat queue and dispatches batchable effectors to the `_accumulate_*_flat_batch!` kernels for N-body, SRP, inverse-square, J2 and harmonics.
