# environment/atmosphere/density_models.jl · _polyfit*

## Purpose
Evaluation helpers for the polynomial log-density fit that the GRAM `getDensity` methods use above the entry interface on non-Keplerian runs, where a full GRAM sample would be wasted on a smooth, near-vacuum region.

## Design & Implementation
`_polyfit_eval_altitude_km` clamps the altitude into the model's validity band and converts to kilometres, `_polyfit_log_density` evaluates the coefficients by Horner's rule and clamps the result to the finite range of `exp`, and `_polyfit_density` exponentiates. All three are `@inline` and allocation-free.

## Limitations
Clamping to the validity band makes density constant outside it rather than decaying, and `density_polyfit` rebuilds the model struct from the planet on every call instead of caching it.
