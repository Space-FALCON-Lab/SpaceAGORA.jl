# gnc/robotics/

## Purpose
Motion planning for the robot arm: quintic joint trajectories for simple moves and the HYPR swarm planner for obstacle-aware moves that minimise base disturbance.

## Design & Implementation
`robot_arm_planning.jl` defines `RobotArmPlan`, `RobotArmPlannerConfig` and quintic sampling; `robot_arm_hypr.jl` includes the `robot_arm_hypr/` planner — config, RRT warm start, swarm and retiming, clearance and planner core.
