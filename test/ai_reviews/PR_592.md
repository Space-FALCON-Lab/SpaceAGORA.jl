## Findings

- The density memo hits only on bitwise-equal model, position, time and wind flag, and is off under any perturbation mode. Bit-identical on 13 cases.
- The new stiffness estimate changes default auto-stiff behavior (not bit-identical); the legacy detector remains behind SPACEAGORA_AUTO_STIFF_DETECTOR=legacy.
- The Jacobian branch showed a larger mass-column error at 500 km than the base path.

## P1 Assessment

No P1 identified. The default solver change in the auto-stiff path is the highest-impact item: measured better against a 1e-12 reference on three physical cases, but genuine stiffness was only exercised by one synthetic case and a Van der Pol test.

## Follow-up validation — 2026-11-12

The earlier measurements and residual-risk statements above describe the original
forward-difference implementation. The follow-up replaces it with centered,
per-component differences using cbrt(eps(Float64)) and the actual representable
positive-to-negative displacement. This reduces cancellation in small mass
columns without the first-order truncation error of a larger forward step.

The independent regression uses nonzero exponential atmospheres at 150 km and
500 km, central/J2 gravity and aerodynamic forces. Its analytic mass column is
zero for gravity and -F/m^2 for aerodynamic acceleration. The entire column must
agree within 2e-12; its nonzero along-track component must also agree to 1e-4
relative tolerance (1e-18 absolute floor). Reinstating the original forward
Jacobian fails the absolute bound at both heights (1.9821290266552953e-10 and
6.89161523249751e-11). The centered implementation passes.

Executed locally with Julia 1.12.1, two threads, on the branch integrated with
main d505d4d44:
- colored_fd_jacobian_tests.jl: 34/34 passed, including the analytic checks and
  60-second Rodas5P/auto-stiff trajectories against Tsit5 at 1e-12 tolerance.
  Both routes meet 1 mm position and 1 micrometer/second velocity bounds at both heights.
- auto_stiff_detector_tests.jl: 8/8 passed (smooth orbit and stiff Van der Pol).
- auto_stiff_switch_tests.jl: 6/6 passed.
- density_memo_tests.jl: 12/12 passed.
- Negative control: original forward method fails both new analytic column bounds;
  its trajectory checks still pass. This isolates Jacobian accuracy from trajectory agreement.

The mass-column accuracy concern is resolved for these explicit fixtures. Full CI
on the updated head is still required. Native-GRAM performance was not remeasured.
Centered differences use two RHS evaluations per color rather than one plus a
shared baseline; historical forward-Jacobian performance measurements must not
be treated as measurements of this revised implementation. No new unresolved P1.
