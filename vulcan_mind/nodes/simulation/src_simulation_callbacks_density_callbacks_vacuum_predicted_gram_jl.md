# simulation/callbacks/density_callbacks/vacuum_predicted_gram.jl

## Purpose
A look-ahead density cache: propagate a drag-free trajectory, sample GRAM along it, fit natural cubic splines to log-density and temperature, and interpolate until the real trajectory deviates too far.

## Design & Implementation
`_build_vacuum_gram_cache!` runs `_vacuum_rk4_step` with `_vacuum_j2_accel` over the horizon and samples the model; `_natural_cubic_spline_build!` and `_eval_natural_cubic_spline` fit and evaluate; `_query_vacuum_gram_cache!` checks deviation and answers or rebuilds; the interpolators handle altitude, position and wind.
