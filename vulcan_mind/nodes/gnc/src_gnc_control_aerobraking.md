# gnc/control/aerobraking/

## Purpose
The legacy aerobraking solar-panel controllers and their integrator callbacks: open-loop, heat-load-limited and reference-frame variants.

## Design & Implementation
`control_solarpanels_heatload` and `control_solarpanels_openloop` with their `_impl` bodies, the `f_ctrl!` closed-form control integrands, drag-passage entry and exit condition/affect pairs, time-switch callbacks, and `asim_ctrl_rf` for the rotating-frame formulation. Exceptions are routed through `_control_exception_fallback` under a strict or lenient policy.
