# dynamics/coupled/

## Purpose
The force and torque models that couple the spacecraft to its environment: aerodynamics, gravity, perturbations, thrusters and robot-arm reactions, plus the `DynamicEffectors` module that gathers them.

## Design & Implementation
`force_torque_models.jl` declares `DynamicEffectors` and includes the `force_torque_models/` directory; `aerodynamic_wrench_models.jl` is the free-molecular aerodynamics; `perturbations.jl` holds harmonics gravity, N-body, SRP, magnetic and attitude-control effectors. All expose `calcForceTorque` and, where migrated, `wrench`.
