# gnc/robotics/robot_arm_planning.jl

## Purpose
Simple joint-space planning: quintic polynomial trajectories between joint configurations with zero endpoint velocity and acceleration.

## Design & Implementation
`RobotArmPlan` and `RobotArmPlannerConfig`; `_quintic_scalar` with its first and second derivatives; `_reference_times` builds the time grid; `plan_robot_arm_motion` produces the plan and `robot_arm_plan_sample` evaluates it at a time.
