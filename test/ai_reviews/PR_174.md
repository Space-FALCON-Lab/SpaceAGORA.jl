## Residual Risk

Fresh CI is required after adding this artifact. A fresh artifact-store download,
full simulation, local strict docs build, and native validation campaign were not
rerun in this review. The retained release evidence and existing CI cover those
separate activities only to their stated scopes.

The upstream validation applies to one frozen P20 instant, the specified
80-to-365-km ellipsoidal domain, pointwise queries within 225 seconds of that
instant, and the tested fixed-panel passes with periapsis from 80 to 130 km.
It does not establish other seasons, all controller configurations, flight
accuracy, lower heights, or a seamless connection to the separate near-surface
candidate. Temperature was reported rather than gated, pressure is absent, and
exact-pole winds keep longitude-labelled ENU components. These limits remain
important when interpreting the release's density, wind, drag and heating claims.

Claude's local check wrapper logs child failures but does not itself propagate
all failures as a nonzero final exit. This review relied on individual check
results and its own direct invocations, not that wrapper's final status.

## Clarification (2026-09-30)

This section follows an independent recomputation of the v3 validation on
2026-09-29. It corrects one scope statement in Residual Risk and does not change
the findings or the P1 assessment above.

Residual Risk refers to "the tested fixed-panel passes with periapsis from 80 to
130 km". The retained validation distinguishes three kinds of evidence:

- pointwise checks cover the 80-to-365-km domain down to its 80 km boundary;
- the propagated passes were designed for periapsis from 85 to 130 km, and the
  lowest altitude they reached was 84.56 km;
- fixed-path arcs reach 80.25 km.

No propagated pass covered the 80 to 84.56 km interval.
