# environment/atmosphere/density_models.jl · _nrlmsise*

## Purpose
NRLMSISE-00 support: input validation, the CelesTrak space-indices provider, the seven-slot Ap history assembly, and the model evaluation.

## Design & Implementation
`_nrlmsise_flux_value` and `_nrlmsise_ap_value` validate inputs; `_nrlmsise_space_indices_*` fetch F10.7 and Ap from the dataset and assemble the Ap vector; `_nrlmsise_provider_indices` and `_nrlmsise_resolved_indices` normalise provider results; `_nrlmsise_eval_datetime` and `_nrlmsise_density_state` evaluate the model at an epoch.
