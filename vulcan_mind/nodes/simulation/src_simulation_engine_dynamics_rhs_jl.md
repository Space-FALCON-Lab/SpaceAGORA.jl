# simulation/engine/dynamics_rhs.jl

## Purpose
The right-hand sides of the simulation ODE in every configuration the engine supports: monolithic, split fast-control and slow, and the partitioned implicit-atmosphere, explicit-remainder and gravity-backbone forms, plus the flat constellation effector queue.

## Design & Implementation
Grouped by prefix: `spacecraft_dynamics_*` are the entry points; `_accumulate_*` sum effectors per satellite or across the constellation; `_prefill_*` stage shared samples; `_gravity_backbone_*` serve the split solver; `_rhs_*` and `_constellation_*` implement the flat queue's cost model and work items; `_robot_arm_*` couple the arm; the remainder are derivative assignment and scheduling helpers.
