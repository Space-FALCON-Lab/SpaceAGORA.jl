# dynamics/translational/

## Purpose
The translational right-hand-side assignments: position, velocity and mass derivatives for each RHS variant the engine runs.

## Design & Implementation
`assign_full_translational_rhs!`, `assign_slow_translational_rhs!`, `assign_force_only_translational_rhs!` and `assign_control_only_translational_rhs!` write derivatives for the monolithic, split and fast-control paths; `acceleration_from_force`, `position_derivative`, `zero_position_derivative` and `mass_derivative` are the shared pieces.
