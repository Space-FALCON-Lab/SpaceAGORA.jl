# environment/ (small files)

## Purpose
The two environment module files: `EnvironmentModels`, which includes the atmosphere and gravity files, and the `AerodynamicEffectors` module declaration.

## Design & Implementation
`EnvironmentModels` declares the module, includes density models and gravity models, and re-exports the `getDensity` generic; `AerodynamicEffectors` is the module wrapper for the aerodynamic wrench models that lives under environment for include-order reasons.
