# simulation/campaigns/

## Purpose
Running many simulations: Monte Carlo campaigns on threads or processes, adaptive outer routing, and constellation ensembles.

## Design & Implementation
`monte_carlo.jl` runs `f(seed)` over seeds with fail-fast semantics; `adaptive_routing.jl` chooses the route, applies the profile environment and records feedback; `constellation_ensemble.jl` validates and builds per-member configurations for uncoupled ensembles.
