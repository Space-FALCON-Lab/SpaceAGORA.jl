# analysis/verification/telemetry_verification/telemetry_loading.jl

## Purpose
Loads flight telemetry files and derives the series the comparison needs: apsis extrema, frame-converted states, and time derivatives.

## Design & Implementation
`_load_telemetry_curve` and `_load_time_aligned_telemetry` read the CSVs by configured column names, `_require_column` and `_to_float_vector` validate them, `_extract_extrema_series` and `_extract_extrema_from_time_aligned_telemetry` find periapsis and apoapsis events, and the `_j2000_to_planet_fixed_state` family rotates states between frames with SPICE.
