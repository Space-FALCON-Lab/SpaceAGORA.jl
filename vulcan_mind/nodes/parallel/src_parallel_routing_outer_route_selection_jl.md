# parallel/routing/outer_route_selection.jl

## Purpose
Picks the outer execution route for a campaign by bucketing its features into a signature hierarchy and selecting the route with the best confidence-bounded history.

## Design & Implementation
Feature bucketers for sample count, density family, effector cost and mission length; `_outer_route_signature_hierarchy` produces coarse-to-fine signatures; `_best_candidate` and its confidence helpers apply the bandit; `default_outer_route` and `_priority_outer_route_montecarlo` give the cold-start choice; `record_outer_route_feedback!` closes the loop.
