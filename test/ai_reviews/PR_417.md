# Review record: PR 417: GNC source layout

## Scope

Review of the SpaceAGORA `gnc-layout` branch against main (ba70d4b8b, merged in
at 681f18147; the regroup was written on c7db8b6fb). A
source regrouping of `src/models/gnc/`, the layout chosen by the PI: the role
folders stay at the top (`guidance/`, `navigation/`, `control/`, `planning/`),
guidance and control hold their models in the same application folders
(`aerobraking/`, `landing/`, `maneuvers/`, `rpo/`; control also `robot_arm/`,
`attitude/`, `interlinks/`), the three hook modules are `hooks.jl` in their
role folder, the general GNC contract moves to `contracts/`, the RPO planner
contract to `planning/rpo/contract/`, and the core-owned adapters of the optional
EDG and HYPR companions to one `companions/` folder. Moves only. Two commits,
the regroup and the gate scopes it needed (below), a README path fix from the
review (470ad2202), a merge of main (681f18147, without conflicts), and the
rename of `control/constellation/` to `control/interlinks/` decided by the PI,
so the folder names interlinks rather than constellations, which are a different
thing (a constellation is a set of spacecraft and their orbits; an interlink is a
connection between two terminals).
