# AerobrakingPolicy — internals

## Purpose
Stands for the `AerobrakingPolicy` module on workflow sheets: the mission-level layer that selects a guidance strategy per drag pass, holds the per-mission firing plans (Odyssey, Magellan, Venus Express, Titan, Earth) and the pass schedule, and converts a firing-plan angle into a signed manoeuvre delta-v for the guidance code.

## Design & Implementation
Members are the policy types (`AerobrakingPolicy`, `AerobrakingPolicyConfig`, `AbstractAerobrakingPolicySelector`), the selectors (`DefaultAerobrakingPolicySelector`, `DRLPolicyAdapterStub`, `select_strategy`, `strategy_for_pass`), the schedule struct and the manoeuvre plans. On a workflow sheet they collapse to one node; calls from guidance dispatch arrive on `members_in` and calls into guidance helpers leave on `members_out`.
