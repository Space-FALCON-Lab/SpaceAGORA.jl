# Viewer 3D models

Models the viewer can draw in place of a spacecraft's link boxes through
`export_visualization(prefix; models=Dict(id => path), model_scale=..., model_rotation_deg=...)`
or `spaceagora visualize --model=<id>=<file>`. STL, OBJ and glTF/GLB are
supported; a `.gltf` must embed its buffers, and glTF files must not require
Draco, meshopt or KTX2 (the exporter refuses them and says how to convert).

|---|---|---|---|
| `iss_nasa_3d_resources_b.glb` | NASA 3D Resources, "International Space Station (ISS) (B)" (github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources) | US government work, public domain (NASA media usage guidelines) | glTF 2.0, Y-up, 36 meshes, 19 materials, no textures, 3.5 MB. NASA's file is Draco-compressed, which the viewer cannot decode, so this copy was decompressed with `npx @gltf-transform/cli copy` (geometry unchanged). Extents 14 x 45 x 46 model units; `model_scale=2.4` puts the truss near 109 m and `model_rotation_deg=(-90, 0, -90)` lays the truss along body y with the modules along body x. |
| `magellan_nasa_3d_resources.glb` | NASA 3D Resources, "Magellan" | Public domain (NASA) | glTF 2.0, Y-up, 6 meshes, textured, 3.5 MB after Draco decompression (`npx @gltf-transform/cli copy`). Authored in meters: 8.9 x 4.5 x 3.7 with the solar panels along model X, the high-gain antenna along +Y and the panel faces normal to Z. `model_scale=1.0`, `model_rotation_deg=(-180, 0, -90)` puts the long (bus + antenna) axis along the flow with the bus and antenna leading (model +Y to body +x) and the wing span along body y (`(0, 0, 90)` flies it antenna aft); the modeled wings are then edge-on to the flow (their faces normal to body z), the cruise configuration, not the broadside aerobraking one. |
| `mars_odyssey_nasa_3d_resources.glb` | NASA 3D Resources, "Mars Odyssey" | Public domain (NASA) | glTF 2.0, Y-up, 11 meshes, textured (WebP with PNG fallback), 6.0 MB after Draco decompression (`npx @gltf-transform/cli copy`). Authored in meters: 5.7 x 3.4 x 8.2 with the solar array along model X on the +Y side of the bus, its face normal to Z (canted 11° about X), and the 6 m gamma-ray spectrometer boom along +Z. `model_scale=1.0`, `model_rotation_deg=(0, -90, 0)` puts the array broadside to the flow (model Z to body -x) with the boom trailing and the array on body +y, the aerobraking attitude; the boom and the high-gain antenna are modeled deployed, which they were not during aerobraking. |
| `cassini_huygens_nasa_3d_resources_a.glb` | NASA 3D Resources, "Cassini-Huygens (A)" | Public domain (NASA) | glTF 2.0, Y-up, 11 meshes, textured, 2.5 MB after Draco decompression. Meters: the 4 m high-gain antenna along +Y, the magnetometer boom along -Z, the 10 m RPWS antennas give the 18 x 11.7 x 13.1 extents. Huygens attached (use for the TA flyby). `model_scale=1.0`, `model_rotation_deg=(-90, -180, 90)` puts the long axis along the flow with the antenna forward (model +Y to body +x) and the magnetometer boom along body +y. |
| `cassini_nasa_3d_resources_a_without_huygens.glb` | NASA 3D Resources, "Cassini-Huygens (A) (without Hyugens)" [sic] | Public domain (NASA) | Same model without the probe, for flybys after the 2004-12-25 release (T5 onward). Same scale and rotation. |
| `apollo_lunar_module_nasa_3d_resources.glb` | NASA 3D Resources, "Apollo Lunar Module" | Public domain (NASA) | glTF 2.0, Y-up, 157 meshes, textured, 2.7 MB after Draco decompression. Legs down (-Y), ascent stage up (+Y), forward hatch and ladder facing +Z; 6.4 x 5.0 x 6.4 model units, so `model_scale=1.45` gives the 4.2 m descent stage and a 7.3 m overall height. `model_rotation_deg=(-180, 90, 90)` puts the legs toward nadir (body +z) and the hatch forward (body x). |
