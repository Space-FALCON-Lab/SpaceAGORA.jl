# simulation/callbacks/registry.jl

## Purpose
Process-wide runtime statistics for the GRAM caches and the indirection that lets callbacks reach the engine module without a circular include.

## Design & Implementation
`GramRuntimeStats` counts calls, hits, misses and interpolation errors; `_gram_runtime_stats_update!`, `_reset!` and `_snapshot` manage it under a lock; `_gram_runtime_stats_enabled` gates collection; `_simulation_engine_module` resolves the engine lazily; `callback_verbose` gates logging.
