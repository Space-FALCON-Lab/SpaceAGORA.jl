# simulation/engine/setup.jl · _typed*

## Purpose
Output-policy toggles for the typed results path: whether normalisation warnings are emitted, whether the transition-period normalise flag is honoured, whether the results bundle is written, and whether checkpointing is active.

## Design & Implementation
`_typed_normalize_warning_enabled`, `_typed_allow_transition_normalize` and `_typed_save_bundle_enabled` read `SPACEAGORA_WARN_NORMALIZE`, `SPACEAGORA_ALLOW_TYPED_NORMALIZE` and `SPACEAGORA_SAVE_BUNDLE` through the engine's override-aware getter; `_typed_checkpoint_enabled` derives from the configuration's `checkpoint_enabled` or `resume_from_checkpoint` settings rather than the environment.
