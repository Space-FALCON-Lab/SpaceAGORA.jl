separate physics partitions. Reviewed implementation commit:
`d832097e3379f4a7ffe99a896694c40529b58826`.

## Findings

Independent review verified the five helper bodies match both original copies
under the required per-spacecraft continue-to-return transformation. Shared
sampling, scheduling, synchronization and interlinks remain outside the helpers.
All focused regressions passed. An initial allocation difference was investigated
with full allocation profiles, normal callback-configuration snapshots and a
separate quiet paired run. The reviewer verified all 93 final paired batches
have matching allocation minima and maxima, and found no remaining adoption
blocker. No source changes were needed during the final measurement follow-up.

## P1 Assessment

No P1 findings. Physics variants remain separate, inactive-state handling and
namespace/public interfaces are unchanged, and no release tag is edited.

## Tests Added/Updated

The new four-thread parity suite passed 88 assertions. Twenty before/after
partition/orientation/route combinations produced exact derivative equality.
Existing wheel, split-solver, external-propagation, articulated and compliant
attachment suites passed, including their allocation and checkpoint cases.
Two 100-call allocation profiles each recorded 170,685 allocations and 4,743,133
bytes. Targeted isolated measurements used 31 alternating pairs for each of
threaded full, slow and fast-control routes; every batch measured 100 allocation
samples and 20,000 timed calls. All 93 pairs had matching allocation ranges.
Timing-ratio medians were 0.966, 1.017 and 0.991. Hosted CI is pending.

## Residual Risk

Timing varies, and this is not a general speedup or full campaign performance
claim. The evidence supports unchanged tested derivatives and no consistent
regression in the investigated threaded routes. Full program scientific
provenance and student-trial acceptance are separate from this cleanup.
