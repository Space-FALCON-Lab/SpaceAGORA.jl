# gnc/guidance/aerobraking/common/

## Purpose
The closed-form aerobraking trajectory solution and switching-function machinery shared by both guidance strategies.

## Design & Implementation
`closed_form` and `closed_form_calculation` integrate the analytic pass model, `lambdas` computes the costate that determines switching, `aoa` turns the switching function into a bang-bang angle profile, and `results` appends a segment into the campaign-wide closed-form record.
