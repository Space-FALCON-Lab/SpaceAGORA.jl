# gnc/guidance/rpo/hypr/pso_path_planning.jl

## Purpose
The swarm loop: seeds particles, evaluates paths in parallel, culls, learns from stagnation, reexplores when infeasible, stops early when converged, and refines the winner.

## Design & Implementation
`rpo_pso_plan_path` orchestrates; closures inside it reset the swarm, evaluate positions, update bests, apply stagnation learning and record iterations; standalone helpers cull the swarm, compute schedule weights, warm-start from RRT, and gate early stopping on feasibility.
