## Scope
Codex left two P2 findings on PR #49 after it merged; this PR addresses both. Both code paths are opt-in and default-off, so no default physics changes.

## Changed Files
- `src/dynamics/coupled/aerodynamic_wrench_models.jl` — (1) `AerodynamicCoefficientfM` and `AerodynamicCoefficientNoBallisticFlight` gain a `per_link_atmosphere::Bool = false` field, and the three ODEParams-aware gates use `_per_link_enabled(model)` (field OR the process-wide switch), scoping the behavior to an effector instance; the process-wide `set_per_link_atmosphere!` is kept for compatibility with a documented caveat. ## Findings
- P2 (addressed, scoping): the process-wide `Ref` leaked the per-link opt-in across every effector, simulation, and thread in the process; an exception before a manual reset silently changed later runs' physics. The per-model field is now the preferred, scoped opt-in; the global remains as a compatibility fallback (`field OR global`), so existing callers (the study drivers) behave identically.
- P2 (addressed, disclosure): per-link wind was silently discarded. The physically complete fix (per-link wind-relative velocity through Mach/q/drag/lift/crosswind) touches the aero hot loop and deserves its own change with bit-exact A/B on the default path; until then the limitation is warned once at the point of use, per the review's "recompute or stop advertising" alternative. No campaign or in-tree scenario combines per-link sampling with wind.
- No P1 findings.
