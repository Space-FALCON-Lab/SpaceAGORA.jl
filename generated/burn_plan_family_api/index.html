Burn-Plan Family API

This page documents the supported public interface available from SpaceAGORA.

Burn-plan family

SpaceAGORA.BurnPlanning.BurnPlanFamily — Type
BurnPlanFamily()

The burn-plan family of the GNC algorithm contract. Its request is a BurnPlanRequest, a snapshot of one spacecraft and of its burn scheduler; its product a PropulsiveBurnPlan, proposed in an AlgorithmResult; its trusted validation validate_burn_plan, which applies the size check the burn scheduler (BaseThrusterModel) applies to its own burns and checks the window; its install target the spacecraft's slot in the scheduler's burn-plan buffer (BurnPlanSlot); its currency rule burn_plan_is_current; and its rejection policy :keep_previous: a refused proposal leaves the installed plan, and the binding records the refusal with the validation's reason.

An AlgorithmGuidance runs any planner of the family (an AbstractBurnPlanner, such as ApoapsisBurnPlanner). The configuration needs one BaseThrusterModel, whose Δv is zero and whose window is unset for that spacecraft, and no thruster guidance model commanding the scheduler's own burns. The scheduler takes up an installed plan at the end of the step it was installed in: it stops the solver at the plan's window ends and fires the engine inside [start, stop), with the plan's direction, thrust and specific impulse. The family takes no settings.

SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.ThrusterModels.BaseThrusterModel — Type
BaseThrusterModel(; thrust, direction, Δv, start_burn_time, stop_burn_time, Isp,
                  actuation=:ideal, engine=1, pointing_frequency_rad_s=0.05, pointing_damping=0.7)

The orbit-maneuver burn scheduler: one entry per spacecraft in each vector. A burn of thrust newtons and specific impulse Isp seconds runs between start_burn_time and stop_burn_time (seconds into the run), prograde when direction is 0 and retrograde when it is π. With a guidance maneuver command, or a nonzero Δv, the scheduler plans the burn itself, symmetric about the next apoapsis, after the spacecraft leaves the atmosphere.

actuation = :ideal (the default) applies the burn as a force along the velocity, whatever the attitude. In both modes the burn switches exactly at the window's ends: the solver stops there and a callback switches the burn between integration steps, so no step straddles an end. actuation = :mounted fires a thruster mounted on the spacecraft instead: the engine-th mounted thruster (mounting order) runs at the burn's thrust during the window, so the force follows the vehicle's attitude and the propellant follows the engine's own Isp. From the moment a burn is planned until it ends, the scheduler also points the engine along the burn with the spacecraft's mounted reaction wheels, at each control tick: a proportional-derivative law on the angle between the engine's thrust direction and the burn direction, with natural frequency pointing_frequency_rad_s and damping ratio pointing_damping, scaled by the spacecraft's inertia and tracking the turn of the velocity along the orbit. A spacecraft without mounted wheels is not pointed. Give the engine the scheduler's thrust and specific impulse so the planned burn delivers its Δv.

A burn the scheduler cannot plan (it needs more propellant than the vehicle carries, its command is not finite, the thrust or specific impulse is not positive) is not scheduled, and the reason is kept: see burn_plan_rejections. The scheduler also executes a plan that an AlgorithmGuidance of the burn-plan family (BurnPlanFamily) installs for a spacecraft whose own Δv is zero and whose window is unset: it takes the plan's window, direction, thrust and specific impulse.

During a run the scheduler plans in its Δv, direction, start_burn_time and stop_burn_time vectors, which show the run's schedule afterwards. Each run starts from the schedule configured before it: initialize_control! restores every entry the scheduler wrote in the previous run, unless it was changed since, and starts the burn state and the refusal records empty.

SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.CommandTypes.PropulsiveBurnPlan — Type
PropulsiveBurnPlan(; valid=false, delta_v_mps=0.0, direction_rad=0.0, source_orbit=0, start_burn_s=-1.0,
                   stop_burn_s=-1.0, thrust_n=0.0, isp_s=0.0, commanded_impulse_n_s=0.0,
                   propellant_required_kg=0.0)

One constant-thrust burn for the burn scheduler (BaseThrusterModel): a burn of delta_v_mps (m/s), prograde for direction_rad = 0 and retrograde for π, fired at thrust_n (N) with specific impulse isp_s (s) on the window [start_burn_s, stop_burn_s) (s into the run), taking propellant_required_kg (kg) for an impulse of commanded_impulse_n_s (N·s). source_orbit is the orbit number of the command it came from, -1 when none. Only a valid plan is executed. The scheduler plans and executes these; a planner of the burn-plan family (BurnPlanFamily) proposes one, with the propellant, impulse and window duration of burn_plan_sizing.

source
SpaceAGORA.BurnPlanning.AbstractBurnPlanner — Type
AbstractBurnPlanner <: AbstractGNCAlgorithm

An algorithm of the burn-plan family (BurnPlanFamily, inherited). A planner implements propose!(state, planner, request::BurnPlanRequest, rng) and returns an AlgorithmResult whose product, for a :candidate, is the PropulsiveBurnPlan it proposes. ApoapsisBurnPlanner is a subtype.

source
SpaceAGORA.BurnPlanning.BurnPlanRequest — Type
BurnPlanRequest(; request_id, reason, spacecraft_index, time_s, position_m, velocity_mps,
                mass_kg, available_propellant_kg=nothing, thrust_n, isp_s, mu_m3ps2,
                planet_radius_m, entry_interface_altitude_m, installed_window_s=(NaN, NaN))

The burn-plan family's owned request snapshot, in SI units, for the spacecraft at spacecraft_index (its position in the run, from 1) at elapsed time time_s: its inertial position and velocity relative to the planet's centre, its mass, the propellant it can still use (nothing when the vehicle declares no limit), the thrust and specific impulse (s) its burn scheduler configures for it, the planet's gravitational parameter and equatorial radius, the altitude of the atmosphere's entry interface, and the window of the plan installed when the request was made ((NaN, NaN) when none is). Trusted family code builds it (family_request); the planner receives its own copy.

source
SpaceAGORA.BurnPlanning.BurnPlanSlot — Type
BurnPlanSlot(plans, spacecraft_index)

The burn-plan family's install target: the entry for one spacecraft of the burn scheduler's plan buffer plans. AlgorithmGuidance finds it in the run (family_link); the binding's link stays unset.

source
SpaceAGORA.BurnPlanning.ApoapsisBurnPlanner — Type
ApoapsisBurnPlanner(; delta_v_mps, direction_rad=0.0)

A planner of the burn-plan family (AbstractBurnPlanner) with the burn scheduler's own timing rule: a burn of delta_v_mps (m/s, finite and positive), prograde for direction_rad = 0 and retrograde for π, at the request's thrust and specific impulse, centred on the next apoapsis. Like the scheduler, it plans only at or above the atmosphere's entry interface and before apoapsis (or on a near-circular orbit); otherwise its result is :infeasible, with termination :inside_atmosphere, :past_apoapsis or :invalid_orbit. It states the burn's propellant and impulse from the scheduler's size check without the propellant limit, so the family's validation is what refuses a burn the vehicle cannot afford. It draws no random numbers (consumes_rng is false).

source
SpaceAGORA.BurnPlanning.validate_burn_plan — Function
validate_burn_plan(request::BurnPlanRequest, result) -> ValidationResult

The burn-plan family's trusted validation of a proposal result against the lifecycle's own copy of request. A result for another request (a different request_id, such as a stale or cached one) is refused with :request_mismatch, whatever its status. A :candidate for this request is accepted, with reason :accepted and metrics propellant_required_kg, commanded_impulse_n_s and burn_duration_s, when its product is a valid PropulsiveBurnPlan that:

  1. does not replace a burn in progress at the request's time (:burn_in_progress);
  2. passes the burn scheduler's own size check at the request's mass, available propellant, thrust and specific impulse (:invalid_mass, :invalid_delta_v, :invalid_direction, :invalid_thrust, :invalid_isp, :invalid_mass_fraction, :invalid_burn_size, :insufficient_propellant);
  3. uses the request's thrust and specific impulse (:thrust_mismatch, :isp_mismatch) and states the propellant and impulse of that check to a relative 1e-9 (:propellant_mismatch, :impulse_mismatch);
  4. has a window with finite ends and stop > start (:invalid_window) that starts no earlier than the request (:window_before_request) and lasts the check's burn duration, to a relative 1e-9 plus the rounding of its ends (:duration_mismatch).

Any other result is refused with its status as the reason (:infeasible, :unsupported, :failed), and a candidate whose product is not a valid PropulsiveBurnPlan with :invalid_product. Validation installs nothing and draws from no random stream.

source
SpaceAGORA.BurnPlanning.burn_plan_is_current — Function
burn_plan_is_current(request::BurnPlanRequest, plan::PropulsiveBurnPlan, time_s) -> Bool

Whether an installed plan may still be used at time_s: it is valid and its window has not ended. The scheduler executes it until then; afterwards it clears the plan at its next scheduling pass.

source
SpaceAGORA.BurnPlanning.burn_plan_sizing — Function
burn_plan_sizing(mass_kg, delta_v_mps, direction_rad, thrust_n, isp_s; available_propellant_kg=nothing)
    -> NamedTuple

The burn scheduler's size check of a constant-thrust burn of delta_v_mps (m/s) in direction direction_rad for a spacecraft of mass_kg (kg), at thrust_n (N) and specific impulse isp_s (s): (reason, propellant_required_kg, commanded_impulse_n_s, burn_duration_s), in kg, N·s and s, with reason :accepted. Otherwise the reason names the problem (:invalid_mass, :invalid_delta_v, :invalid_direction, :invalid_thrust, :invalid_isp, :invalid_mass_fraction, :invalid_burn_size) and the values are NaN, or it is :insufficient_propellant, with the values, when the burn needs more than available_propellant_kg (nothing: no limit). validate_burn_plan checks a proposed PropulsiveBurnPlan against it, so a planner states these values and a window of this duration.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.ControlHooks.burn_plan_rejections — Function
burn_plan_rejections(model::BaseThrusterModel, spacecraft_index) -> Vector{NamedTuple}

The burns the scheduler refused to plan for the spacecraft at spacecraft_index (its position in the run, from 1) in the run, oldest first, as copies. Each record has time_s (elapsed s), the rejection reason, and the commanded delta_v_mps, direction_rad and source_orbit. The reasons are those of the burn-plan family's validation (validate_burn_plan): :invalid_mass, :invalid_delta_v, :invalid_direction, :invalid_thrust, :invalid_isp, :invalid_mass_fraction, :invalid_burn_size and :insufficient_propellant. A command of exactly zero Δv asks for no burn and leaves no record. A refused command leaves any burn already planned in place. The records start empty at each run's start; after run_simulation with the default isolate_state=true, read them from the configuration that ran (solution.prob.p.args).
