# gnc/guidance/rpo/hypr_planning/

## Purpose
Replanning during an approach: detects drift from the reference, activates replanning spheres, and rebuilds the plan from the current state.

## Design & Implementation
`RPOReplanningConfig` and `RPOReplanningSphere`; `rpo_reference_tracking_error` and `rpo_replanning_decision` decide when to replan; `rpo_geometry_with_replanning_spheres` adds temporary obstacles; `rpo_plan_from_path`, `rpo_remaining_reference_path` and `rpo_retime_existing_plan` rebuild the plan.
