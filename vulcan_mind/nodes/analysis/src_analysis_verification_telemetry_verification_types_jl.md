# analysis/verification/telemetry_verification/types.jl

## Purpose
The configuration and result types of telemetry verification: scenario configs, atmosphere truth, calibration, requests, results and the study config.

## Design & Implementation
`AbstractScenarioConfig` with `OrbitEventsScenarioConfig` and `TimeAlignedScenarioConfig` subtypes carry every parsed manifest field; `AtmosphereTruthConfig`, `CalibrationConfig` and `SpacecraftConfig` are their sub-tables; `VerificationRequest`, `StudyConfig` and `VerificationResult` frame a run. `_parse_scenario_list` splits a comma-separated selection.
