# AI Review — PR #39

## Scope
- Reviewed the element-frame root-cause fix for the remaining telemetry accuracy regression: the VEX/Odyssey scenario elements in `test/telemetry_benchmark_manifest.toml` are body-mean-equator flight-dynamics products (ESA Damiani et al. ISSFD 2015 for VEX; the mission aerobraking NAV solution for Odyssey) that had been consumed as J2000 elements since the propagation frame switched to J2000 (d354addc) — silently rotating the orbit plane and moving periapsis latitude and local solar time for any planet whose pole is not the J2000 pole, while leaving the radius-based periapsis-altitude channel accurate.
- Review method: the frame diagnosis was established adversarially before implementation — the element→state path was re-traced independently, the manifest elements were matched against the mission SPICE kernels in both candidate frames (they match the body-equator reading to small fractions of a degree and disagree with the J2000 reading by many degrees), and native GRAM density was sampled at both geometries to measure the drag-error mechanism. Quantitative results are recorded in the lab notes (lab members only): Space-FALCON-Lab/SpaceAGORA-notes, notes/2026-07-telemetry-regression-bugfix-study.md.
- Implementation verified by: kernel-anchored regression tests (transform of manifest elements must land on kernel-derived J2000 osculating elements for both planets), the full integration suite (zero failures), the telemetry quick profile (all rows pass), and the full-profile before/after study on the native GRAM rig.

## Findings
- P1 (fixed): the frame misinterpretation is the verified root cause of the Odyssey impact-termination (the sim flew a mid-inclination dayside-periapsis orbit instead of the real near-polar nightside one, with materially higher climatology density on every pass) and of the VEX early-campaign over-drag; Earth scenarios were structurally immune, matching the observed failure pattern.
- P2 (fixed): Odyssey periapsis-altitude data error in the manifest (below the truth curve's own minimum); airspeed sign flip replicated across six files, biasing drag and heating wherever winds are enabled.
- P3 (noted, not in this PR): three hand-duplicated copies of the EI/2000-km density-gate policy (ext GRAM, ext surrogate, isolated-pool); planetodetic latitude passed to GRAM flagged planetocentric (matters for oblate planets); `planet.omega` about the J2000 z-axis rather than the body pole (small velocity bias); locally modified VEX kernels in the GRAMSuite submodule checkout (reproducibility).
- Tolerances loosened after the frame switch were intentionally left untouched in this PR at the PI's direction.

## P1 Assessment
- The element-frame misinterpretation was the P1 candidate; it is fixed and its mechanism was verified against mission kernels and native GRAM before implementation.
- Unresolved P1: No
