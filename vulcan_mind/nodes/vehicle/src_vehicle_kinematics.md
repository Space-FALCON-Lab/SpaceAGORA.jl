# vehicle/kinematics/

## Purpose
Link-frame rotations: transforming vectors between a link's body frame and inertial, and re-orienting a link about an axis.

## Design & Implementation
`rotate_to_inertial` and `rotate_to_body` build the rotation from a link's quaternion and the root attitude; `rotate_link` sets a link's orientation from an axis and angle, used by the panel effectors.
