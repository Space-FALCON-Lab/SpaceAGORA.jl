# dynamics/multibody_cloth/

## Purpose
Compliant multibody dynamics for cloth-like panels and the robot arm that manipulates them: penalty-joint bodies, integrators, and the coupling into the spacecraft right-hand side.

## Design & Implementation
`cloth_multibody.jl` is the generic compliant model — bodies, spring-damper joints, actuators, RK4 and implicit-midpoint steppers; `cloth_robot_arm_dynamics.jl` builds an arm from the `Robotics` kinematic model, drives it toward a `RobotArmPlan`, and assigns the coupled derivatives into the satellite state.
