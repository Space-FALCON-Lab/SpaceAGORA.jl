# gnc/guidance/rpo/hypr/pso_refinement.jl

## Purpose
Post-swarm refinement of the winning path: shortcut and refit, pull Bezier handles toward chords, and lower the degree, each accepted only if the objective improves.

## Design & Implementation
Segment safety checks with adaptive sampling, the least-squares fixed-endpoint Bezier fit with Bernstein basis, the three refinement strategies, and `rpo_try_accept_refinement` as the single acceptance gate with `rpo_refinement_better` as its test.
