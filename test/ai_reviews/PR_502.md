What it does:
- `VisualizationScene` gains `link_schedule`, the schedule's feather file by
  name. The sidecar of a run with interlinks whose results wrote the schedule
  names it under `results.link_schedule`.
- The exported viewer payload gains an optional `interlinks` block
  (`interlink_payload`): the links and their active and contact windows. The
  export checks the schedule against the results manifest's hash, takes the
  block's bytes out of `data_budget_mb`, and leaves it out with
  `interlinks=false`.
- The scene schema and `viewer_schema` stay 1. The SpaceAGORAVisualizer
  companion PR draws the block; the pin update (I-6d) brings it in.

Rule: scenes and payloads of runs without interlinks are byte-identical to
the base's. In an interlinked run, only the sidecar's entry and the block are
new, and the frames are identical whenever the budget does not bind.

## Findings

No P1 or P2 findings in the author's own review. Points a reviewer should check:

- **F1, fixed: the shared budget was untested.** A mutant that dropped the
  subtraction from `data_budget_mb` survived the first tests. It also survived
  a first test on the gve run's own schedule, whose block is smaller than one
  frame, so the frame count could not change. The test now repeats that
  schedule 4000 times. With the links, the frames equal those of a payload
  without them and a budget smaller by the block, and they are fewer than
  under the whole budget. The mutant is caught.
- **F2, open, for the merge order: C-5a's long layout.** C-5a adds
  `results_layout` to `VisualizationScene`, `scene_dict` and `_scene_from_dict`,
  where this branch adds `link_schedule`. Its long manifest would not list the
  schedule that `_saved_link_schedule` looks up. The second PR to merge keeps
  both fields, both outer constructors and both sidecar entries, and lists the
  schedule in the long manifest (as I-6b's F4).
- **Optional block, schema 1 (D-I6-8).** A renderer without the block ignores
  it, so neither schema moves. The companion validates the block when present.
- **The manifest binding** follows the mission history's: the export refuses a
  schedule whose hash differs from `files.link_schedule_feather`, or that is
  missing when the scene names it. With `interlinks=false` the export goes on
  without it.
- **The budget.** A long schedule under a tight budget leaves fewer frames.
  The export refuses a block that alone exceeds the budget, and names the two
  remedies.
- **Low: the scene names a file that is there.** `_scene_link_schedule` checks
  only that the file exists. An interlinked run that writes no bundle could
  find another run's schedule in the folder. The export's hash check refuses
  it unless that run's manifest is also there, which is the binding the
  results feather already has.

## P1 Assessment

No P1 findings. Runs without interlinks have byte-identical scenes and
payloads (Tests). An interlinked run's frames are unchanged when the budget
does not bind. The block is additive and optional under schema 1, and the
export refuses a schedule its manifest does not vouch for.
