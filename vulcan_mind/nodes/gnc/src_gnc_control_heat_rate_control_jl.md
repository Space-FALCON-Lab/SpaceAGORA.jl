# gnc/control/heat_rate_control.jl

## Purpose
The instantaneous heat-rate constraint: the largest angle of attack whose free-molecular heat rate stays under the limit.

## Design & Implementation
`_energy_depletion_heat_rate_calc` is the Maxwellian flat-plate heat-rate formula, `_energy_depletion_heatrate_root_alpha` solves it for alpha by Newton with bisection fallback, and `_edg_heat_rate_alpha` and `_edg_maxwellian_heat_rate` adapt them to the controller's environment sample.
