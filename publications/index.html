Publications

Use this page to find papers that are directly connected to SpaceAGORA.jl. The project citation metadata remains in the repository CITATION.cff file; the papers below document SpaceAGORA-based analyses, verification, and guidance studies.

Direct SpaceAGORA papers

  • Evan Yu, Nihal Simha, and Giusy Falcone, "A Comparative Analysis of Aerobraking Dynamics Across Celestial Bodies Using SpaceAGORA," AIAA SCITECH 2026 Forum, Orlando, FL, 2026. DOI: 10.2514/6.2026-1692.

    This paper uses SpaceAGORA.jl for comparative aerobraking perturbation analysis across Mars, Venus, Earth, and Titan, including atmosphere, gravity, third-body, and solar-radiation-pressure effects.

  • Evan Yu, James Cutler, and Giusy Falcone, "Verification of SpaceAGORA.jl 6-DoF Dynamics Using University of Michigan SmallSats Telemetry," 2026 IEEE Aerospace Conference, Big Sky, MT, 2026, pp. 1-14. DOI: 10.1109/AERO66936.2026.11520056.

    This paper validates SpaceAGORA.jl translational and attitude dynamics against University of Michigan SmallSat telemetry and Basilisk comparisons.

Closely connected implementation papers

  • Jakob Gorisek-Gazze and Giusy Falcone, "HyPR: Safety- and Fuel-Aware Motion Planning and Replanning for Autonomous SmallSat Servicing," accepted to the IEEE International Conference on Space Robotics (iSpaRo), 2026. Lab publication record.

    This study develops a planner for spacecraft motion near obstacles, combining an RRT-Connect warm start, particle-swarm refinement of Bézier paths, retiming, and LQ-MPC tracking. It reports static and replanning comparisons. The HYPR guide explains the method's connection to SpaceAGORA and distinguishes its reported results from the current examples. The citation records acceptance; a DOI and proceedings pages are not yet established by the cited record.

  • Nihal Simha, Evan Yu, and Giusy Falcone, "Generalized Energy Depletion Guidance for Aerobraking at Mars, Venus, Earth, and Titan," AIAA SCITECH 2026 Forum, Orlando, FL, 2026. DOI: 10.2514/6.2026-1378.

    This paper implements and exercises the generalized Energy Depletion Guidance framework in SpaceAGORA.jl for drag passages at Mars, Venus, Earth, and Titan. The EDG guide describes the current configuration, physical quantities and limits of that implementation.

Foundational guidance paper

  • Giusy Falcone and Zachary R. Putnam, "Energy Depletion Guidance for Aerobraking Atmospheric Passes," Journal of Guidance, Control, and Dynamics, Vol. 45, No. 4, 2022, pp. 651–668. DOI: 10.2514/1.G006171.

    This paper develops the original EDG formulation for atmospheric passes with articulating panels. It provides the foundation for the generalized guidance study above; it should not be cited as validation of every later software option or adapter.
