## Findings

Previously, density workers reconstructed from a planet name and reused by planet, dropping the coordinator's nondefault settings, epoch and paths. They now use owned construction recipes and recheck them under a worker lock held through each batch. Failed construction/warm-up clears the cache; failed batches leave coordinator outputs untouched for local fallback. The coordinator remembers a recipe whose worker setup or batch failed and declines it at once on later batches instead of rebuilding native models on every worker at every RHS evaluation; `clear_density_service_failures!` or a pool restart makes the service try again, and a changed recipe is tried once more. Legacy planet-only APIs remain available. Raw-core wrappers with unknown provenance use local evaluation.

The first coverage run (job `105631685037`) failed when a density worker could not load SpaceAGORA: `StaticArraysChainRulesCoreExt` failed to precompile with a missing ChainRulesCore message. Locally, Distributed's inheritance of a prepended GRAMSuite environment reproduced mixed package resolution: StaticArrays and Serialization were identified through the vendored manifest while Distributed was identified through the application project. The exact extension-precompile exception was **not reproduced** on local Julia 1.12.3; CI used 1.12.7. The mixed stack is a suspected cause, not a fully established causal chain. The repair starts workers with their pool project first and preserves other coordinator environments as fallbacks. Current-head Linux coverage must confirm the repair. Required native reporting correctly rejected the incomplete child.

Native completion is emitted only after construction and density-service probes both return successfully, then forwarded by their parent. The coverage job requires that completion and successful child exit. Optional developer runs can explicitly skip unavailable native setup; required mode rejects skips, missing/invalid markers and child failures.
