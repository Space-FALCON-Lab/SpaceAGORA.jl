# parallel/policy/

## Purpose
The inner threading policy: decides per call site whether a loop runs threaded and with how many workers, learns from measured outcomes, and persists what it learned across runs.

## Design & Implementation
`env_config.jl` parses the `SPACEAGORA_*` knobs; `types.jl` defines contexts, telemetry and controller state; `context.jl` manages policy scopes and the persistent worker pools; `thread_execution.jl` is the family of `threaded_*` primitives call sites use; `policy_telemetry.jl` records decisions; `persistent_hints.jl` is the UCB bandit over allotments with an on-disk history.
