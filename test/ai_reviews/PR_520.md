## Findings

The first implementation used different rays for force and mounted torque.
A regression reproduced nonzero net angular momentum for offset endpoints.
The repair transforms mount offsets into inertial axes, uses one terminal ray
and applies each lever-arm torque in body axes. Six assertions failed before
the repair; the complete corrected laser suite then passed.

Independent feature review found checkpoint identity omitted the terminal mount.
The repair records only geometry-reading endpoints and their mounting identity;
changed geometry is refused before row recovery or stream truncation. Hinge state
continues to belong to the callback checkpoint owner. Five changed-mount refusal
assertions failed before the repair and passed after it. Older geometry-aware
checkpoints without mounting identity are refused; center-based payloads retain
their previous identity behavior.

Independent post-merge review found one further P2: automatic registration could
select a virtual terminal for a link requiring physical hardware. That follow-up
also covers Shannon-budget data links. Independent follow-up review found that
automatic selection also ignored transmitter/receiver roles for directed power
links. Both repairs are covered by the automatic-registration follow-up; the
original feature's hosted success did not establish these untested paths. The public-record P2 is corrected here by
removing stale scope, inaccessible evidence paths and unrelated inherited files.
