## Findings

- Equation 9 of the current manuscript bounds heat over one atmospheric passage. The prior 34.564736 and 31.390909 J/cm² two-pass cumulative totals do not establish a per-passage violation. Retained boundary-sample analysis gives approximately 26.3918 and 8.1702 J/cm² for the original two passages. The earlier contrary finding is withdrawn; its numeric outputs remain historical evidence.
- The added unconditional cumulative-heat override changed commands without paper or original-code support. It is removed. Passage accounting now subtracts entry heat separately for each panel and freezes at exit; cumulative physical state is never reset. This corrects the input quantity without replacing the flight policy.
- All 192 command cases match the exact pre-guard d45ee26fa controller; the published guard differs in 28 cases. The existing switch-recomputation and targeting-solve definitions, legacy guidance files, heat equations, security threshold and cadence are unchanged by this correction.
- The original minimum-angle remaining prediction and its scheduling/98-percent gates differ from the manuscript VI.C planned-command, every-update description. Targeting cadence, fallback/heat-feasible bracket interpretation, and reduced sensitivity/truth-model differences also require reconciliation. This repair does not resolve them by inventing or replacing policy.
- Low-orbit minimum-angle structural exceedance remains a bounded modeled finding. The structural proxy is distinct from raw dynamic pressure. It is not a global infeasibility certificate or automatic simulator stop.
- Checkpoint files on this branch omit EDG guidance and passage history. Nonzero-time EDG resume now fails explicitly; full checkpoint support is not claimed.

- The F1 repair records heat on the same geodetic surface used by EDG's existing passage gate and predictor. Spherical phase tolerances, atmosphere mask and scheduling remain owned by the original unchanged handlers. Pairing can add saved event states at distinct geodetic roots; identical solver sampling is not claimed.
- The public API inventory now includes five existing exported EDG types and their root-module documentation. No dependency pins change.

The October 12 change treats the existing solver's finite `(t, t)` result as a
completed no-switch decision for the current passage. Applied control and integrated
prediction both require a strictly positive interval before commanding its low-drag
segment. Genuine intervals retain inclusive endpoints. Exit/reentry reset, the
configured security logic, switching equations, physical models and 3 Hz command
hold are preserved. Historical altitude, aerodynamic and J2 substitutions used to
isolate numerical differences are diagnostic only and are excluded from this patch.

The atmospheric predictor now evaluates the command the configured callback can
apply. Off-tick starts retain the current angle until the next tick; off-tick final
points retain the held command. RK4 steps are at most one second independently of
the control period. An outward state exactly at the geodetic interface is a
zero-duration exit; an inward state continues. Missing outbound exit is an explicit
failure. The selected switch minimizes absolute exit-energy residual among the
evaluated adjacent executable ticks, with finite residuals exposed in its certificate.
This is not an exact continuous root or a reachability proof.

The target-energy construction, vacuum predictor functions, atmosphere, forces,
initial orbit, constraints, one-switch family, energy objective and cached finite
switch schedule are unchanged from the parent. A proposed vacuum refinement was
excluded to keep scope and attribution separate. No safety margin, controller
frequency change or new protection policy is introduced.
