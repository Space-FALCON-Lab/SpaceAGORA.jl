# PR review: remove the legacy E-EDG/T-EDG aerobraking strategy routes

## Scope

Review of PR 352, the SpaceAGORA branch `legacy-aerobraking-strategy-removal` (`ce440860c`, plus the follow-up commits `76beac99c`,
`8c7caa6d4`, `98e4123cb` and `160e18a11`) against main (`bc823fecc`). The PR deletes the older E-EDG and
T-EDG aerobraking strategy routes: 19 source files under `src/` (the strategy
solvers, the legacy tracking executor and controllers, the bridge helpers and
the mission strategy selector), the tests and gates that only exercised them,
and the `NLsolve` dependency that only they loaded. It records the removal in
the contracts and contributor docs. The PI decided the deletion on 2026-10-15
after a reachability check found no live caller.

Authorship disclosure: this record was drafted by the author of the PR. It
does not replace an independent review; the PI or the second reviewer accepts
it.
