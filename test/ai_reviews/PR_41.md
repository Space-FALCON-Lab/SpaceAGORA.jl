# AI Review — PR #41

## Scope
- Reviewed the Odyssey campaign-replay and comparison-bookkeeping fixes that close out the telemetry-scenario forensics: the maneuver-numbering origin mismatch (double-applied walk-in burns), the sim-axis step/anchor error, post-termination clamp scoring, the full-mission-mean calibration bias distorting reported apsides, and the ambiguous Terminated retcode.
- Review method: every mechanism was established empirically before implementation — the maneuver numbering by aligning NAV-kernel periapsis-altitude jumps against the burn list under both origin hypotheses (sign agreement on all detectable burns under campaign numbering; near-zero matches under epoch numbering), the axis error by event-count arithmetic against the kernel periapsis list, and the bias distortion by arithmetic identity between the full-profile and instrumented quick-profile outputs. Fixes verified by probe tests, a quick-profile rig smoke (pre-epoch burns dropped and logged, clean orbit_count terminations, masking active only for the opted-in scenario), the full integration suite, and a full-profile validation run. Quantitative record in the lab notes (lab members only): Space-FALCON-Lab/SpaceAGORA-notes, notes/2026-07-telemetry-regression-bugfix-study.md.

## Findings
- P1 (fixed): the five pre-epoch walk-in burns were replayed on top of an initial condition that already contained their effect, driving periapsis ~25 km below the flown corridor into order-of-magnitude higher density — the verified cause of the scenario's early trajectory collapse.
- P2 (fixed): the axis stretch inflated apparent coverage (~2.5x for the Odyssey apoapsis channel) and biased the decay-rate diagnostic by the same factor; post-termination clamp scoring owned the scenario's worst-point statistics; the full-mission-mean bias (clamp-saturated at the cap) shifted accurately extracted apsides by up to hundreds of km in reports.
- P3 (noted): the manifest's delta-v list is itself a smoothed reconstruction of the real ABM sequence (kernel shows early events larger than the impulsive predictions and one large unlisted event); the offset has a +/-1-orbit alignment ambiguity (19 vs 20) — both are scenario-data questions, not code, and the validation run reads on them.

## P1 Assessment
- The double-applied walk-in burns were the P1 candidate; fixed via the offset filter and verified by the rig smoke and validation run.
- Unresolved P1: No

## Tests Added/Updated
- Maneuver offset: campaign→epoch conversion, pre-epoch burn dropping, delta-v list kept in sync, negative offset rejected.
- Calibration: early-orbit median recovers a datum offset under late drift; cap saturation yields bias 0 (reported).
- Comparison: masked scoring span, coverage as masked fraction, raw-vs-adjusted column identity, legacy path unchanged when keys absent.

## Residual Risk
- The offset value (19) carries a +/-1 alignment ambiguity from the kernel burn detection; if the validation run shows systematically early/late corridor behavior, 20 is the fallback — a one-line manifest change.
- Coverage semantics changed for opted-in scenarios (masked fraction vs min-count ratio); consumers comparing coverage across runs should note the definition change.
- Reported metrics for opted-in scenarios will shift relative to historical runs because the axis, masking, and bias semantics changed together — that is the point of the PR, but historical comparisons must use like-for-like bookkeeping.
