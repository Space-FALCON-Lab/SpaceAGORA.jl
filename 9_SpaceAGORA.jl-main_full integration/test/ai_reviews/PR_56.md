# AI Review — PR 56: digital-twin productization 1/2 — `tabulated_time` density source + decay diagnostics

## Scope
First of two PRs productizing the CYGNSS digital-twin machinery (per PI direction: capabilities public, mission data private). Two tightly coupled pieces: (A) a first-class scenario density source that flies the simulator on an externally supplied ρ(t) history, and (B) a public decay-diagnostics utility whose windowed flight-density extraction emits exactly that source's table format. Together they replace the include-world density overrides and scratch estimators the campaign used.

## Findings
- P2 (documented, by design): a secular SMA slope from a finite arc is never physical alone — J2 apsidal precession modulates the osculating harmonics and any fixed-amplitude regression leaks the drift into its slope (+11–12 m/day drag-free, measured; issue #52). The module header and every docstring state the zero-reference requirement; `zero_referenced_decay` is the only sanctioned path to a physical number. This lands the documentation and utility follow-ups of #52.
- P3 (accepted): the ρ(t) table timebase is scenario elapsed seconds; a mismatched epoch shifts the history rather than failing loudly. Documented at the loader and model; an epoch-stamp column is a possible follow-up.
- P3 (accepted): held-constant ends (no extrapolation) and log-linear interpolation are the conservative choices for replay tables covering the scored arc; node count and span are logged at load.
- P3 (accepted): `flight_density_table` absorbs the supplied effective CdA — a CdA scale error rescales ρ uniformly without changing its shape (exactly the campaign's calibratable-scale convention).
- No P1 findings.
