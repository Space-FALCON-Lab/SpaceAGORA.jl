# dynamics/coupled/perturbations.jl · _spice*

## Purpose
SPICE naming and locking helpers for the perturbation effectors.

## Design & Implementation
`_spice_query_name` canonicalises a body name and appends `_barycenter` for outer planets, `_spice_lock` locates the process-wide lock by walking the module tree, and `_spice_rhs_memo_reset_if_stale!` invalidates the single-epoch memo when the epoch or primary changes.
