## Scope

`actuator-contract` (PR #317). The PR completes the actuator-layer plan
(steps 3 to 6) in one commit per step: reaction wheels, torque rods, laser
terminals and hinges as mounted actuator kinds; one shared propellant formula;
the retirement of the pseudoinverse thruster allocator; a tested walkthrough
page; and two fixes from the automated review. The PI asked for steps 3 to 6 in one PR on 2026-10-15.

Authorship disclosure: this record was drafted by the author of the PR. The
plan assigns behaviour PRs of the actuator layer an independent reviewer (the
PI, or the second reviewer when available); this record does not replace that
review.

## Findings

No P1 findings. Two P2 findings from the automated review of the first push,
both fixed in the second push (b3438ba35), each with a test
that fails without its fix:

- P2, fixed: mounted wheels were integrated through the root assembly's
  least-norm split of their summed torque, so with redundant wheels (a
  four-wheel pyramid) uncommanded wheels gained momentum and commanded ones
  did not get minus their level, and the momentum-limit gate read wrongly
  evolved states. Each mounted wheel's rate now reaches the orientation
  right-hand side wheel by wheel through the assembly's `mounted_rates`,
  rebuilt by every evaluation; the pseudoinverse stays only for wheels given
  through the `Link` keywords and for ideal controllers' wheel torques. Test:
  a pyramid with a pure null-space command spins every wheel at minus its
  level while the body stays still; a mixed command matches body and wheels
  and conserves angular momentum.
- P2, fixed: `add_actuator!` accepted a link outside `model.links`, so the
  actuator was silently ignored (and a hinge rotated an unrelated link). Every
  kind now refuses such a link before changing anything. Test: one refusal per
  kind, the stray link unchanged.

Points a reviewer should check:
- Low, by design: a wheel at its momentum limit stops any torque that would
  store more; the gate reads the integrated momentum, so the stored momentum
  can pass the limit by at most one integration step's worth (the test bounds
  the overshoot).
- Low, by design: hinges are applied only for effectors in `:mounted`
  actuation, after their control ticks, and a rate-limited hinge holds its
  angle on its first tick. The energy-depletion controller's panels keep
  their instantaneous actuation.
- Revision from the plan, deliberate: `Magnet` stays the passive dipole the
  torque-rod effector applies; the commanded rod is the new `Magnetorquer`.
  Replacing `Magnet` would have changed what the eddy-damping and torque-rod
  effectors compute.
- Left out, with reasons in the PR description: a mounted burn scheduler, the
  removal of the stored counters and the thruster Jacobian, and rate-limited
  EDG panels.
