## Findings

- The density memo hits only on bitwise-equal model, position, time and wind flag, and is off under any perturbation mode. Bit-identical on 13 cases.
- The new stiffness estimate changes default auto-stiff behavior (not bit-identical); the legacy detector remains behind SPACEAGORA_AUTO_STIFF_DETECTOR=legacy.
- The Jacobian branch showed a larger mass-column error at 500 km than the base path.

## P1 Assessment

No P1 identified. The default solver change in the auto-stiff path is the highest-impact item: measured better against a 1e-12 reference on three physical cases, but genuine stiffness was only exercised by one synthetic case and a Van der Pol test.

## Tests Added/Updated

Three new unit files, registered in test/unit/runtests.jl. Density-memo bit-identity and detector comparisons were run on a remote host. The unit suite and contract gates have not been run on the merged tree.

## Residual Risk

False stiff switches on callbacks that modify state over 11 consecutive steps; Rodas phases that begin with dt above 3.2 s are untested; the mass-column Jacobian accuracy at 500 km is unresolved.
