## Findings

Previously a successful `perf_<case>_<mode>_t<threads>_mc<samples>.csv` was reused
without checking whether the equal-core cap was enabled. Partially resumed runs
could combine capped and uncapped points, and saved rows did not identify the
cap or pinned hardware class.

The controller now derives one launch context, passes its experiment identity
to workers, and validates the entire resume tree before new work begins. The
identity includes the equal-core flag, base core budget, base hardware class
and requested CPU pinning. Performance and parity rows record this identity
along with the worker's actual resolved core budget and hardware class.
Hardware snapshots also retain the resolved values. The effective environment
records literal overrides, keeping them distinct from resolved values.

Both performance and parity completion schemas are checked. Missing or
incompatible provenance raises an error directing the operator to a fresh
output directory. Successful rows in partially completed files are checked as
well, including retained raw CSVs when worker rows have been removed. A capped
predictive row must report its requested rung and pinned class. Other unpinned
rows must report the controller's base budget and class.

PPC and paper aggregates retain the condition and per-arm values. Serial
baselines, parity comparisons and regret calculations partition on the shared
experiment condition; the predictive arm's smaller cap is not incorrectly
required to match its serial baseline's cap. Historical CSVs remain readable
and are labeled `legacy_unrecorded`. They cannot safely resume because older
capped and uncapped files have indistinguishable provenance.

## Tests Added/Updated

`test/unit/parallel/benchmark_budget_condition_tests.jl` exercises launch commands,
the real performance/parity CSV writers with synthetic samples, compatible and
incompatible resumes, partial files, changed caps/classes, legacy files and
condition-separated aggregates. It is registered in the normal unit runner.
The worker-launch regression suite and the mode/profile and workload-coverage
gates pass on Julia 1.12.3 with one thread, `--depwarn=error`, existing compiled
modules/package images and the existing depot: 73 new assertions, 44 existing
worker-launch assertions and 307 workload-gate assertions (424 total), plus the
mode/profile-parity gate. `git diff --check` passes. The initial workload-gate
attempt stopped on a tracked launcher omitted by the sparse checkout; restoring
that exact HEAD blob and executable mode allowed the final run to pass.

No simulation campaign, native GRAM execution, process-worker startup, plotting,
new dependency installation or performance measurement is part of this check.

## P1 Assessment

No P1 identified in this bounded fix review. This record is implementation and
regression evidence, not a replacement for independent publication review or
fresh CI.

## Residual Risk

Legacy resumes intentionally require a new directory. The condition
covers this experiment's resource settings; it does not claim a general resume
identity for every possible benchmark option. Historical measurements keep
their original commits and have not been revalidated.

The separate PR198 baseline-layout fix must preserve these condition keys when
the two branches are integrated.
