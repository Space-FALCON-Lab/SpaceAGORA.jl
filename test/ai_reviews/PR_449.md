# Review record: PR 449: named steps of a periodic control tick, and the callback order in one place

## Scope

Review of the SpaceAGORA `callback-phases` branch against main (6183d00d2, after #444 and #445). The work began on v0.5.0 (41cd1f2ef) and merged main in after #444. This is item C2a of the post-0.5.0 structure and readability cleanup plan ("C2. Make engine sequencing readable in two small PRs").

The change is behavior-preserving:

- the periodic control tick becomes a sequence of named private steps;
- the GNC block of the callback assembly becomes one named phase;
- one maintainer section states the callback order;
- one testset pins the order of callbacks that apply at one time.

No public signature, serialization format, numerical result or callback order changes. No new scheduler is added.

The plan's Preserve list, and where each item stays:

- **Sensors before navigation:** `_gnc_phase_callbacks` keeps sensors, then navigation.
- **Placement of selected and remaining guidance:** guidance before control is EDG targeting (`_guidance_precedes_control`); the rest comes after CMG, both in configuration order.
- **Linked producers run once before their consumers:** `_prepare_linked_producers!` is step 1 of `_periodic_control_tick!`, with the same `_ticks_any` condition.
- **Corrected inactive handling:**
  - the periodic ticks still go through `_gnc_tick!`;
  - #437's `_refresh_burn_keeping_release!`, the burn edge and the mounted burn tick are unchanged;
  - #411's mounted-actuator tick (`_apply_mounted_tick!`) is unchanged.
- **Serial stop registration after possibly threaded ticks:** `_register_control_stops!` runs after `_apply_control_ticks!`.
- **Event-driven and extra callback placement:**
  - `get_control_callbacks` adds an event-driven model's `gnc_callbacks` at its configuration position;
  - priority, extra and factory callbacks keep their places in `get_callbacks` and `run_simulation`.
- **RPO policy separate from generic events:** no callback change; the order section states where the policy lives.

## Findings

No P1 or P2 findings.

Points a reviewer should check:

- **The policy observation's span.** `elapsed_ns` still runs from the thread decision to after stop registration, as before. The policy's threading decisions see the same observations.
- **Construction order.** `_gnc_phase_callbacks` calls the `get_*` builders in the order the inline block did: sensors, navigation, guidance, control, faults, power, CMG. A builder that allocates state or a `gensym` key does so at the same point.
- **The tick's return value.** The old closure returned the policy observation's result; `_periodic_control_tick!` returns `nothing`. The solver ignores an affect's return value, and the tests that call `affect!.affect!` directly ignore it too.
- **Current behavior stated, not changed.** The order section states as current behavior that:
  - guidance that does not feed the same tick's control ticks after control, faults, power and CMGs;
  - a discrete callback that terminates a solve does not stop later discrete callbacks in that step;
  - the EDG heat observer wraps every callback except the engine's two interlink scheduler callbacks;
  - the observer is first among the assembly's discrete callbacks, with only #444's initialization-only scheduler copy before it.
