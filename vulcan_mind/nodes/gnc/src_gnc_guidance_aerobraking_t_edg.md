# gnc/guidance/aerobraking/t_edg/

## Purpose
Time-based entry-descent guidance: chooses the switch time so the pass hits a target apoapsis, with closed-form, heat-load-limited and numerically integrated targeting variants.

## Design & Implementation
`compute_t_edg_guidance_window!` dispatches to `control_solarpanels_targeting_closed_form`, `_heatload` or `_num_int`; the `func_*` integrands and `shooting_residual!` drive the targeting solve; drag-passage callbacks bracket the integration; `closed_form_targeting` and `_target_planning_impl` assemble the result.
