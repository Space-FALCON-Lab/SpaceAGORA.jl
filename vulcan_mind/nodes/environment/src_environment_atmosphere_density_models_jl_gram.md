# environment/atmosphere/density_models.jl · _gram*

## Purpose
The core-package side of the GRAM integration: lock-scope policy, the function slots the extension fills, and the stubs that raise until GRAMSuite loads.

## Design & Implementation
`_gram_lock_scope` reads the global-versus-per-model lock policy, `_gram_default_surrogate_file` and `_gram_not_loaded_error` are the slot accessor and the uniform failure, and `_gram_core_density_state` and `_gram_point_density` are the evaluation stubs the extension overrides.
