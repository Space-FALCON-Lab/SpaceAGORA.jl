## Findings

Previously, density workers reconstructed from a planet name and reused by planet, dropping the coordinator's nondefault settings, epoch and paths. They now use owned construction recipes and recheck them under a worker lock held through each batch. Failed construction/warm-up clears the cache; failed batches leave coordinator outputs untouched for local fallback. Legacy planet-only APIs remain available. Raw-core wrappers with unknown provenance use local evaluation.

Native completion is emitted only after construction and density-service probes both return successfully, then forwarded by their parent. The coverage job requires that completion and successful child exit. Optional developer runs can explicitly skip unavailable native setup; required mode rejects skips, missing/invalid markers and child failures.
