# gnc/control/targeting_control.jl

## Purpose
The energy-depletion control effector: selects the guidance mode, solves the targeting switch time by certified prediction, applies heat-rate, structural and heat-load constraints, and articulates the solar panels.

## Design & Implementation
`AerobrakingEnergyDepletionControlModel` and `SolarPanelAngleOfAttackControlModel`; the environment sampler; the RK4 pass predictors for targeting and max-energy profiles; candidate certification and the energy and apoapsis switch solvers; `_edg_command_alpha!` applying constraints; `_apply_solar_panel_aoa!` rotating the links.
