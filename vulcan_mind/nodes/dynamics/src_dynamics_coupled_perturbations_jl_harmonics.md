# dynamics/coupled/perturbations.jl · _harmonics*

## Purpose
The spherical-harmonics evaluation machinery: per-satellite scratch workspaces, the batched kernel and its worker pool, the planet-frame rotation cache, and the model memo key.

## Design & Implementation
`_harmonics_workspace_for_sat!` and `_harmonics_calcforcetorque_with_lpi` serve the single-satellite Pines path; `_harmonics_flat_batch_kernel!` with `_get_harmonics_batch_pool` serve the flat constellation path; `_harmonics_lpi_at!` shares one rotation per RHS call; `_harmonics_model_cache_key` memoises constructed models.
