Actuators API

This page documents the supported public interface available from SpaceAGORA.

Actuators

SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.AbstractTypes.AbstractActuator — Type
AbstractActuator

Stable extension interface for a piece of hardware mounted on a spacecraft link that acts on the vehicle when commanded: a thruster, and the kinds added after it. A kind implements actuator_wrench and actuator_limits; the other actuator methods have defaults. Mount it with add_actuator!.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.Thruster — Type
Thruster(; max_thrust, location, direction, Isp, ...)

One jet mounted on a spacecraft link. direction is the direction of the thrust it puts on the vehicle (not the direction of its exhaust), in the link frame; location is the nozzle position in the link frame, relative to the link's centre of mass, metres; max_thrust is its rating in newtons and Isp its specific impulse in seconds. The remaining fields describe its firing dynamics and hold its state during a run.

A Thruster is an AbstractActuator whose level is a thrust in newtons, from zero to max_thrust. Mount it with add_actuator!.

With firing_dynamics=true, a mounted thruster does not deliver its command at once: at each control tick of the effector commanding it, the commanded thrust becomes an on-time within the control period, subject to min_firing_time and the Schmitt trigger (level_on, level_off), and the thrust ramps with cutoff_frequency; the vehicle feels the resulting average thrust until the next tick. Off by default, the thruster acts at its saturated command.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.ReactionWheel — Type
ReactionWheel(; spin_axis, max_torque_nm=Inf, max_momentum_nms=Inf,
              spin_inertia_kgm2=NaN, base_power_w=0.0, motor_efficiency=1.0, regenerative_efficiency=0.0)

One reaction wheel, mounted on the spacecraft's root link with add_actuator!. Its level is the torque it puts on the vehicle about its spin axis, in newton-metres; the wheel's own angular momentum along that axis changes at minus the same rate. spin_axis is in the root-link (body) frame. max_torque_nm bounds the level and max_momentum_nms stops a torque that would store more momentum; both default to unlimited.

Power (see wheel_power): the wheel draws base_power_w (electronics, bearing losses) plus the mechanical power its motor puts into the wheel, divided by motor_efficiency. The mechanical power is the motor torque on the wheel (minus the level) times the wheel speed, its stored momentum over spin_inertia_kgm2; without a spin inertia (the default) only the base power counts. While the motor slows the wheel, regenerative_efficiency of the mechanical power returns to the bus (zero by default: nothing returns, nothing extra is drawn).

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.Magnetorquer — Type
Magnetorquer(; axis, max_dipole_am2=Inf, power_at_max_dipole_w=0.0)

One magnetic torque rod mounted on a spacecraft link with add_actuator!. Its level is the dipole it is commanded to, in ampere square metres, along axis (in the link frame); limits ±max_dipole_am2, unlimited by default. The torque on the vehicle is the dipole crossed with the local magnetic field, which the engine takes from the run's MagneticTorqueRodModel field source (a tilted dipole or IGRF, Earth only), or the dipole when the run has none.

Power: a rod's dipole is proportional to its current, so its resistive power grows with the square of the dipole, power_at_max_dipole_w * (level / max_dipole_am2)^2. A nonzero power_at_max_dipole_w needs a finite max_dipole_am2.

Magnet above is a permanent dipole, not an actuator: the MagneticTorqueRodModel effector applies it whether or not anything commands the vehicle.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.LaserTerminal — Type
LaserTerminal(; boresight, max_power_w, location=zero, magnification=1.0, range_m=200e3, efficiency=1.0)

One optical terminal mounted on a spacecraft link with add_actuator!. Its level is the optical power it transmits, in watts, from zero to max_power_w, along boresight (in the link frame) from location (link frame, metres). The beam carries momentum away, so the vehicle recoils with a force of level / c opposite the boresight; the terminal draws level / efficiency watts of electrical power.

magnification and range_m describe an inter-satellite link between two terminals of this design: the open-cavity magnification factor and the maximum range of LaserMomentumLink(terminal), the link kind whose force pushes linked spacecraft apart.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.PowerReceiver — Type
PowerReceiver(; normal, radius_m, efficiency, max_power_w=Inf, location=zero)

The receiving end of a power beam, mounted on a spacecraft link with add_actuator!. It is interlink terminal hardware: a PowerBeamLink can end on it, and the power it delivers is generation in its spacecraft's power budget.

  • radius_m: the radius (m) of its circular aperture, which sets how much of a beam it captures.
  • efficiency: the fraction of the captured optical power it turns into electrical power, in (0, 1].
  • max_power_w: its converter's limit (W) on the power it delivers.
  • normal: the aperture's facing, and location, its position, both in the link frame. They are kept for the incidence-angle and pointing models to come (I-7); the delivered power does not use them yet.

It applies no force or torque and draws no power: its level is always 0. It mounts through the generic actuator path, so it takes its place among the actuators of user-defined kinds in the mounting order.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.RadioTerminal — Type
RadioTerminal(; boresight, max_power_w, gain_dbi, efficiency, noise_temperature_k=500.0, max_rate_bps=Inf, location=zero)

A radio mounted on a spacecraft link with add_actuator!. It is interlink terminal hardware: a DataLink can start or end on it. Its level is the radio power it transmits, in watts, from zero to max_power_w.

  • gain_dbi: its antenna gain (dBi), the same for sending and receiving.
  • efficiency: the fraction of its electrical draw it transmits, in (0, 1]. It draws level / efficiency watts while it transmits, and nothing while it receives (a receiver's electronics are a constant PowerLoad).
  • noise_temperature_k: its system noise temperature (K) when it receives.
  • max_rate_bps: the most data it sends or receives, in bits per second.
  • boresight: the antenna's facing, and location, its position, both in the link frame. They are kept for the pointing models to come (I-7); the rate does not use them yet.

It applies no force or torque. The battery's shedding treats it as a laser terminal (tier 1, see terminal_shed_tier). It mounts through the generic actuator path, so it takes its place among the actuators of user-defined kinds in the mounting order.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.PanelHinge — Type
PanelHinge(; axis, min_angle_rad=-Inf, max_angle_rad=Inf, max_rate_rad_s=Inf, angle_rad=0.0)

A hinge that turns one non-root link (a solar panel, a boom) about axis, in the body frame, mounted on that link with add_actuator!. Its level is the commanded hinge angle in radians; the link's attitude is the rotation by the hinge's current angle about axis. The angle stays within [min_angle_rad, max_angle_rad] and moves at most max_rate_rad_s between control ticks; all three default to unlimited. angle_rad holds the current angle (the state the engine advances at each control tick).

The angle a run starts from is kept apart from it: each run with isolate_state=false starts from the angle the previous run started from, unless angle_rad was changed after that run's last tick, and then from the new angle.

A hinge changes the vehicle's geometry, not its wrench: the engine applies its level at the control tick of the effector that commands it, between accepted integration steps, and the aerodynamic and radiation models see the new link attitude from then on.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.ThrusterGimbal — Type
ThrusterGimbal(thruster; axis, min_angle_rad=-Inf, max_angle_rad=Inf, max_rate_rad_s=Inf, angle_rad=0.0)

One axis of a gimbal that turns the thrust of thruster (a Thruster mounted on the same link) about axis, in the link frame. Its level is the commanded gimbal angle in radians: the thrust direction turns by the gimbal's current angle about axis (right-handed), about the thruster's location, which stays fixed. The angle stays within [min_angle_rad, max_angle_rad] and moves at most max_rate_rad_s between control ticks; all three default to unlimited. angle_rad holds the current angle, previous_angle_rad the angle before its latest tick (at last_update_s). As for a PanelHinge, the angles a run starts from are kept apart from them: each run with isolate_state=false starts from the previous run's start, unless they were changed after that run's last tick.

A two-axis gimbal is two ThrusterGimbals on the same thruster; their rotations compose in mounting order (the first mounted turns the direction first). Mount with add_actuator! on the thruster's link.

Like a hinge, a gimbal changes geometry, not the wrench by itself: the engine applies its level at the control tick of the effector that commands it, between accepted integration steps, and the thruster's wrench, propellant use and allocation column follow the turned direction from then on.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.thrust_direction — Function
thrust_direction(thruster::Thruster) -> SVector{3}

The direction of the thruster's thrust in its link frame: its direction, turned by its gimbals' current angles when a ThrusterGimbal is mounted on it. Not normalized.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.ControlMomentGyro — Type
ControlMomentGyro(; rotor_momentum_nms, gimbal_axis, spin_axis, max_gimbal_rate_rad_s=Inf, gimbal_angle_rad=0.0,
                    min_gimbal_angle_rad=-Inf, max_gimbal_angle_rad=Inf)

A single-gimbal control moment gyro on the root link: a rotor spinning at a constant momentum rotor_momentum_nms (N m s) whose spin axis turns about gimbal_axis (body frame). At gimbal angle δ the rotor's momentum is h = rotor_momentum_nms (cos δ s + sin δ (g × s)), with s = spin_axis (perpendicular to g = gimbal_axis) at δ = 0. Its level is the gimbal rate δ̇ in rad/s, within ±max_gimbal_rate_rad_s. The gimbal stops at min_gimbal_angle_rad and max_gimbal_angle_rad: an event puts the angle exactly on a stop when the integration reaches it, and there it does not move further, whatever the command (cmg_angle_saturate); a command back into the range moves it off.

The gimbal angle is integrated with the vehicle's state (cmg_angles, present only when a CMG is mounted), starting from gimbal_angle_rad. The vehicle feels the torque -dh/dt = -δ̇ (g × h) and the rotor's momentum in the gyroscopic term of its attitude equation. Mount with add_actuator! on the root link; cmg_steering turns a desired torque into gimbal rates.

source

cmg_momentum(cmg::ControlMomentGyro, angle) -> SVector{3}

The rotor's momentum of inner's double-gimbal CMG in the body frame at the outer and inner gimbal angles, N m s: h₀ R(g_o, α) R(g_i, β) s.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.cmg_torque_axis — Function
cmg_torque_axis(cmg::ControlMomentGyro, angle) -> SVector{3}

g × h at gimbal angle angle: the CMG puts the torque -δ̇ (g × h) on the vehicle at gimbal rate δ̇ (the column of the CMG's torque Jacobian, negated).

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.cmg_angle_saturate — Function
cmg_angle_saturate(cmg::ControlMomentGyro, rate, angle) -> Float64

The gimbal rate the CMG applies at gimbal angle angle when commanded rate: rate, or zero when the gimbal is at or beyond a stop and rate would drive it further. The attitude equation applies it to the gimbal angle and to the torque alike.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.CMGInnerGimbal — Type
CMGInnerGimbal(cmg; axis, max_gimbal_rate_rad_s=Inf, gimbal_angle_rad=0.0,
               min_gimbal_angle_rad=-Inf, max_gimbal_angle_rad=Inf)

The inner gimbal of a double-gimbal control moment gyro. cmg's gimbal is the outer one, fixed in the body; this one turns the rotor's spin axis about axis, given in the body frame at outer angle 0 (perpendicular to cmg's spin axis, and not along its gimbal axis), which the outer gimbal carries round. At outer angle α and inner angle β the rotor's momentum is h = h₀ R(g_o, α) R(g_i, β) s and the vehicle feels -(α̇ (g_o × h) + β̇ (g_i(α) × h)), with g_i(α) = R(g_o, α) g_i the inner axis as the outer gimbal has turned it.

Its level is the inner gimbal rate β̇ in rad/s, with its own rate limit and stops, as the outer gimbal's. Mount it with add_actuator! on the root link after cmg (one inner gimbal per CMG); the inner angles follow every CMG's outer angle in the cmg_angles state and the inner gimbals follow the CMGs in the mounting order.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.cmg_torque_axes — Function
cmg_torque_axes(inner::CMGInnerGimbal, outer_angle, inner_angle) -> (outer, inner)

The two columns of a double-gimbal CMG's torque Jacobian, negated: g_o × h and g_i(α) × h, so the vehicle feels -(α̇ outer + β̇ inner).

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.ActuatorMounting.cmg_steering — Function
cmg_steering(model::SpacecraftModel, angles, torque_body; λ0=0.01, μ=10.0) -> Vector{Float64}

Gimbal rates for model's mounted control moment gyros, at gimbal angles angles (the CMGs' gimbals in mounting order, then the double-gimbal CMGs' inner gimbals, as the cmg_angles state), that put torque_body (N m, body frame) on the vehicle: the singularity-robust inverse of the gimbals' torque Jacobian (Bedrossian): with A the columns g × h of every gimbal (a double-gimbal CMG's outer g_o × h and inner g_i(α) × h), normalized by the largest rotor momentum, δ̇ = -Aᵀ (A Aᵀ + λ I)⁻¹ τ and λ = λ0 exp(-μ det(A Aᵀ)), which stays bounded near a singular gimbal configuration at the cost of a torque error (λ0 = 0 gives the pseudo-inverse). A CMG at a gimbal stop whose rate would drive it further is left out and the others are solved again, so it is commanded zero; rates beyond a CMG's limit are then scaled down together, keeping the torque's direction.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Actuators.ActuatorContext — Type
ActuatorContext(; link_r_body, link_to_body, mass_kg, q_ib, ω_body, magnetic_field_body, environment=nothing)

What an actuator kind may use when it is evaluated:

  • link_r_body: the mounting link's centre of mass in the spacecraft body frame, relative to the body reference point, metres;
  • link_to_body: the rotation from the link frame to the body frame;
  • mass_kg: the vehicle's current mass;
  • q_ib: the attitude quaternion, inertial to body, scalar last;
  • ω_body: the body angular velocity, rad/s;
  • magnetic_field_body: the local magnetic field in the body frame, T, when a mounted actuator asks for it (see actuator_requirements); NaN otherwise;
  • environment: the sampled environment (an EnvironmentSample: planet frame, atmosphere, Sun) when a mounted actuator asks for any of it; nothing otherwise.

The link pose is the link's configured geometry. The attitude and the rate are the identity and zero when the run does not propagate attitude.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Actuators.actuator_wrench — Function
actuator_wrench(actuator, level, ctx::ActuatorContext) -> (force_body, torque_body)

Required for every actuator kind: the force (N) and the torque (N m) the actuator puts on the vehicle at the commanded level, both in the spacecraft body frame, the torque about the body reference point. level is in the kind's unit (newtons for a thruster) and has already been passed through actuator_saturate.

actuator_wrench(thruster::Thruster, level, ctx) -> (force_body, torque_body)

A thrust of level newtons along the thruster's direction, applied at its location: the force in the body frame and its torque about the body reference point. A zero or non-finite direction gives no wrench.

actuator_wrench(cmg::ControlMomentGyro, level, ctx) -> (force_body, torque_body)

None here: a CMG's torque depends on its gimbal angle, a state; the engine applies -δ̇ (g × h) in the attitude equation.

actuator_wrench(wheel::ReactionWheel, level, ctx) -> (force_body, torque_body)

A torque of level newton-metres on the vehicle about the wheel's spin axis, in the body frame; no force.

actuator_wrench(rod::Magnetorquer, level, ctx) -> (force_body, torque_body)

The torque m x B of a dipole of level A m² along the rod's axis in the body-frame field ctx.magnetic_field_body (T); no force. No field (NaN) gives no torque.

actuator_wrench(terminal::LaserTerminal, level, ctx) -> (force_body, torque_body)

The recoil of transmitting level watts: a force of level / c opposite the boresight, applied at the terminal's location, in the body frame.

actuator_wrench(hinge::PanelHinge, level, ctx) -> (force_body, torque_body)

None: a hinge moves geometry. Its level is applied at control ticks.

actuator_wrench(gimbal::ThrusterGimbal, level, ctx) -> (force_body, torque_body)

None: a gimbal turns its thruster's thrust, whose wrench carries the turn.

SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Actuators.actuation_mode — Function
actuation_mode(controller) -> Symbol

How a control effector acts on the vehicle.

  • :ideal, the default: the effector's own control_wrench, control_propellant_rate and control_wheel_torque give the wrench and the mass rate.
  • :mounted: the engine derives the wrench, the propellant mass rate and the thruster firing levels from the actuators mounted on the spacecraft, at the levels actuator_levels returns. A reaction-wheel torque the effector reports through control_wheel_torque is still applied.
source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Actuators.actuator_levels — Function
actuator_levels(controller, i) -> Union{Nothing, AbstractVector{<:Real}}

For a control effector in :mounted mode: the level of every actuator mounted on spacecraft i (its position in the run, not its ID), in mounting order (see mounted_actuators), held between control ticks. Return nothing for a spacecraft the effector does not drive.

The engine calls this at every right-hand-side evaluation, and in the power, fault and result readers, for the spacecraft the controller's spacecraft_scope names (for every spacecraft in SpaceAGORA 0.6 and earlier), so compute the levels in update_control! and return stored values here.

The defaults of this and the other optional actuator methods take untyped arguments, so a method written for one kind or one controller, typed or not, is always the one that runs.

The chaser's mounted actuator levels held since the last control tick (:mounted actuation); none before the first tick.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.ActuatorMounting.add_actuator! — Function
add_actuator!(model::SpacecraftModel, link::Link, actuator) -> actuator

Mount actuator on link, which must be one of model.links (any other link is refused, since the dynamics would never see it). A Thruster joins link.thrusters (its direction is normalized and the link's thruster torque Jacobian gains its column). A ReactionWheel mounts on the root link only and joins its wheel assembly, whose momentum the engine integrates. A Magnetorquer joins link.magnetorquers and a LaserTerminal link.laser_terminals. A PanelHinge mounts on a non-root link, one per link, and sets that link's attitude to its current angle. A ThrusterGimbal mounts on the link of the thruster it turns and joins link.gimbals; the thruster's thrust turns by its current angle. A ControlMomentGyro mounts on the root link only and joins its wheel assembly's cmgs; its gimbal angle becomes a state the engine integrates. An actuator of any other AbstractActuator kind joins link.custom_actuators. Returns the actuator.

mounted_actuators(model::SpacecraftModel) -> Vector{NamedTuple{(:link, :actuator)}}

Every actuator mounted on model, with the link it is mounted on, in mounting order.

actuator_context(link::Link; mass_kg=NaN, q_ib=identity, ω_body=zero, magnetic_field_body=NaN, environment=nothing) -> ActuatorContext

The evaluation context of an actuator mounted on link: the link's configured pose in the body frame (its r and, for a non-root link, its attitude q), the vehicle's mass, attitude, rate and local magnetic field, and the sampled environment the mounted actuators asked for.

SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.ActuatorMounting.allocation_matrix — Function
allocation_matrix(model::SpacecraftModel) -> Matrix{Float64}

The 6 × n allocation matrix of the actuators mounted on model, in mounting order: column k stacks the force (rows 1 to 3, N) and the torque about the body reference point (rows 4 to 6, N m) of actuator k at level one, both in the body frame (see actuator_unit_wrench). Use it with allocate_actuators.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.ActuatorMounting.mounted_wrench — Function
mounted_wrench(model::SpacecraftModel, levels; mass_kg=NaN, q_ib=identity, ω_body=zero, wheel_momentum=nothing,
               magnetic_field_body=NaN, wheel_rates=nothing, environment=nothing)
    -> (force_body, torque_body, mass_rate_kg_s, wheel_torque_body)

The wrench of every actuator mounted on model at its level in levels (one per mounted actuator, in mounting order), each level first saturated: the total force and torque in the body frame, the torque about the body reference point; the total propellant mass rate (kg/s, positive while consuming); and the part of the torque the reaction wheels produce, which the engine integrates into their momentum. wheel_momentum holds the root wheel assembly's stored momenta (the integrated h_wheels state); with it, a wheel at its momentum limit produces no torque that would store more. Torque rods act in magnetic_field_body, the local field in the body frame (T). When wheel_rates is given (one entry per mounted wheel), each mounted wheel's own momentum rate, minus its saturated level, is subtracted into it: the engine integrates the mounted wheels from these, wheel by wheel. environment is the sampled environment the mounted actuators asked for, passed to each one's context. A thruster with firing dynamics acts at the thrust its last control tick scheduled rather than at its level.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Actuators.allocate_actuators — Function
allocate_actuators(B, command, lower, upper; method=:least_norm, minimum_fraction=0.0) -> Vector{Float64}

Actuator levels that produce command through the allocation matrix B, one column per actuator holding its wrench at level one (see allocation_matrix), within lower and upper (scalars, or one bound per actuator).

  • :least_norm: the least-norm solution pinv(B) * command, each level clipped to its bounds; a non-finite level gives zero. command has one entry per row of B.
  • :axis_clamp: six actuators whose force columns push along +x, -x, +y, -y, +z, -z, in that order. Each body axis of the force command (its first three entries) is served by the jet on its side, up to that jet's upper bound; jets below minimum_fraction of their upper bound are turned off. Every level is then clamped to its bounds, so a positive lower bound keeps an idle jet at that bound.

SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.SolarArray — Type
SolarArray(; normal, area_m2, efficiency, max_power_w=Inf)

A solar array on a spacecraft link: cells of area_m2 square metres facing normal (in the link frame, so an array on a hinged panel turns with it), converting the incident sunlight at efficiency. It generates area_m2 * efficiency * S * max(0, cos θ) * ν watts, capped at max_power_w, where S is the solar flux at the spacecraft's distance from the Sun (1361 W/m² at 1 AU), θ the angle between the normal and the Sun and ν the lit fraction of the Sun's disk (the eclipse model of the solar radiation pressure). Only the cell side generates.

SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.Battery — Type
Battery(; capacity_wh, initial_soc=NaN, charge_efficiency=1.0, discharge_efficiency=1.0,
        max_charge_w=Inf, max_discharge_w=Inf, recovery_soc=0.05)

The spacecraft's battery, mounted on the root link. Its stored energy, in watt-hours, is a state the engine integrates: it charges with the surplus of the generated power over the loads (times charge_efficiency, at most max_charge_w) and discharges to cover a shortfall (divided by discharge_efficiency, at most max_discharge_w). A full battery curtails the surplus; an empty one sheds actuator load until it has recharged to recovery_soc (see PowerSystems). initial_soc, a fraction of capacity_wh, defaults to the spacecraft's battery_energy_index (a percentage).

SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.ActuatorFault — Type
ActuatorFault(actuator; kind, start_s=0.0, stop_s=Inf, value=NaN)

A failure of one mounted actuator (the record mounted with add_actuator!) between start_s and stop_s (seconds into the run):

  • :failed: it applies level zero; a failed hinge or gimbal holds its angle.
  • :stuck: it holds value, or, when value is NaN, the level it was commanded to (a hinge or gimbal: the angle it had) when the fault began.
  • :degraded: its level and its limits scale by value, from 0 to 1.
  • :biased: value is added to its command before saturation.

Faults act on the mounted path only: the wrench, the propellant and power, hinge moves, the actuator_level and thruster-level columns and the plumes see them; the controller sees its own commands. On a thruster with firing dynamics a fault acts on the thrust it delivers, from the fault's start rather than from the next control tick: failed, none; stuck, the held level; degraded, the fraction value; biased, offset by value within its limits. Mount with add_fault!.
