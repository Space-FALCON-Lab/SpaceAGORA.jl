# gnc/guidance/rpo/hypr/path_costs.jl

## Purpose
The path objective: length, clearance violation, fuel proxy and obstacle penalty, normalised so the terms are comparable.

## Design & Implementation
`rpo_clearance_stats_from_samples` measures minimum clearance and violation fraction, `rpo_obstacle_sigmoid_penalty` smooths the penalty, `rpo_fuel_proxy_from_samples` approximates delta-v from curvature and speed, `rpo_path_cost_normalization_refs` sets scales, and `rpo_normalized_path_cost_components` and `rpo_path_cost` assemble the total.
