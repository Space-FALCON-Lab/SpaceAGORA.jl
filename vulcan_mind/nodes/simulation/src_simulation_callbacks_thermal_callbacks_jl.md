# simulation/callbacks/thermal_callbacks.jl

## Purpose
Computes per-link heat rates at each accepted step from the staged atmosphere and stores them for the heat-load integration.

## Design & Implementation
`get_thermal_callback` builds the callback; `update_thermal_sat!` and `_compute_stage_heat_rates!` evaluate the thermal model per link; `_heat_rate_buffer_for_sat!` sizes buffers; the callback runs threaded under the thermal thread decision.
