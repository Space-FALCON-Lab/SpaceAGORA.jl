# dynamics/coupled/aerodynamic_wrench_models.jl

## Purpose
Free-molecular aerodynamic force and torque on the multibody vehicle, per link, with the Hart coefficient model and several incidence conventions.

## Design & Implementation
`AerodynamicCoefficientfM` is the effector; `aerodynamic_coefficient_fM` evaluates lift and drag coefficients from speed ratio, temperature and incidence; per-link helpers resolve incidence from fixed attitude, quaternion or tumbling average, sum forces at each link's centre of pressure, and reduce to a body wrench. Batch variants serve the flat RHS path.
