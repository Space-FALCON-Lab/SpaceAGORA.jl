# dynamics/multibody_cloth/cloth_multibody.jl

## Purpose
The generic compliant multibody model: rigid bodies joined by six-axis spring-damper joints, with PD joint actuators, load diagnostics, and two fixed-step integrators.

## Design & Implementation
Value types for bodies, joints, topologies, actuators and loads; `build_compliant_topology` and `build_rectangular_compliant_grid` construct models; `compliant_joint_loads` and `compliant_multibody_dynamics` evaluate; `step_compliant_multibody_rk4`, `step_compliant_multibody_implicit_midpoint` and `simulate_compliant_multibody` integrate.
