# Third-party model files

`cases/caseA_hinged_panel.xml`, `cases/caseB_thruster_arms.xml` and `cases/caseC_contact.xml` are derived from the
MJCF scenes `sat_w_branching_panels.xml`, `sat_w_deployable_panels.xml`, `sat_w_thruster_arms.xml` and `sats_dock.xml`
of Basilisk 2.12 (`examples/mujoco/`).
