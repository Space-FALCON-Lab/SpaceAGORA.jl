# gnc/control/propulsive_maneuvers.jl

## Purpose
Executes propulsive manoeuvres commanded by guidance: converts a delta-v request into a constant-thrust burn window, fires the thruster, tracks propellant and applies the resulting force and mass flow.

## Design & Implementation
`_guidance_maneuver_command` reads the active command, `_constant_thrust_burn_duration_s` and `_effective_burn_window` size the burn, `_active_burn_plan` and `_clear_burn_plan!` manage the plan buffer, `_available_propellant_kg` guards against overdraw, and trace helpers emit optional per-burn logging under an environment flag.
