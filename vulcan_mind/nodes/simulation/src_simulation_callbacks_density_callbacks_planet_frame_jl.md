# simulation/callbacks/density_callbacks/planet_frame.jl

## Purpose
Shares one planet-fixed rotation per accepted step across every callback and RHS stage, and clears the per-step plan cache.

## Design & Implementation
`update_planet_frame_callback` is a `DiscreteCallback` whose `affect!` computes `l_pi` once — from the ephemeris cache, the backend or a memo — and stores it; `_planet_lpi_at` is the accessor other callbacks use; `_planet_relative_state` rotates a state into the planet frame.
