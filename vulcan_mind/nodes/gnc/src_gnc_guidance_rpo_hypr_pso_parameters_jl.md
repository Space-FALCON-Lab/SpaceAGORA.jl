# gnc/guidance/rpo/hypr/pso_parameters.jl

## Purpose
The planner configuration: `RPOPSOConfig` and its nested settings groups, the configurator that builds them from keywords, and the sampling-density rules.

## Design & Implementation
Settings structs for adaptive sampling, adaptive sizing, culling, early stopping, objective weights, probing, reexploration, refinement, RRT warm start, schedules and stagnation learning; `rpo_pso_config` and `RPOPSOConfigurator` assemble and copy configurations; `rpo_hypr_sampling_density_m` ties sampling density to the safety distance.
