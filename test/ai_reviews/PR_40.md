# AI Review — PR #40

## Scope
- Reviewed the two follow-ups to #39 addressing the PI's objection that residual telemetry errors compound more than honest physics mismatch should allow: a frame-kinematics bug in the co-rotation (transport) term of the atmosphere-relative velocity, and a new non-compounding drag-fidelity metric that separates per-pass drag error from campaign-long accumulation.
- Review method: the transport-term algebra was derived independently (v_pf = L_PI·v_i − ω_pf×(L_PI·r_i) from differentiating r_pf = L_PI·r_i with L̇_PI·r = −ω_pf×(L_PI·r)) and pinned by unit tests including a tilted-pole frame-fixed-point test and a legacy-equivalence test for z-spin frames; the diagnostic was validated on synthetic curves with known decay ratios; the full integration suite passed; and a full-profile telemetry study on the native GRAM rig confirmed the expected directional impact (deeper Odyssey survival at Mars, negligible change at slow-spinning Venus). Quantitative results are in the lab notes (lab members only): Space-FALCON-Lab/SpaceAGORA-notes, notes/2026-07-telemetry-regression-bugfix-study.md.

## Findings
- P1 (fixed): the misdirected transport term biased the airspeed magnitude and direction by a few percent per pass at fast-rotating planets — small enough to hide under the frame bug #39 removed, large enough to compound into corridor drift over a several-hundred-orbit aerobraking replay.
- P2 (measured, not a code defect): with both fixes in, the new diagnostic shows per-pass drag within a few tens of percent of flight at both planets; the remaining absolute-threshold exceedances are that residual model bias compounding through open-loop maneuver replay, plus the still-truncated Odyssey run. These are atmosphere-model and study-design questions, documented with numbers in the lab notes.
- P3 (noted, unchanged): pass/fail thresholds untouched per the PI's instruction; the diagnostic columns are non-gating.

## P1 Assessment
- The transport-term bug was the P1 candidate; it is fixed, unit-pinned, and its directional impact confirmed in the full study.
- Unresolved P1: No

## Tests Added/Updated
- Tilted-pole transport term: frame-fixed point has zero planet-frame velocity; round trip restores the inertial state; z-aligned pole reproduces the legacy formula exactly.
- Decay diagnostic: synthetic k×-decay returns ratio k (median and total); a maneuver inside an interval drops exactly that interval; truncated sim axis restricts to the overlap; flat telemetry and too-short series return NaN-safe placeholders.

## Residual Risk
- Trajectories change slightly for every wind/drag-enabled run at fast-rotating planets; studies calibrated against the old co-rotation direction may shift within a few percent.
- The decay diagnostic adds three columns to the telemetry summary CSV; downstream consumers that hardcode column sets should be tolerant (the runner builds rows by merge, and the enforce gate reads named fields).
- The onboard aerobraking guidance predictors (t_edg) use the SPICE sxform path and were already consistent; they were not modified here.
