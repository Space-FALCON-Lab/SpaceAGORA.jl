## Findings

No P1 or P2 findings. Points a reviewer should check:

- Low, by design: the specific force uses the solver's derivative at the
  accepted step (`get_du`): exact for first-same-as-last methods, from the
  dense interpolant otherwise; at a step where another callback switches the
  dynamics (a burn edge, a fault), the reading is the acceleration before the
  switch.
- Low, by design: gravity is the force of the effectors for which
  `is_gravitational` is true, each evaluated by the route the dynamics take
  (a model with only the deprecated `calcForceTorque` included, from review);
  a user-defined gravity model without an `is_gravitational` method is felt
  by the accelerometer as a non-gravitational force.
- Low, modelling: the accelerometer and the GNSS antenna are at the centre
  of mass (no lever-arm, centripetal or Euler terms); GNSS has no outages and
  reports the inertial frame, not a planet-fixed one; the sun sensor has no
  albedo or Earth-limb effects and uses the body's mean solar distance.
- Low: the Sun's direction needs ephemerides that can place it (SPICE, or
  `SimpleEphemeridesModel` for Earth); without them the sun sensor reads NaN.
