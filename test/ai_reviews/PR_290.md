## Findings

No blocking source, catalog or integration defect was identified. The shipped entry pins near-surface 1.2.0 and
upper 1.0.0. Existing entries are byte-preserved apart from the catalog revision line. The composite adds no payload
and verifies each component through its existing named-entry path. The catalog checks the supported component kinds,
publication status, planet, frozen epoch, ellipsoid, stored winds and handover contract; nested composites are refused.

Construction checks provider availability before component retrieval, forwards offline policy, and rejects explicit
files and unreleased composites. Direct single-payload resolution refuses a composite. Evaluation retains the existing
composition dispatch and component errors. Returned provenance is independent of the model's metadata, and copying
the model preserves its record. The CLI uses component resolution for fetching and model construction for checking.

The retained equivalence and geometric-overlap evidence supports this pinned pairing. Documentation and public PR
wording keep the frozen epoch, component refusals, abrupt handover and bounded validation scope. They do not establish
uniform accuracy across the stored domain or extend validation to other applications.

The earlier evidence-record findings are closed: chronology is qualified as local file-time/session evidence without
a pre-run immutable pin; the installation check discloses its synthetic results-writer fixture instead of claiming a
simulation; and the reporting counts are corrected. The original records remain preserved. No numerical result or
acceptance threshold changed.

## Residual Risk

The model retains component refusals and abrupt handover behavior. Scientific validation remains limited to the
catalog's stated descent envelope and frozen epoch; it does not establish skimming or repeated handover crossings,
other vehicles or epochs, landing accuracy, or engine use of the near-surface sound speed. The lower component's
geometric overlap is supported for this pinned pairing and must be checked separately for future pairings.

Catalog overrides remain trusted inputs. Malformed optional metadata shapes can be accepted or produce lower-level
exceptions instead of a curated argument error; informational metadata is not a comprehensive schema contract. These
robustness limitations do not affect the shipped entry or its verified component data.

This review accepts the implementation within that scope and does not authorize a merge.
