Actuators API

This page documents the supported public interface available from SpaceAGORA.

Actuators

SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.AbstractTypes.AbstractActuator — Type
AbstractActuator

Stable extension interface for a piece of hardware mounted on a spacecraft link that acts on the vehicle when commanded: a thruster, and the kinds added after it. A kind implements actuator_wrench and actuator_limits; the other actuator methods have defaults. Mount it with add_actuator!.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.Thruster — Type
Thruster(; max_thrust, location, direction, Isp, ...)

One jet mounted on a spacecraft link. direction is the direction of the thrust it puts on the vehicle (not the direction of its exhaust), in the link frame; location is the nozzle position in the link frame, relative to the link's centre of mass, metres; max_thrust is its rating in newtons and Isp its specific impulse in seconds. The remaining fields describe its firing dynamics and hold its state during a run.

A Thruster is an AbstractActuator whose level is a thrust in newtons, from zero to max_thrust. Mount it with add_actuator!.

With firing_dynamics=true, a mounted thruster does not deliver its command at once: at each control tick of the effector commanding it, the commanded thrust becomes an on-time within the control period, subject to min_firing_time and the Schmitt trigger (level_on, level_off), and the thrust ramps with cutoff_frequency; the vehicle feels the resulting average thrust until the next tick. Off by default, the thruster acts at its saturated command.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.ReactionWheel — Type
ReactionWheel(; spin_axis, max_torque_nm=Inf, max_momentum_nms=Inf)

One reaction wheel, mounted on the spacecraft's root link with add_actuator!. Its level is the torque it puts on the vehicle about its spin axis, in newton-metres; the wheel's own angular momentum along that axis changes at minus the same rate. spin_axis is in the root-link (body) frame. max_torque_nm bounds the level and max_momentum_nms stops a torque that would store more momentum; both default to unlimited.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.Magnetorquer — Type
Magnetorquer(; axis, max_dipole_am2=Inf)

One magnetic torque rod mounted on a spacecraft link with add_actuator!. Its level is the dipole it is commanded to, in ampere square metres, along axis (in the link frame); limits ±max_dipole_am2, unlimited by default. The torque on the vehicle is the dipole crossed with the local magnetic field, which the engine takes from the run's MagneticTorqueRodModel field source (a tilted dipole or IGRF, Earth only), or the dipole when the run has none.

Magnet above is a permanent dipole, not an actuator: the MagneticTorqueRodModel effector applies it whether or not anything commands the vehicle.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.LaserTerminal — Type
LaserTerminal(; boresight, max_power_w, location=zero, magnification=1.0, range_m=200e3, efficiency=1.0)

One optical terminal mounted on a spacecraft link with add_actuator!. Its level is the optical power it transmits, in watts, from zero to max_power_w, along boresight (in the link frame) from location (link frame, metres). The beam carries momentum away, so the vehicle recoils with a force of level / c opposite the boresight; the terminal draws level / efficiency watts of electrical power.

magnification and range_m describe an inter-satellite link between two terminals of this design: the open-cavity magnification factor and the maximum range of InterLinkParameters(terminal), which the constellation interlink model uses for the force between linked spacecraft.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.PanelHinge — Type
PanelHinge(; axis, min_angle_rad=-Inf, max_angle_rad=Inf, max_rate_rad_s=Inf, angle_rad=0.0)

A hinge that turns one non-root link (a solar panel, a boom) about axis, in the body frame, mounted on that link with add_actuator!. Its level is the commanded hinge angle in radians; the link's attitude is the rotation by the hinge's current angle about axis. The angle stays within [min_angle_rad, max_angle_rad] and moves at most max_rate_rad_s between control ticks; all three default to unlimited. angle_rad holds the current angle (the state the engine advances at each control tick).

A hinge changes the vehicle's geometry, not its wrench: the engine applies its level at the control tick of the effector that commands it, between accepted integration steps, and the aerodynamic and radiation models see the new link attitude from then on.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Actuators.ActuatorContext — Type
ActuatorContext(; link_r_body, link_to_body, mass_kg, q_ib, ω_body, magnetic_field_body, environment=nothing)

What an actuator kind may use when it is evaluated:

  • link_r_body: the mounting link's centre of mass in the spacecraft body frame, relative to the body reference point, metres;
  • link_to_body: the rotation from the link frame to the body frame;
  • mass_kg: the vehicle's current mass;
  • q_ib: the attitude quaternion, inertial to body, scalar last;
  • ω_body: the body angular velocity, rad/s;
  • magnetic_field_body: the local magnetic field in the body frame, T, when a mounted actuator asks for it (see actuator_requirements); NaN otherwise;
  • environment: the sampled environment (an EnvironmentSample: planet frame, atmosphere, Sun) when a mounted actuator asks for any of it; nothing otherwise.

The link pose is the link's configured geometry. The attitude and the rate are the identity and zero when the run does not propagate attitude.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Actuators.actuator_wrench — Function

```julia actuator_wrench(actuator, level, ctx::ActuatorContext) -> (force_body, torque_body) ``` Required for every actuator kind: the force (N) and the torque (N m) the actuator puts on the vehicle at the commanded `level`, both in the spacecraft body frame, the torque about the body reference point. `level` is in the kind's unit (newtons for a thruster) and has already been passed through [`actuator_saturate`](@ref).

```julia actuator_wrench(thruster::Thruster, level, ctx) -> (force_body, torque_body) ``` A thrust of `level` newtons along the thruster's `direction`, applied at its `location`: the force in the body frame and its torque about the body reference point. A zero or non-finite direction gives no wrench.

```julia actuator_wrench(wheel::ReactionWheel, level, ctx) -> (force_body, torque_body) ``` A torque of `level` newton-metres on the vehicle about the wheel's spin axis, in the body frame; no force.

```julia actuator_wrench(rod::Magnetorquer, level, ctx) -> (force_body, torque_body) ``` The torque `m x B` of a dipole of `level` A m² along the rod's axis in the body-frame field `ctx.magnetic_field_body` (T); no force. No field (NaN) gives no torque.

```julia actuator_wrench(terminal::LaserTerminal, level, ctx) -> (force_body, torque_body) ``` The recoil of transmitting `level` watts: a force of `level / c` opposite the boresight, applied at the terminal's location, in the body frame.

```julia actuator_wrench(hinge::PanelHinge, level, ctx) -> (force_body, torque_body) ``` None: a hinge moves geometry. Its level is applied at control ticks.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Actuators.actuator_limits — Function
actuator_limits(actuator) -> (lower, upper)

Required for every actuator kind: the bounds of its level, in the kind's unit. An infinite bound means unlimited.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Actuators.actuator_saturate — Function
actuator_saturate(actuator, level) -> Float64

The level the hardware can produce. Default: level clamped to actuator_limits; a non-finite level gives the idle level, zero clamped to the limits.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Actuators.actuator_consumption — Function

```julia actuator_consumption(actuator, level, ctx::ActuatorContext) -> (mass_rate_kg_s, power_w) ``` Optional: the propellant the actuator uses per second at `level` (kg/s, positive while consuming) and the electrical power it draws (W). Default: no consumption.

```julia actuator_consumption(thruster::Thruster, level, ctx) -> (mass_rate_kg_s, power_w) ``` Propellant use at a thrust of `level` newtons, `level / (Isp g0)`; zero when `Isp` is not positive. No electrical power.

```julia actuator_consumption(terminal::LaserTerminal, level, ctx) -> (mass_rate_kg_s, power_w) ``` No propellant; `level / efficiency` watts of electrical power.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Actuators.actuator_fraction — Function
actuator_fraction(actuator, level) -> Float64

Optional: level as a fraction of the actuator's capability, from 0 to 1. It fills the thruster_level result columns and the viewer's plumes. Default: the magnitude of level over the larger magnitude of the two limits; zero when that capability is zero or not finite.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Actuators.actuator_unit_wrench — Function
actuator_unit_wrench(actuator, ctx::ActuatorContext) -> (force_body, torque_body)

Optional: the wrench at level one, which is the actuator's column in an allocation matrix. Default: actuator_wrench(actuator, 1.0, ctx), right for a kind whose wrench is linear in its level.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Actuators.actuation_mode — Function
actuation_mode(controller) -> Symbol

How a control effector acts on the vehicle.

  • :ideal, the default: the effector's own calcControlForceTorque, calcControlMassFlowRate and calcReactionWheelTorque give the wrench and the mass rate.
  • :mounted: the engine derives the wrench, the propellant mass rate and the thruster firing levels from the actuators mounted on the spacecraft, at the levels actuator_levels returns. A reaction-wheel torque the effector reports through calcReactionWheelTorque is still applied.
source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Actuators.actuator_levels — Function

```julia actuator_levels(controller, i) -> Union{Nothing, AbstractVector{<:Real}} ``` For a control effector in `:mounted` mode: the level of every actuator mounted on spacecraft `i` (its position in the run, not its ID), in mounting order (see `mounted_actuators`), held between control ticks. Return `nothing` for a spacecraft the effector does not drive. The engine calls this at every right-hand-side evaluation, so compute the levels in `calcControlEffect!` and return stored values here. The defaults of this and the other optional actuator methods take untyped arguments, so a method written for one kind or one controller, typed or not, is always the one that runs.

The chaser's mounted actuator levels held since the last control tick (`:mounted` actuation); none before the first tick.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.ActuatorMounting.add_actuator! — Function
add_actuator!(model::SpacecraftModel, link::Link, actuator) -> actuator

Mount actuator on link, which must be one of model.links (any other link is refused, since the dynamics would never see it). A Thruster joins link.thrusters (its direction is normalized and the link's thruster torque Jacobian gains its column). A ReactionWheel mounts on the root link only and joins its wheel assembly, whose momentum the engine integrates. A Magnetorquer joins link.magnetorquers and a LaserTerminal link.laser_terminals. A PanelHinge mounts on a non-root link, one per link, and sets that link's attitude to its current angle. An actuator of any other AbstractActuator kind joins link.custom_actuators. Returns the actuator.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.ActuatorMounting.mounted_actuators — Function
mounted_actuators(model::SpacecraftModel) -> Vector{NamedTuple{(:link, :actuator)}}

Every actuator mounted on model, with the link it is mounted on, in mounting order.

SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.ActuatorMounting.actuator_context — Function
actuator_context(link::Link; mass_kg=NaN, q_ib=identity, ω_body=zero, magnetic_field_body=NaN, environment=nothing) -> ActuatorContext

The evaluation context of an actuator mounted on link: the link's configured pose in the body frame (its r and, for a non-root link, its attitude q), the vehicle's mass, attitude, rate and local magnetic field, and the sampled environment the mounted actuators asked for.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.ActuatorMounting.allocation_matrix — Function
allocation_matrix(model::SpacecraftModel) -> Matrix{Float64}

The 6 × n allocation matrix of the actuators mounted on model, in mounting order: column k stacks the force (rows 1 to 3, N) and the torque about the body reference point (rows 4 to 6, N m) of actuator k at level one, both in the body frame (see actuator_unit_wrench). Use it with allocate_actuators.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.ActuatorMounting.mounted_wrench — Function
mounted_wrench(model::SpacecraftModel, levels; mass_kg=NaN, q_ib=identity, ω_body=zero, wheel_momentum=nothing,
               magnetic_field_body=NaN, wheel_rates=nothing, environment=nothing)
    -> (force_body, torque_body, mass_rate_kg_s, wheel_torque_body)

The wrench of every actuator mounted on model at its level in levels (one per mounted actuator, in mounting order), each level first saturated: the total force and torque in the body frame, the torque about the body reference point; the total propellant mass rate (kg/s, positive while consuming); and the part of the torque the reaction wheels produce, which the engine integrates into their momentum. wheel_momentum holds the root wheel assembly's stored momenta (the integrated h_wheels state); with it, a wheel at its momentum limit produces no torque that would store more. Torque rods act in magnetic_field_body, the local field in the body frame (T). When wheel_rates is given (one entry per mounted wheel), each mounted wheel's own momentum rate, minus its saturated level, is subtracted into it: the engine integrates the mounted wheels from these, wheel by wheel. environment is the sampled environment the mounted actuators asked for, passed to each one's context. A thruster with firing dynamics acts at the thrust its last control tick scheduled rather than at its level.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Actuators.allocate_actuators — Function
allocate_actuators(B, command, lower, upper; method=:least_norm, minimum_fraction=0.0) -> Vector{Float64}

Actuator levels that produce command through the allocation matrix B, one column per actuator holding its wrench at level one (see allocation_matrix), within lower and upper (scalars, or one bound per actuator).

  • :least_norm: the least-norm solution pinv(B) * command, each level clipped to its bounds; a non-finite level gives zero. command has one entry per row of B.
  • :axis_clamp: six actuators whose force columns push along +x, -x, +y, -y, +z, -z, in that order. Each body axis of the force command (its first three entries) is served by the jet on its side, up to that jet's upper bound; jets below minimum_fraction of their upper bound are turned off. Every level is then clamped to its bounds, so a positive lower bound keeps an idle jet at that bound.
source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Components.six_axis_thrusters — Function
six_axis_thrusters(model::SixAxisThrusterModel) -> Vector{Thruster}

The six jets of a six-axis thruster model as mountable thrusters, in the model's column order: each one's thrust direction, location, rating, specific impulse and minimum firing time. Mount them on the vehicle with add_actuator! to drive them in :mounted actuation.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Actuators.propellant_mass_rate — Function
propellant_mass_rate(thrust_n, isp_s) -> Float64

The propellant a rocket engine uses per second (kg/s) at thrust_n newtons with a specific impulse of isp_s seconds: thrust_n / (isp_s * g0). Every thrust model of the package uses this one formula; callers handle zero or invalid inputs.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Actuators.ActuatorRequirements — Type
ActuatorRequirements(; planet_frame=false, atmosphere=false, solar=false, magnetic_field=false)

What an actuator kind needs sampled before it is evaluated: the planet-fixed frame, the atmosphere (density, temperature, wind), the Sun's position, the local magnetic field. The engine samples the union over the actuators mounted on a spacecraft, once per evaluation, and passes it in the ActuatorContext: the field as magnetic_field_body, the rest as environment.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.Actuators.actuator_requirements — Function
actuator_requirements(actuator) -> ActuatorRequirements

Optional: what the actuator needs sampled to be evaluated. Default: nothing. The torque rod asks for the magnetic field.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.ActuatorMounting.applied_actuator_levels — Function
applied_actuator_levels(model::SpacecraftModel, levels; wheel_momentum=nothing) -> Vector{Float64}

The level each mounted actuator actually applies, in mounting order: its command saturated to its limits; a thruster with firing dynamics at the thrust its last tick scheduled; a wheel at its momentum limit at zero when the command would store more (given wheel_momentum); a hinge at its current angle. These fill the actuator_level result columns.

source
SpaceAGORA.SimulationModel.ActuatorMounting.mounted_power — Function
mounted_power(model::SpacecraftModel, levels; wheel_momentum=nothing) -> Float64

The electrical power (W) the mounted actuators draw at their applied levels (see applied_actuator_levels): the sum of each one's actuator_consumption(...).power_w, evaluated without a sampled environment. It fills the actuator_power result column.
