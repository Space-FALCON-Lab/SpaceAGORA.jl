# simulation/engine/dynamics_rhs.jl · _rhs*

## Purpose
The flat queue's cost model: static and observed per-effector costs, per-item estimates, work statistics, and the packet-scheduler decision.

## Design & Implementation
`_rhs_effector_cost_rank` and `_rhs_effector_static_cost_ns` give cold-start estimates; `_rhs_effector_estimated_cost_ns` prefers observed EMAs; `_rhs_flat_item_estimated_cost_ns` and `_rhs_flat_packet_work_stats` aggregate over items; `_rhs_flat_use_packet_scheduler` applies the thresholds; `_rhs_flat_state_sample_from_buffers` builds samples for workers.
