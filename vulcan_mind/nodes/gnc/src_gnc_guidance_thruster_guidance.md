# gnc/guidance/thruster_guidance/

## Purpose
Guidance for campaign propulsive manoeuvres: schedules apoapsis burns by orbit number for replay, and raises periapsis at campaign end.

## Design & Implementation
`AerobrakingCampaignPropulsiveManeuverGuidanceModel` fires configured delta-v at the Nth apoapsis with optional flight-apoapsis-ratio scaling; `ApoapsisTargetPeriapsisRaiseGuidanceModel` burns near apoapsis until a target is met; oblate-altitude helpers convert between radii and altitudes.
