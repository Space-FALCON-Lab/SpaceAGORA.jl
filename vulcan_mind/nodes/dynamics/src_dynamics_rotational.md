# dynamics/rotational/

## Purpose
Rigid-body attitude dynamics: quaternion kinematics and Euler's equation with gyroscopic and reaction-wheel terms.

## Design & Implementation
`quaternion_derivative` gives the scalar-last kinematic rate, `angular_acceleration` solves Euler's equation with optional wheel momentum, `combine_torques` sums contributions, and `body_angular_velocity` and `body_torque` normalise inputs to static vectors on the hot path.

## Limitations
