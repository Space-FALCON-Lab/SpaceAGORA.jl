# RPOReplanningSphere

## Purpose
Describes a dynamic spherical obstacle in the RTN (radial, transverse, normal) frame of the RPO target, with a lifetime window and constant drift velocity, so that HYPR replanning can inject time-varying keep-out zones into the path-clearance checks used for tests and demos.

## Design & Implementation
A `Base.@kwdef struct` with fields `center_rtn::SVector{3,Float64}` (m), `radius_m::Float64`, `appear_time_s::Float64 = 0.0`, `disappear_time_s::Float64 = Inf`, `velocity_rtn_mps::SVector{3,Float64}` defaulting to zero, and `label::String = "dynamic_sphere"`. A second, validating outer constructor `RPOReplanningSphere(center_rtn, radius_m::Real; appear_time_s, disappear_time_s, velocity_rtn_mps, label)` converts every input to `Float64`/`SVector`/`String`, throws `ArgumentError` when `radius_m <= 0` or when `disappear_time_s < appear_time_s`, then calls the positional constructor. `rpo_replanning_sphere_center` evaluates the drifted centre at time `t`, and `rpo_active_replanning_spheres` filters by the lifetime window.

## Limitations
The keyword constructor generated by `@kwdef` bypasses the validation, so `RPOReplanningSphere(center_rtn=..., radius_m=-1.0)` is accepted. Motion is restricted to constant velocity from `appear_time_s`; no acceleration or trajectory is representable. Because `label` participates in `rpo_replanning_signature`, two spheres differing only in label force a replan even when geometry is unchanged.
