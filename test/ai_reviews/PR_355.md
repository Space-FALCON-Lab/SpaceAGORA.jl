## Findings

- Resolved the confirmed P2 finding: at `adcae83d2`, the gate accepts `randperm!(rng_buffer)`, `randcycle!(rng_buffer)`, and `randsubseq!(rng_output, values, p)` because the omitted arity rules let the destination name satisfy the first-stream naming check. The new candidate rejects all three and accepts the corresponding explicit-stream calls. A before/after probe demonstrates each result.
- A two-argument requirement alone would not fix `randsubseq!`: its default-stream form already has three positional arguments. Its explicit-stream form requires four.
- Every recognized mutating draw now comes from the same table that defines its arity. It cannot be recognized by the gate while silently missing an arity rule.

| Mutating draws | Required explicit-stream form | Positional arity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `randperm!`, `randcycle!` | `(rng, A)` | Exactly two |
| `randsubseq!` | `(rng, S, A, p)` | Exactly four |
| `rand!`, `randn!`, `randexp!`, `shuffle!` | `(rng, A, ...)` | At least two |

Keywords are excluded from the positional count. Splats cannot supply any required position; the original four forms still permit extra arguments or splats after their explicit stream and destination. The fixed-arity forms reject additional positional arguments and trailing splats.

The first argument must still be an identifier or field whose final name contains `rng`, case-insensitive. This remains a static syntax-and-naming check, not type inference or proof of stream provenance. The header retains that limit.

## Residual Risk

Arity and naming still cannot establish runtime types or stream ownership. For example, the global-stream overload `rand!(rng_buffer, Float64)` can satisfy the two-argument naming rule; this follow-up does not claim to reject every implicit-stream overload. Indirect aliases, macro-generated or broadcast calls, and provenance remain outside the static check. Required arguments expressed through splats are conservatively refused because their arity cannot be established from the source. Legitimate RNG expressions that are not named identifiers or fields must first be bound to a suitably named variable. The textual global-stream pattern checks retain their existing limits; behavioral tests remain necessary for reproducibility.
