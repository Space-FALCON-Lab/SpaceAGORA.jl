# gnc/guidance/rpo/hypr/pso_adaptive_policy.jl

## Purpose
Sizes the swarm search from the geometry: probes the straight-line approach and scales particles, iterations and waypoints by estimated complexity.

## Design & Implementation
`rpo_probe_geometry_metrics` samples the chord for clearance, `rpo_estimate_geometry_complexity` blends buffer and clearance terms into a unit score, and `rpo_adaptive_pso_config` maps that score onto a configuration.
