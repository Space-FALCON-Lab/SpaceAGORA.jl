# PR329 review: write the viewer payload's heat rate in W/m²

## Scope

Review of the SpaceAGORA `viewer-heat-rate-units` branch against main
(`2ea9d92f2`). The PR converts the saved heat rate from W/cm² to W/m² where
the viewer payload is built, in the single-run and the ensemble exporters,
and adds a unit test for both.

Authorship disclosure: this record was drafted by the author of the PR. It
does not replace an independent review.

The defect: `sc<i>_heat_rate` is saved in W/cm² (outputs page), and both
payload builders copied it unconverted into the frames field
`heat_rate_w_m2`. The companion viewer pinned in
`packages/VisualizerSource.toml` (revision `6c0080743`) reads that field as
W/m²: `viewer/src/data.js` decodes it as the `heat_rate` scalar documented as
W/m², the selection panel (`viewer/src/main.js`) divides it by 1e4 to show
W/cm², and the trail colour mode (`viewer/src/spacecraft.js`) labels it
"heat rate (W/m²)". A Mars aerobraking pass peaking at 0.14 W/cm² therefore
showed 0.0000 W/cm² in the panel and a W/m² legend ending at 0.140.

## Changed Files

- `src/interfaces/analysis/visualization/scene/viewer_payload.jl`: new
  constant `HEAT_RATE_W_CM2_TO_W_M2 = 1.0e4` beside the other payload
  constants; `build_viewer_frames` multiplies each heat-rate sample by it.
- `src/interfaces/analysis/visualization/scene/ensemble_export.jl`:
  `build_ensemble_frames` multiplies each resampled heat-rate sample by the
  same constant (same module, `SceneVisualization`). `NaN` rows outside a
  sample's span stay `NaN`.

## Findings

No P1 or P2 findings.

Points a reviewer should check:
- Low, by design: no other producer or consumer of `heat_rate_w_m2` exists in
  this repository (`src`, `ext`, `test`, `docs`, `scripts`, `examples`); the
  articulation test only checks that the field is present. The viewer reads
  the field only through its `heat_rate` scalar, so no viewer change is
  needed. Viewer pages exported before the change keep the old values.
- Low, outside this repository: the companion's standalone CSV loader
  (`viewer/src/standalone.js`) also copies `sc<i>_heat_rate` into
  `heat_rate_w_m2` without the factor; it needs the same change there.
- Low, unchanged: the ensemble builder's byte budget per frame does not count
  the density and heat-rate blocks, so an ensemble payload can exceed its
  budget slightly. Not touched here.

## P1 Assessment

No P1 findings. The change scales one payload field by a constant; no
simulation, saved-results column or other payload field changes, and the
Float32 encoding holds the converted values without loss of relative
precision.

## Tests Added/Updated

- `test/unit/visualization/viewer_bundle_tests.jl`, new testset "heat rate
  reaches the viewer in W/m²": a synthetic results table without
  `sc1_heat_rate` leaves the field absent; with a known linear
  `sc1_heat_rate` column, the decoded single-run field equals 1e4 times the
  column; two ensemble samples of different lengths on one scene give 1e4
  times each sample's column on the common axis, and the shorter sample stays
  `NaN` after its end.

Evidence on the branch:
- `viewer_bundle_tests.jl`: 414 of 414 pass. With the conversion constant
  redefined to 1 (main's behaviour), exactly the three value comparisons of
  the new testset fail and the rest of the file passes.
- `ensemble_tests.jl`: 88 of 88 pass. `articulation_tests.jl` (with the EDG
  companion loaded as the unit runner does): 64 of 64 pass; it runs a short
  aerobraking pass with the viewer page written and checks that the page
  carries the heat-rate field.
- The 31 PR gates (`test/contracts/pr_runtests.jl`), including the P1
  findings gate: all pass.

## Residual Risk

- The test covers the payload builders, not the rendered page; the viewer's
  division by 1e4 and its W/m² legend were checked by reading the pinned
  source, not by a browser run.
- The standalone CSV route in the companion viewer keeps the old behaviour
  until that repository is changed.
