# PR351 review: control moment gyros

## Scope

Review of the SpaceAGORA `control-moment-gyro` branch against its base,
`thruster-gimbal` (PR #346). Step 7d of the actuator plan, second part: the
single-gimbal control moment gyro, with its gimbal angle integrated in the
ODE state (D-18). The code with its tests, the docs, a merge of
`thruster-gimbal` (its review fix and record), then the review fix below.

Authorship disclosure: this record was drafted by the author of the PR. The
plan assigns behaviour PRs of the actuator layer an independent reviewer (the
PI, or the second reviewer when available); this record does not replace that
review.

## Findings

No P1 or P2 findings. Points a reviewer should check:

- Fixed after the automated review of c61cdb1d4 (94eeb7547): a run without
  control effectors takes a fast path that skipped rebuilding the commanded
  rates, so a spacecraft reused from an earlier run (`isolate_state=false`)
  kept that run's last CMG rates and its gimbals kept moving; the mounted
  wheels' rates (from #319) had the same gap. Both are now cleared on the
  fast path too (`_clear_mounted_rates!`), at no allocation.
- Low, by design: the rotor's momentum is constant (no spin-motor dynamics)
  and the gimbal follows the commanded rate at once (no gimbal-motor
  dynamics or gimbal inertia); the gimbal angle has no limits.
- Low, by design: CMGs are held by the root link's wheel assembly, which
  every right-hand-side route passes to the attitude equation; that keeps
  one integration point at the cost of a wider assembly record.
- Low: `cmg_steering` normalizes the Jacobian by the largest rotor momentum
  before the singularity measure, so `λ0` and `μ` are dimensionless; it
  does not plan around singularities (no null-space motion).
- Low: the split solver modes are refused rather than supported, as for the
  battery.
- Low: the Apollo descent controller holds a CMG at gimbal rate zero (from
  #346's fix), like any non-thruster actuator.

## P1 Assessment

No P1 findings. Without a CMG no state, column or term is added (the CMG
branch in the attitude equation is skipped when the assembly holds none);
the comparison below shows the Apollo fingerprint, the momentum example, the
walkthrough and an ideal burn's final state bitwise unchanged against #346.

## Tests Added/Updated
