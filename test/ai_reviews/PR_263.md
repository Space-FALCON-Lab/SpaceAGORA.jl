## Findings

- **The problem (TRX50, P1, 32 threads, median of 11).** With the barrier forced on for every workload, the static routes ran 64 spacecraft in 27.5-28.6 s against 3.02-3.08 s without it, and 256 spacecraft in 7.2-7.6 s against 2.65-2.74 s. At 1024 spacecraft the barrier was faster: 1.64 s against 1.83-1.99 s.
- **The barrier is more than a dispatch.** With it on, the default plan also drops the flat route's satellites-per-worker floor (`_rhs_flat_default_admits`) and the pre-pass slice floor. The auto probe therefore times the whole bundle, and the default plan reads the per-call arm (`_spin_barrier_active`). Without the floors, routing would have differed from `=1`.
- **The probe was iterated three times on local measurements before it held.** Each version failed on a measured case:
  1. Timing the RHS call alone over 5-call medians misjudged the 64-spacecraft case. The barrier won the windows but lost the solve by 35%, because part of its cost falls between calls and arrives as rare stalls.
  2. A challenger allowed to retry over several windows picked noise.
  3. The current probe times RHS calls start to start, takes the incumbent's mean over 256 calls, and cuts the challenger after any 32-call window in which it is behind by 5%. The challenger takes over only after a full 256 calls ahead by 5%.
- **Spinner placement (fixed).** Left to the scheduler, spinners could share a thread for a whole solve, since each yields only every 1024 polls. At 256 spacecraft on 12 threads this cost 300 µs per call instead of 80. They are now sticky tasks, one per default-pool thread, none on the creating thread. Forced on improved too (median 4.3 → 3.9 s there).
- **Pool release.** Turning the barrier off releases the scope's pool (`release_spin_barrier_pool!`) under its `run_lock`. A dispatcher that fetched the pool before the release finds `stop` set under the lock and takes a fresh pool, instead of waiting on workers that have exited. Pools remain per `with_policy_context` scope.
- **Observation labelling.** `_rhs_region_ran_full` returns false while the probe runs the non-incumbent arm, so the cost model and policy do not learn that arm's timings as the solve's configuration. The two dispatches keep separate region-width tuners (`:rhs_harmonics_batch`, `:rhs_harmonics_batch_spin`). The width search, the observation fix from #259 and the control-effector fast path from #260 are unchanged.
- **Pre-solve.** The tuner is disabled during the calibration sweep, which runs with the barrier off, and is reset after it. The in-run width trial (`rhs_width_trial`) takes precedence: while it runs, no barrier probe advances.
- **Harness.**
  - `calibrate_machine.jl --repeats` read `dispatch_ns_base`, a field since renamed. It now reports the four `dispatch_{pool,batch}_ns_*` fields; verified with `--repeats=2`.
  - `SPACEAGORA_PPB_SERIAL_FIRST_RUNG_PHASES=P6ps` times serial on the first rung only. Verified with a dry run.

## P1 Assessment

No result changes. Every arm computes the same derivative bit for bit: the barrier changes only which thread runs which spacecraft slice, and the routes it selects are bit-identical to one another. Tests compare the RHS across `0`, `1` and `auto` with the arm switching mid-sequence, and whole-solve final states across the three settings. In the local sweeps every case and seed had one final state across all modes and settings.

## Residual Risk

- The probe runs only in `spacecraft_dynamics!`. The split-solver right-hand sides (`spacecraft_dynamics_slow!`, `_explicit_remainder!`) never probe, so under `auto` they run with the barrier off, main's previous default.
- At 64 spacecraft on 12 threads the two arms are close over long windows. Auto still switches a few times per solve there, and lands within about 1-4% of the faster arm.
- The first probe holds the barrier off for 264 calls. A short solve that would gain from the barrier gives up that part.
- Pinning changes forced `=1` performance (measured faster at 256 spacecraft). A coordinator task that migrates onto a spinner's thread shares it until the spinner yields. That was already possible unpinned.
