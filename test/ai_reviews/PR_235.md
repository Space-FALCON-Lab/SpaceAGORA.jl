# AI Review Artifact — PR #235

## Scope
Relative gravity in Encke's cancellation-resistant form for articulated bodies and compliant attachments. Stacked on PR #234 and PR #233. Rigid spacecraft are untouched; articulated and attachment results change only at roundoff.

## Changed Files
- src/environment/gravity/gravity_models.jl — `encke_point_mass_difference(μ, r, ρ)`, generic and allocation-free; relative-gravity methods for the constant, inverse-square and J2 models, with a `_j2_only_gravity_accel` helper.
- src/core/types/effector_sampling.jl — the `gravity_backbone_relative_acceleration_ii` hook, whose fallback subtracts two evaluations.
- src/dynamics/coupled/force_torque_models/gravity_effectors.jl, src/dynamics/coupled/force_torque_models/perturbation_effectors.jl — hook imports and wiring.
- src/dynamics/coupled/perturbations.jl — harmonics relative gravity: Encke for the central term plus a direct difference of the non-central part, via an `include_central` keyword on `_harmonics_scalar_force_ii` (default unchanged).
- src/dynamics/coupled/articulated_body.jl — `relative_gravity` / `root_gravity` keywords on `articulated_dynamics!`: root-relative positions, Δg for non-root bodies, root gravity added back. Without the keywords the old path is unchanged.
- src/dynamics/multibody_cloth/compliant_attachment_dynamics.jl — attachment relative gravity through a `relative_gravity` hook.
- src/simulation/engine/dynamics_rhs.jl — engine method for the hook (it peels the position-only gravity effectors), a `_RootRelativeGravity` callable, and the articulated path switched to the relative form.

## Findings
- P3 (documented): the relative form improves `articulated_dynamics!` accuracy only when the gravity gradient drives the joints. With free joints the error falls from 1.7e-12 to 3.1e-15; with springs it is at roundoff either way.
- P3: the design memo predicted a naive Float32 error of order 1e1 at ρ/r = 1e-5, but the observed value is 1.2e-2, which is eps·r/ρ. The test asserts the observed Encke/naive ratio, not the memo's figure.
- P3: third-body gravity was left unchanged on purpose, because Encke there would alter rigid-spacecraft results. It is a separate decision.

  - Float64 and Float32 against a BigFloat reference: Encke ≤ 5.46 eps and ≤ 7.37 eps, against a 16 eps tolerance; naive differencing loses ≥ 1e3x more;
  - ρ = 0 exact, BigFloat in and out;
  - zero allocations, skipped under coverage;
  - `articulated_dynamics!` absolute vs relative form (BigFloat agreement to 1e-60; free joints at least 100x more accurate).

## Residual Risk
Low. The change reorders gravity arithmetic for articulated and attachment bodies only, and the effect is roundoff-level and covered by tests. The new hook has a safe subtraction fallback for gravity models without a dedicated method.
