`split-mode-actuator-rates` (#391, d66f63bc8), on which it is stacked: it

## Findings

No P1 or P2 findings. Points a reviewer should check:

- Low, by design: the stop is a state-dependent switch of the right-hand side
  with no event callback (as the wheels' momentum limit): the integrator may
  pass the stop by a step's error before the rate drops to zero; the test
  bounds the overshoot at 1e-3 rad, and the angle never ends below the stop.
- Low: the reported level saturates each mounted effector's command at the
  stop, while the right-hand side saturates the sum of the effectors' rates
  for a CMG; with one effector commanding a CMG (the usual case) they agree.
- Low: `cmg_steering` drops a stopped CMG whole; it does not keep its
  inward-moving share when the solution would move it back.

## P1 Assessment

No P1 findings. Without stops `cmg_angle_saturate` returns the rate itself,
the steering loop exits after its first solve, and the four fingerprints are
bitwise unchanged against the base.
