# PR314 review: regroup src/models/dynamics by what each folder holds

## Scope

Review of the SpaceAGORA `dynamics-layout` branch against `main`. The PR renames
`src/models/dynamics/coupled/` to `effectors/` with its three subfolders intact,
creates `src/models/dynamics/multibody/` holding `articulated_body.jl` and the
former `compliant_multibody/` as `compliant/`, and moves
`external_propagation.jl` to the dynamics root. Every include, gate pin, test
path and documentation reference follows. No function, type, module name or
export changes; every moved file keeps its content except one comment that
names a sibling's new path.

Authorship disclosure: this record was drafted by the author of the PR and
committed by the principal investigator, because the independent reviewer was
unavailable. Readers should weigh the record accordingly.

## Findings

No defects. The three dynamics includes in the composition module keep their
order; the gravity module's five effector includes keep theirs; the compliant
multibody includes keep theirs. `force_torque_models.jl` and `perturbations.jl`
include their subfolder files relative to their own directory, so the folder
rename leaves them untouched. `articulated_body.jl` and
`external_propagation.jl` include nothing. The path-policy gate's required
list names the four compliant files at their new home; its retired-path and
forbidden-token lists are unchanged. The architecture figure's PDF twin could
not be regenerated on the author's machine (no SVG converter); its label may
still read the old folder name.

## Residual Risk

Branches opened before this change that add files under `dynamics/coupled/`
or `dynamics/compliant_multibody/` will need a rebase; the path-policy gate
does not retire the old names, so such a branch would reintroduce them
silently until reviewed. The architecture figure's PDF may lag its SVG.
