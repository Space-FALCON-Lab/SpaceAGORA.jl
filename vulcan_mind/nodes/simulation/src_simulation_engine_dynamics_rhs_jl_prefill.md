# simulation/engine/dynamics_rhs.jl · _prefill*

## Purpose
Staging shared samples once per RHS call so effectors and workers read buffers instead of resampling: state vectors, planet frame and atmosphere, Sun and third bodies.

## Design & Implementation
`_prefill_rhs_flat_state_samples!` copies state into flat buffers; `_prefill_environment_samples!` computes planet frame and optionally atmosphere per satellite in parallel; `_prefill_atmosphere_samples!` is its atmosphere-on wrapper; `_prefill_shared_body_samples!` samples the Sun and third bodies from the first active satellite.
