## Findings

Claude corrected the moment-reference translation before composing link rotations and lever arms, preserved aerodynamic force in radial flight, and centralized surrogate degree/shape/value validation. His logs report 1,708 focused assertions and 26 gates on the earlier candidate. They are retained as author evidence, not acceptance of this integrated source.

Independent bounded review identified three further corrections implemented here:

1. Checked coefficient-count arithmetic rejects integer overflow before accepting matrix shapes or reading JSON coefficient arrays. Regressions reject overflowing constructor and JSON inputs without evaluating the unsafe earlier object.
2. The whole-vehicle convenience form selects the actual spacecraft root, including when SpacecraftModel appends it after children. Explicit dictionary keys remain literal link indices. A rotated, offset child makes incorrect first-link selection distinguishable in both propagated and fixed attitude modes.
3. Fitting requires enough directions for the harmonic basis and enough distinct speeds for its polynomial factor. A large total sample count cannot compensate for either rank deficiency. Three input regressions exercise this before panel tabulation.

The frame conversion, mean-wind convention, force/moment normalization and reference-point translation were reviewed against the existing aerodynamic owner. Pure wrench evaluation does not write diagnostic state; caching writes remain in the caching hook. No remaining concrete source blocker was identified in this bounded review.
