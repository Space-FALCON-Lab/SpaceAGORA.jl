# parallel/policy/ (small files)

## Purpose
The public face of the threading policy: the module, the decision function, its boolean wrapper, and the two feedback recorders.

## Design & Implementation
`ParallelPolicy` is the module; `thread_policy_decision` returns the full decision record; `use_threads_policy` reduces it to a boolean; `record_policy_observation!` feeds measured elapsed time back to the adaptive controller and the persistent hints; `record_route_discard!` counts outer-routing rejections.
