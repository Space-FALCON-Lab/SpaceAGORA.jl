# simulation/engine/effector_sampling.jl

## Purpose
Environment sampling for wrench-based effectors: planet frame, atmosphere, Sun and third bodies, drawn from prefilled buffers when valid and computed fresh otherwise.

## Design & Implementation
`build_state_sample` packs the spacecraft state; `sample_planet_frame`, `sample_atmosphere`, `sample_solar_ephemeris` and `sample_third_body_ephemerides` compute; the `sample_buffered_*` and `_sample_reusable_*` variants read buffers; `sample_environment_with_reusable_buffers` assembles the `EnvironmentSample`; `_wrench_method_available` routes effectors.
