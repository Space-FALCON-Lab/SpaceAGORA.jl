# simulation/callbacks/gram_track_cache/

## Purpose
Along-track GRAM density prediction: at atmosphere entry, predict the passage, sample density along it, and answer subsequent queries by interpolation while the vehicle stays within tolerance of the prediction.

## Design & Implementation
`GramTrackCache` and its config; `_gram_track_cache_fill_from_trajectory!` predicts the passage with a Kepler-or-linear target model and samples GRAM; `_gram_track_cache_segment`, `_gram_track_cache_ready` and `_gram_track_cache_eval` serve queries with altitude and angle tolerances; `_gram_track_cache_profile` picks entry or orbit horizons; environment readers configure it.
