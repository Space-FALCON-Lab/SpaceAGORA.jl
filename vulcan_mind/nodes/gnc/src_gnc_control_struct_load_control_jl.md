# gnc/control/struct_load_control.jl

## Purpose
The structural-load constraint: the largest angle of attack whose dynamic pressure times drag area stays under the panel load limit.

## Design & Implementation
`_energy_depletion_struct_drag_area` sums the controlled links' projected areas, the local `f` closure is the load residual, `_energy_depletion_struct_load_root_alpha` solves it, and `_edg_structural_alpha` adapts it to the controller's environment sample.
