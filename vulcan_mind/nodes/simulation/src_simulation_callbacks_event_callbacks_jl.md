# simulation/callbacks/event_callbacks.jl

## Purpose
Event detection and data saving: atmosphere entry and exit, orbit and pass counting, impact, quaternion projection, and the periodic results saver.

## Design & Implementation
`get_drag_state_callback` flags atmosphere presence with up- and down-crossing affects; `get_entry_end_callback` and `get_orbit_end_callback` terminate or count; `get_impact_callback` stops at the surface; `get_quaternion_projection_callback` renormalises attitude; `get_data_saving_callback` with `save_func` records the save fields.
