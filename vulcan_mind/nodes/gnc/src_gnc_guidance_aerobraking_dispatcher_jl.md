# gnc/guidance/aerobraking/dispatcher.jl

## Purpose
The seam between aerobraking policy and strategy: maps a strategy kind to its singleton and forwards the guidance input.

## Design & Implementation
`strategy_from_kind` returns `EEdgStrategy()` or `TEdgStrategy()`; `dispatch_aerobraking_guidance` resolves the kind and calls `compute_aerobraking_guidance`, with a second method that first runs policy selection.
